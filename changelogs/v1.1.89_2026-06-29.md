# v1.1.89 — 2026-06-29

## Added — Total G/L column on the Portfolio open-positions table

### What
The Portfolio → Open Positions table previously displayed only **Day's G/L** (the
single-session dollar/percent change). It now also shows a **Total G/L** column
immediately to its right: the full-position **unrealized gain/loss since entry**,
in dollars and percent (e.g. `+$1.2K (+8.40%)`), green when positive and red when
negative, mirroring the Day's G/L styling.

### Why
Day's G/L only answers "how did this move today." A wheel/swing trader managing
open positions needs the full picture — total gain since the trade was opened —
to decide on rolls, profit-taking (the 50% rule), and assignment risk. That figure
was already synced from E*Trade but never surfaced in the grid.

### How
- E*Trade's dollar `totalGain` is already ingested and persisted as
  `unrealizedPnl` (see `etrade-portfolio-service.ts:427`), and the percent as
  `totalGainPct`. No new data, schema, or sync work was required.
- New `totalGain` column in the open-tab column list; new table cell renders
  `et.unrealizedPnl ?? pos.unrealizedPnl` (dollars) with `et.totalGainPct`
  (percent), reusing `fmtLargeNumber` / `fmtPct`.
- Falls back to the stored `unrealizedPnl` when no E*Trade row is present, and
  renders `—` when neither is available.

### Scope
Display-only. No change to scoring, entry/exit policy, sizing, or any
trade-logic path — not subject to the FR-SEY-5 validate-before-ship rubric.
