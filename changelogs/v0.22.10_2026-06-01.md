# v0.22.10 — 2026-06-01

## Daily Brief — Strategy Lab grade alignment on pick cards

Each pick card now shows the grade for the strategy-relevant options structure
alongside the best overall structure when they differ. This makes the
scoring-engine vs. live-options-chain gap immediately visible.

### Example (TSCO — Wheel pick)

```
CSP (Short Naked Put)  B  ·  Best fit: Reverse Big Lizard  A+
```

The Brief says TSCO is a top Wheel candidate (stock-level signals: IV Rank 88.5,
Fundamentals 85, RSI 38.3). The new row shows that the Wheel execution (CSP) grades
a B on today's chain, and the best executable structure right now is a Reverse Big
Lizard at A+. User can act on either.

### New components

**`DailyBriefView.tsx`**
- `STRATEGY_TO_SLUGS` — maps each Brief strategy to its relevant Strategy Lab slugs
- `SLUG_SHORT` — human-readable short names (CSP, Call Spread, PMCC, etc.)
- `GRADE_COLOR` — A+ green → F red color map
- `gradeOf()` — finds the best-scoring relevant slug in `strategyLabResult.scores`
- `bestOverall()` — picks `scores[0]` (already sorted best → worst by Strategy Lab)
- `StrategyLabSummary` component — renders the grade row; hidden when not validated
- `PickCard` — accepts `strategy` prop, renders `StrategyLabSummary` between signals and rationale

**`styles.css`** — `.pick-lab-summary`, `.lab-grade-badge`, `.lab-grade-sep`, `.lab-grade-missing`
