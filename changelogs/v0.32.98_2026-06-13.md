# v0.32.98 — 2026-06-13

## Inflection strategy — Phase 1: breakout/continuation signals

First of three phases building a new "Inflection" brief strategy: a **directional credit
spread anchored at a breakout level**, ranked top-5. This phase lays the data foundation
(safe/additive — nothing consumes it yet).

### Background (offline validation)

A breakout *by itself* has ~no forward edge in a bull tape (top-line continuation +0.23% /
51% win, vs a +0.26% / 53% unconditional baseline). But:
- The composite ranking (decisiveness + volume surge + ATR + with-trend) sorts breakouts
  cleanly and monotonically (top quintile held the level 60–65% over 5d vs ~29% bottom).
- A **directional credit spread** placed ~0.5–1 ATR beyond the level on top-quintile
  breakouts expires OTM **~84–90%** of the time (both bull put and bear call sides).
- Requiring high IV rank (rich premium) costs only ~2% win rate — essentially free.

So the strategy is a top-quintile, credit-spread play. This phase computes the inputs.

### What

- **Migration 041** adds to `technical_signals`: `breakout_state` (−1/0/1),
  `breakout_level`, `breakout_decisiveness`, `volume_surge`, `atr_pct`.
- **`DailyTechnicalSignalsJob`** computes them from price bars each night
  (`computeBreakout` + `computeAtr14`). Fresh 20-day high/low break, **gated to
  with-trend continuation** (up-break must be above SMA50, down-break below).
