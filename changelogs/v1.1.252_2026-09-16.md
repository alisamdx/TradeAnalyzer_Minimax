## The finding: E*Trade's Greeks can be computed off the wrong price

`packages/core/scripts/qc410-study.mjs`, three rounds over 2026-09-15 and 2026-09-16:

- **The option prices are right.** Put-call parity on the quoted mids recovers the session close to a median of −0.03 % across ~500 tickers, including the broken ones.
- **The Greeks are not, on 50–70 tickers a day.** The delta-implied spot sits away from the close: PCTY 113.58 then 113.62 on closes of 147.32 and 147.22, JAZZ 249.65 then 249.67 while the stock moved, AMG 405 on 343, WSM 235 on 218.
- **E*Trade recomputes them per request.** On 2026-09-15 two runs fetched the AIZ $280 put with the same quote timestamp (15:55:15) and got IV 0.481 / delta −0.995 and IV 0.216 / delta −0.782.
- **Not stale by a fixed lag.** The delta-implied spot matched an earlier close within 1 % on 16–18 of 25 worst tickers, at lags of 4 to 31 sessions with no consistent value, and PCTY matched no close in 45 days.
- **Our model is fine.** Tickers whose delta-implied spot is within 0.1 % of the close breach a 0.05 delta tolerance on 0.9–1.5 % of contracts.

Consequence for the existing app: strike selection by delta (`analysis-service`, `wheel-shortlist`, Strategy Lab) picks the wrong contract on those tickers. Strategy Lab's 8 % guard reads the strike nearest put delta −0.50, so it resolves no finer than strike spacing and misses the 3–8 % cases.

## TradeAnalyzer 2 — gate

- **QC-410 is per ticker.** Median delta-implied spot vs put-call-parity spot, fires past 1 %; also fires when ≥ 5 near-the-money deltas are pinned at 0/±1 where the model reads 0.2–0.8 (the PCTY case, where nothing can be inverted). Latest observation per contract, so a re-run day is not counted twice (126,396 observations of 63,198 contracts on 2026-09-15). Previously: "delta off by > 0.05 on > 5 % of all ATM contracts" — RED for the domain, naming no ticker.
- **QC-414 (new, blocking): Greek scale.** Split out so a unit fault still fires: a delta outside [−1, 0] / [0, 1] or not invertible at 0.5–1.5× spot counts as unreachable; fires under 50 % reachable. Tested with delta ×100 and a sign flip.
- **QC-407 compares against `day_close`.** It read `raw_eod_quote.last`, which Polygon never sends on this plan: ~13,000 rows a day, every `last` null, zero tickers evaluated, no finding. It now also fires a `coverage:<date>` finding when both sides exist and nothing is comparable.
- **QC-008 passes a capped window the sweep re-covered.** A capped `from`/`to` response now passes when uncapped responses to the same endpoint in the same run cover every day of it (3 blocking findings on 2026-09-16 for a correct subdivision). A capped single day still blocks.
- **QC-009: `raw_edgar_fact` natural key includes `period_start`** (dictionary v17). A 10-Q's quarter and year-to-date values share end, filing and fiscal period. 1,641 false duplicates.
- **`raw_option_contract_snapshot` indexed on `source_response_id`.** QC-403 counts contracts per chain response ~4,900 times a run; `EXPLAIN` showed a full `SCAN` per count. Measured on a synthetic table: 7.4 ms per count unindexed at 400k rows, all 4,000 counts in 7 ms indexed. The 2026-09-16 gate held the main thread from 18:47 to 19:14; this is a confirmed and growing part of that, not proven to be all of it — see TODO #50.
- **Gate email subject counts this run's blocking findings.** It counted every open blocking exception in the generation (3,438 against the gate's 2,321). The body now reads `BLOCKING (N: X found by this run, Y still open from earlier runs)`.

## TradeAnalyzer 2 — collection

- **A subset member E*Trade no longer recognises is closed as `delisted`.** An expirations call answering HTTP 400 "The symbol entered is invalid" closes the member (`removal_reason = 'delisted'`) unless it has a live pin. The dictionary declared the reason; nothing wrote it, so EA, EQR and WBS cost an error every run. Any other 400 does not close. A relisting is re-admitted by the rule on its next bar.
- **The host's universe lists exclude `constituents_excluded`.** Defensive: migration 085 already keeps those names out of `constituents`, but this reader was the one that did not check.
