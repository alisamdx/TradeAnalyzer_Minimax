## The rule

The Greeks are consistent if they agree with **either** reading of the price: `min(|implied/chain − 1|, |implied/parity − 1|) ≤ 1 %`. A dividend before expiry separates the two readings, and whether a provider's Greeks follow the chain price (EIX) or the dividend-adjusted one (BBY) depends on its model, which E*Trade does not publish. A Greek engine computing off a wrong price is far from both — AIZ 12.2 % from the chain and 12.0 % from parity.

Applied to the 33 tickers printed in the preview: 7 drop (BBY 1.6 %/0.2 %, AJG 1.3 %/0.8 %, AYI 1.2 %/1.0 %, DY 1.2 %/0.7 %, EXPE 1.2 %/0.9 %, FPS 1.3 %/0.4 %, IQV 1.3 %/0.9 %); 26 stay, including every gap above 2 %. Parity is omitted when fewer than 3 strikes quote both sides, and the chain gap alone decides.

The old app's `assessChainGreeks` now computes the parity spot from the chain's own quotes and applies the same rule, so both apps keep one definition.
