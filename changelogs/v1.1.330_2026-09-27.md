## QC-603: a recent SEC report filing means the next date is simply not announced yet

QC-603 fires when a chain-subset stock has had no future earnings date observed for 7 days. On
2026-09-27, 11 stocks had never been dated by any source. Finnhub's calendar had never listed them, and
E*Trade, asked on every run, answered with no `nextEarningDate` (an unparseable value would have
recorded a parse failure; there were none).

For 3 of them the reason is normal. They had just reported:

| ticker | last report filing |
|---|---|
| IOT | 10-Q filed 2026-09-08 |
| JHX | 10-Q filed 2026-08-06 |
| JAN | 10-Q filed 2026-08-05 |

Their next results are still a month or more away and not yet announced. QC-603's existing "just
reported" grace can only see a report date the calendar once listed as *upcoming*. So a company that
reported before gen-002 began on 09-11, or that Finnhub never covers, looks exactly like one that has
dropped out of the calendar.

**Change:** a third exemption. When nothing is dated, and the stock's last 10-K/10-Q/20-F/40-F in
`raw_edgar_filing` (joined on its latest CIK) was filed within **60 days**, the check evaluates 0 and
closes `rule_changed`, naming the filing and its age.

- **The 60 days is a working threshold, not a measured one.** A quarterly cycle is about 91 days, a
  10-Q is filed on or up to about 40 days after the release, and dates are typically announced 2–5
  weeks ahead.
- **Past 60 days it fires again,** which is when a missing date starts to matter to a 21–35 DTE trade.

**Rehearsed on 2026-09-25:** IOT, JAN and JHX closed. The other 8 stay open (BMNR, BRK.B, GLXY, LLYVA,
LLYVK, MDLN, ROIV, SUNB). None of them has a report filing stored yet: 5 have never been reached by
the submissions sweep, and LLYVA and LLYVK carry a wrong CIK (below).

## Found, not fixed: three Liberty tickers carry a stale CIK from Polygon

Polygon's reference data gives FWONA, LLYVA and LLYVK the CIK **0000869614**. At SEC that is the old
*Liberty Media Corporation*: no tickers, and no 10-Q or 10-K anywhere in its filing history.
`company_tickers.json` maps FWONA to **1560385** (Liberty Media Corp) and LLYVA/LLYVK to **2078416**
(Liberty Live Holdings, Inc.). The collector has therefore been asking SEC about the wrong filer for
these three, so their filings, facts and insider mapping are empty. A cross-check of all 1,083
universe tickers against SEC's map found **1,027 matching and only these 3 different**; the other 53
are ETFs, which SEC's map does not list.
