# v0.27.1 — 2026-06-06

## Enhanced Daily Brief PDF with full trade detail

The PDF attachment now contains every available detail for each pick so you can review and place trades from the document alone.

### New sections added to each pick card

- **Market Context** — ATM IV, IV Rank, IV Percentile, direction bias, 5-day momentum, MA20, MA50, IV data point count
- **Score Breakdown** — sub-scores for IV (0–30), Direction (0–30), Premium (0–25), Liquidity (0–15), complexity tier, and all strategy flags (reason chips)
- **All Structures Evaluated** — every strategy in the current bucket (e.g. Big Lizard, Call Spread, PMCC, Call Zebra, Call Butterfly for Bullish) shown in a compact table with Grade, Score, sub-scores, and setup summary; best structure highlighted
- **Strategy Fit** — the Strategy Lab's per-structure AI rationale explaining why that specific structure fits (separate from the pick-level AI analysis)
- **Complete Signal List** — all scoring signals (not just the top 7); active signals show label, value, and weight; inactive/missing signals listed separately in gray

### Legs table unchanged
The existing legs table (action, type, strike, mid, delta, IV%, OI, bid×ask) was already complete — no columns removed.
