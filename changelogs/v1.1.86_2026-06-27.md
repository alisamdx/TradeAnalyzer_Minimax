# v1.1.86 — 2026-06-27

## Why the bullish brief picks a Big Lizard, not a bull put spread — investigation + disabled rubric fix

Follow-up to the v1.1.85 Ranked Grid work. The user took the top bullish Conviction pick (AMD),
ran Validate, and asked why the brief recommends a **Big Lizard** when the lab's own validator
suggests a **bull put spread** (`short-put-vertical`) as a cleaner A+ alternative.

### Root cause

Two different candidate pools. The brief picks within the *bullish strategy's* relevant structures
(`STRATEGY_RELEVANT_SLUGS.bullish`), which **exclude** `short-put-vertical` (it's filed under
`inflection`). The Validate screen reasons by *intent* and its allowlist includes the spread.

Adding the slug to the bullish bucket was tested and **rejected**: across 114 historical bullish
picks it would change the recommendation in only 22.6% of cases, by ~1 pt, and grades *lower* 58.5%
of the time. The real issue is the grading rubric, not the candidate list.

### The rubric blindspot (quantified)

`premiumScore` already divides credit by capital-at-risk — but **saturates** at a 1.5%/30d
threshold, so a Big Lizard (return-on-risk ~16%) and a bull put spread (~30%) both peg the 25-pt
cap. And **PoP is absent from `totalScore` entirely**. Static analysis of the 102 picks where both
structures were constructible (using the lab's stored `maxLoss`/`popEstimate`):

| | Big Lizard (recommended) | Bull Put Spread |
|---|---|---|
| avg lab totalScore | 84.1 | 82.3 |
| avg max profit | $5,630 | $848 |
| avg max loss / capital | $34,175 | $2,298 |
| avg return-on-risk | 15.7% | **30.3%** |
| avg PoP (lab estimate) | **52.7%** | **70.6%** |
| breakeven cushion | 12.8% | 11.3% |

The spread has higher return-on-risk in **86%** of picks, higher PoP in **100%**, for ~15× less
capital — yet the lab grades the lizard ~1.8 pts higher.

A correction to an earlier claim: the Big Lizard's "no upside risk" only holds when net credit ≥
the call-spread width, which the hedge picker often can't meet (it falls back to the nearest
strike). So the lizard is frequently a **coin-flip range trade** (PoP ~52%), not a one-sided
premium play — which is exactly what the 52.7% average PoP reflects.

### What shipped (disabled)

`USE_POP_WEIGHTED_PREMIUM` (**default false**) in `strategy-lab-service.ts`. When enabled, the
short-premium `premiumScore` becomes graduated capital-efficiency × probability-of-profit:

```
premiumScore = round( 25 × clamp(ret30d/3, 0, 1) × clamp((PoP−40)/35, 0.3, 1) )   ∈ [0,25]
```

On the sample this docks the lizard ~25→9 and the spread ~25→22, flipping selection toward the
spread. Pure exported `popWeightedPremiumScore()`; range preserved so grade thresholds stay
calibrated. With the flag off, scoring/selection is **byte-for-byte unchanged**.

### Why not enabled

Static + PoP geometry is necessary but not sufficient — it shows better probability-weighted
risk-adjusted shape, not realized dollars (the lizard's larger credit means it wins bigger in the
~53% of cases it wins). Per **FR-SEY-5**, a structure-selection change needs a realized backtest
delta showing it's at-worst-neutral before shipping live. The 2026-06 bullish sample **doesn't
settle until Jul 2–24 2026** (zero had expired at analysis time), so the realized test can't run yet.

### The harness (ready for July)

`scripts/backtest-lizard-vs-spread.mjs` settles each structure at **hold-to-expiry intrinsic** — no
IV/time-value assumptions — using `historical_prices.close` on the last trading day ≤ expiration,
from the legs + price paths already stored on each pick. Today it reports `0 settled / 76 pending`.
**Re-run after Jul 24 2026**; enable the flag only if the realized `spv − lizard` return-on-capital
delta is ≥ 0. Settlement math is the unit-tested `src/shared/structure-settle.ts`.
