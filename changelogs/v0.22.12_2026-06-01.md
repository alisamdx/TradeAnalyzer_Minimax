# v0.22.12 — 2026-06-01

## Daily Brief — stock price + Wheel capital requirement on pick cards

Each pick card now shows the last known stock price. Wheel picks additionally
show the CSP capital requirement (~price × 100), making it immediately clear
how much buying power the trade needs before opening Strategy Lab.

### Example (TSCO — Wheel pick)

```
#1  TSCO  $245.30  ~$24,530 cap  Score 62.3  ✓ Validated  Strategy Lab →
```

### Implementation

- `BriefPick.price: number | null` added to `src/shared/types.ts`
- `DailyIntelligenceBriefJob` Stage 6: populates `price` from
  `signalData.get(ticker)?.lastPrice` (already gathered from `historical_prices`
  / `analysis_snapshots` — no extra DB query)
- `DailyBriefView` pick header: renders `$price` and `~$N cap` (Wheel only)
- `.pick-price` and `.pick-capital` CSS added
