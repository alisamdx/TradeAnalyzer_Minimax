# v1.1.168 — 2026-09-03

## feat — Roll the Dice picks on the trade's merit; your size and conviction list only annotate

### What prompted it

The 2026-09-03 email was a **no-dice day**, and the operator asked the right question of it: *was
there an opportunity, excluded because of my conditions?* The rejection line read:

> 2 rejected: MPC ($37,000 over $30,000 — not on your conviction list) · AAPL ($32,000 over
> $30,000 — score 65.4 under the 70 exception bar)

Both had cleared every gate about the *trade* — critic `pass` and non-degraded, a constructible
CSP, liquidity ≥ 8 — and died on gates about the *operator*. MPC died on the first stretch check,
so its composite was **never evaluated at all**; the operator no longer actively maintains that
list, and it holds 13 names. AAPL got further and failed on merit (65.4 against a 70 bar, below
the 65.9 median of a normal day) — the bar working as intended, but only after the $30,000 line
had pushed a $32,000 position into an exception tier at all.

### The change

The gates were doing two different jobs under one name. They are now separated:

- **Trade quality — still HARD.** Critic `pass` and not `degraded`, a constructible
  `short-naked-put`, liquidity ≥ 8, earnings known/fresh/after expiry, a real net credit. These
  are facts about the setup and they are what makes acting on the pick defensible.
- **Operator fit — now REPORTED, never excluding.** Capital committed and conviction-list
  membership are facts about the operator, not evidence about a trade. They travel on `pick.fit`
  and remove nothing.

`qualifiesForStretch` and the whole exception tier are **deleted** — `STRETCH_MIN_SCORE`,
`STRETCH_MIN_LIQUIDITY`, `DEFAULT_STRETCH_CAPITAL`. That machinery existed for exactly one
purpose, letting good trades escape the capital gate, and the capital gate is gone.

`maxCapital` ($30,000 default) survives as a **reporting split rather than a ceiling**. When the
merit pick exceeds it, the result also carries `withinBudget`: the highest-scoring eligible
candidate that *does* fit. So demoting the gate never leaves a day with nothing fundable in it —
the operator sees the best trade *and* an affordable one, and chooses.

### What it looks like

- Over-budget pick → the email and panel lead with **"TIES UP $37,000 — ABOVE YOUR USUAL
  $30,000"** and say plainly that it is *not* a rejection and the funding call is theirs. It is
  deliberately not framed as an exception being granted.
- Conviction is stated in words, in four distinguishable states: not on the list / listed with no
  never-above band / strike inside the band / **strike ABOVE the band** (surfaced loudly — on
  assignment you would be buying at a price you already ruled out).
- The "passed every gate" line no longer claims the price was checked against a limit, and the
  footer says size and conviction are reported, not filtered.

### Validation (FR-SEY-5)

`scripts/dice-fit-demotion-study.mjs` replays all 62 stored wheel briefs under both rule sets with
identical trade-quality gates, so the delta is attributable solely to the demotion:

| | fit gated (old) | fit reported (new) |
|---|---|---|
| days with a pick | 26 (41.9%) | **34 (54.8%)** |
| median composite of the winner | 65.9 | 65.9 |

The unchanged median is the sanity check — removing a non-merit filter can only raise the winner's
merit, never lower it, and a drop would have meant a bug. Of the 34 pick-days: 12 exceed $30,000
and only **3 of those have a within-budget alternative to offer**, so on 9 days the operator sees
one unfundable pick and nothing else (still strictly more than the old rules gave them); 17 are
not on the conviction list; **0** breach a never-above band. The 8 days gained include today's MPC
and four AAPL setups that were over the line by $500–$1,500.

The replay's earnings gate remains invalid retrospectively (`earnings_calendar` keeps one row per
ticker with no history) but is applied identically to both arms, so it cancels out.

### The cost, recorded deliberately

- **The feature's promise shifts.** "Close your eyes and do it" becomes "here is today's best
  wheel entry — check you can fund it." More honest, but different.
- **Nothing enforces own-at-strike any more.** The conviction band encoded the operator's own
  first rule, and it is now a sentence rather than a gate. A badge is not a band.
- **The hit rate rose without the bar moving.** The critic verdict still rejects by far the most
  and is untouched; it remains the only real lever left, and pulling it would defeat the premise.
