## 1,004 blocking QC-409 findings nothing could close

QC-409 checks that every pinned contract (held or recommended) has a snapshot each run.

**What happened.** On 2026-09-22 the 17:05 collector run (run 28) finished in 14 minutes with nothing
from E*Trade:
- 599 of 599 subset tickers returned no expirations (QC-401);
- no balance (QC-801);
- no VIX (QC-901).

It raised **1,004 QC-409 findings**, one per pinned contract. Run 30 re-collected the same date at
20:00 and captured every pin, and each has been captured on every day since.

**Why they stayed open.** A QC-409 finding could close only through remediation R1, which had never run
on any of them (`remediations_json = []`). So all 1,004 sat open and blocking. They were invisible in
the day report, which counts only findings that fired in the run, but they counted against the open
queue and the pilot's clean-day streak.

**Fix.** A finding closes itself as `data_corrected` when two things are true:
- the pin is captured tonight;
- the trading dates of its first and last firing run now hold a snapshot, meaning the missed day was
  re-collected.

A pin that works again after a day nobody re-collected has left a real hole in that contract's price
history. The gate cannot repair that, so the finding stays open for the operator.

## Rehearsed on 2026-09-25 (snapshot)
