## Fix: My Stocks email column alignment

The email report's header cells were all left-aligned while data cells were right/
center-aligned, so headers didn't sit over their columns. buildEmailReport() now drives
both <th> and <td> alignment from one per-column definition (numeric → right, text →
left, flag → center).
