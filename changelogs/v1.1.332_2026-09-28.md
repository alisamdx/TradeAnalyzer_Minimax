## Fix: v1.1.331 silently emptied QC-701

v1.1.331's shared CIK rule (`effectiveCikSql`) named its inner tables `s` and `r`. QC-701 calls it with
`r.ticker` from its own `raw_ticker_reference r`. Inside the subquery, `r.ticker = r.ticker` bound both
sides to the inner table, which is always true. So every stock got the CIK of the newest reference row
in the table, found no facts under it, and was skipped as "never pulled". **On rehearsal QC-701 went
from 7 of 69 evaluated to 0 of 0**, which looks the same as a quiet night.

The existing QC-701 test held one ticker, and for one ticker "the newest row" is the right one, so it
could not see this. The rehearsal on real data did. The inner aliases are now `cik__sec` and `cik__ref`.
A new test with two stocks that must get different CIKs **fails against v1.1.331 and passes now**.
QC-603 (`m.ticker`), QC-702 (`t.ticker`) and the collector's `cikMap` (`u.ticker`) were unaffected.

v1.1.331 is the build currently running. This must ship before the next collector run, or that run's
QC-701 evaluates nothing.

## QC-701 says "omitted" when it isn't lag, and QC-704 finds every such filing

On 2026-09-28 QC-701 had 7 findings (IQV, JCI, LNT, HCA, HUM, KNX, KO), each blaming "SEC's aggregate is
behind". Measured:

| 10-Q filed | read afterwards | facts present |
|---|--:|--:|
| 07-21 to 07-24 | 15 | 15 |
| **07-28** | 6 | **1** |
| **07-29** | 12 | **4** |
| 07-30 | 16 | 15 |
| 07-31 | 8 | 7 |
| 08-03 to 08-11 | 53 | 52 |

- **This is SEC dropping a filing window, not lagging.** Fetched live on 2026-09-28, KO's companyfacts
  still ends at period 2026-04-28, although its 10-Q filed 07-29 carries inline XBRL and its April 10-Q,
  filed through the same agent (Workiva), is present.
- **It isn't SEC in general, or one filing agent.** JPM's aggregate is current to 09-25, and the seven
  used three different filers.

**QC-701's wording.** When companyfacts was read 14 or more days after the filing, the finding now says
SEC's aggregate **omits** the filing, naming the gap, and points to QC-704. Under 14 days it still says
"behind".

**New QC-704 (warning, per accession, exempt from repeat escalation).** A stored report filing that is
absent from `raw_edgar_fact`, although companyfacts for its CIK was read at least 14 days after filing,
is flagged. It closes `provider_fixed` if SEC fills it in.
- QC-701 sees only chain-subset stocks more than 120 days stale, so it named 7.
- On rehearsal QC-704 names **17**. That includes KRC, LAZ, INCY, LFUS, LII, LSTR, HUBB, IDA and JBS,
  and **IDA's April 10-Q, missing 145 days after filing**.

The weekly companyfacts retry keeps trying; nothing else is done about the hole yet. Reading the
filing's own XBRL instance is the repair, and it was left for later.
