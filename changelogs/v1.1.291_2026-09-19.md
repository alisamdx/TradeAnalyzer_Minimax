# v1.1.291 — 2026-09-19

TradeAnalyzer only. `core` and `next` are unchanged.

Both changes come from reading the first shadow report (v1.1.290) on real data: 19 pick rows became 12 positions, with 80 rejections over 10 runs.

## A position is a ticker episode, as in the #43 study

v1.1.290 counted a position as one ticker, expiry and strike pair. The first week then held **EOG 145/135 (09-14) and EOG 140/130 (09-16)**, and likewise two CF spreads, as separate positions. That is one bet counted twice. It inflates the gate's n with a single risk, and it is not how the study that set the gate measured. `buildSpreadShadowReport` now applies the study's episode rule: a pick of a ticker within 14 calendar days of that ticker's last **kept** entry is a repeat, whatever its strikes or expiry, and the first entry is kept.

## Rejections name their tickers, and a missing leg says why

Each rejection kind now lists its tickers with counts. The two missing-leg reasons ("no two-sided put at 0.25–0.35Δ" and "no two-sided long put 5–7.5% below the short") were 27 of 80 rejections with no way to tell a thin chain from blanked Greeks. `diagnoseLegs` re-reads that run's captured entry chain (nearest 30 DTE in the window) with `buildBullPutSpread`'s leg rules and names the first cause it meets:

- no chain stored or no underlying price;
- the chain carried no deltas (v1.1.255's stale-Greeks guard, or E*Trade);
- no put in the Δ band, or the band put is not two-sided;
- no strike in the wing range, or the wing strike has no bid;
- legs present, meaning another expiry was chosen before earnings.
