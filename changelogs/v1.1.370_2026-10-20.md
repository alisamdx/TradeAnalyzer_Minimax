# v1.1.370 — 2026-10-20

## The wheel snapshot says why a ticker got no CSP

### Why

The wheel analysis writes one explanation for every ticker it finds no cash-secured put for:
"No suitable CSP found (30–45 DTE, delta 0.20–0.30)." On 2026-10-20 the brief's ISRG pick had an
empty `premiumYield` for that reason while Strategy Lab priced a $400 Nov-20 put. Splitting the
causes took a join of the snapshot against `stale_greek_shadow_picks` by date and slot. From the
10-06 after-close run to 10-20 it showed:

- 274–366 misses per after-close run;
- **194–285 (70–78 %) were chains whose Greeks `STALE_GREEK_GUARD` withheld** (E\*Trade's evening
  Greeks lag the close);
- 76–87 were genuine.

Full measurement: `Requirements/v2-execution/06-seykota-risk-extensions.md`, the
`STALE_GREEK_MODEL_DELTA` entry.

### What changed

- `findSuitableChain` takes an optional `ChainSearchTally` and fills it in place. The tally holds
  whether the expiration list failed, how many expirations were in the window, how many chains came
  back with contracts, and how many of those had no delta on any contract. Nothing reads it inside
  the walk, so the contract chosen is the same as before.
- `classifyNoCsp(tally)` returns a `WheelNoCspReason` (new, in `src/shared/types.ts`):
  - `expirations_unavailable`: the expiration list could not be fetched.
  - `no_expiry_in_window`: no listed expiration in the DTE window (the monthly gap).
  - `chain_unavailable`: every in-window chain failed or came back empty.
  - `greeks_withheld`: every chain that came back had no deltas.
  - `none_in_band`: Greeks were present but no put or call had a delta in the band with a bid. If any
    chain had Greeks, the band is named as the cause, not the guard.
  - `no_quote`: a contract was found but the underlying has no price.
- `analyzeWheel` writes the reason into the explanation, after the unchanged prefix:
  `No suitable CSP found (30–45 DTE, delta 0.20–0.30) [greeks_withheld]: Greeks withheld as stale on
  every chain in the window.` It also sets `WheelResult.noCspReason`. The field is null when a
  contract was found and absent on older snapshots. The window in the text is now the one actually
  searched, so it reads 28–52 if `WHEEL_MONTHLY_FIRST` is on.
- Options-income misses are unchanged.

### Not a trade-logic change

Selection, scoring and every gate are untouched, so FR-SEY-5 does not apply. A test pins that a hit is
unchanged with the tally present.

### Tests

`tests/wheel-no-csp-reason.test.ts`: classification of each cause, the "any chain with Greeks means
the band" rule, the kept prefix, and the real `findSuitableChain` walk on fake providers for
withheld, out-of-band, failing, out-of-window and hit cases.
