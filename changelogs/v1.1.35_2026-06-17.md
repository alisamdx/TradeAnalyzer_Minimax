# v1.1.35 — 2026-06-17

## Critic cites the precomputed max loss (no more "unlimited" on bounded short puts)

### The finding
On the 2026-06-17 brief the critic described every bullish big-lizard's short-put downside as
"uncapped" / "nearly unlimited" / "unlimited":

- ALAB: "uncapped downside on the put leg"
- AMD: "the put leg alone has nearly unlimited downside"
- MRVL: "uncapped downside on the short put (to zero)"  ← internally contradictory

This is technically wrong. A short PUT's loss is **bounded** — the worst case is the underlying
going to 0 (loss = strike × 100 − credit). Only a naked short **CALL** is truly unbounded. And
the bounded figure was already computed by `classifySingleExpiryPnl`:

| Pick | big-lizard maxLoss | maxProfit |
|---|---|---|
| AMD | −$44,836 | +$6,164 |
| ALAB | −$29,931 | +$6,069 |
| COHR | −$32,618 | +$5,383 |
| MRVL | −$23,459 | +$4,541 |

Cause: the critic's structure context carried `legs`, `shortStrike`, `credit`,
`strikeOtmPct`, `breakevenDistancePct` — but **not** `maxLoss`/`maxProfit`. So the LLM
characterised the risk from general knowledge of short puts and reached for "unlimited."

### The fix
- The critic structure context now includes `structure.maxProfit`, `structure.maxLoss`
  (per-contract, ×100; `null` ⇒ genuinely unbounded — only a naked short call), and
  `structure.breakevens`.
- The prompt's STRUCTURE section now instructs the model to cite these provided numbers for any
  reward/risk or max-loss statement, to treat a **finite** `maxLoss` as DEFINED (never
  "unlimited/uncapped" — a short put is bounded at S→0, state the figure), and to flag a
  **null** `maxLoss` as the real unbounded case (naked short call).

Same remediation as the precomputed expected-move (v1.1.31) and the IV context (v1.1.20/29): a
number the critic reasons about numerically must be given to it, not invented.

### Impact / validation
- **Rationale-accuracy only** — no rule check, gate, or trade selection changed; the
  deterministic defined-risk preference already consumed `maxLoss`, this only surfaces it to the
  LLM. The bounded-short-put and unbounded-short-call facts are covered by
  `classifySingleExpiryPnl` tests (`tests/strategy-lab-service.test.ts`, incl. the big-lizard
  bounded-max-loss case). Full suite: **542 passing**.
- Bonus for the launch path: the critic can now affirm the **defined-risk max loss** on
  inflection credit spreads instead of only describing the move through the strikes.
- The persisted 2026-06-17 brief keeps the old wording; **re-run the brief** for corrected
  rationales.
