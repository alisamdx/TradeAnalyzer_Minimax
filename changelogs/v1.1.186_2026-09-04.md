# v1.1.186 — 2026-09-04

## T8 — can the Validate view's entry machinery judge a CSP strike?

**No behaviour change.** A study and its result. `scripts/fr-wheel-entry-quality-study.mjs`.

## The question

Asked by the operator, looking at the Validate panel:

> "It has Entry zone, RSI, MACD and all the other indicators to show me a buy signal — why can't
> we use it to determine whether it's a good strike price to own? I know the wheel is a premium
> strategy, but it's a mix of both. I can't wheel something I'm not comfortable owning, and to own
> it the strike needs to be a reasonable point or discount."

## Three quarters of it was already settled

Before running anything, three of the panel's four technical ingredients turned out to be in the
wheel composite already, with published results:

| Validate ingredient | already in wheel as | prior result |
|---|---|---|
| RSI (35–65 healthy) | `technicalNeutral`, weight 11–12 | **flat at every level** (T6, v1.1.178) |
| SMA50 > SMA200 stack | `notDowntrend`, weight 8–9 | **anti-predictive twice** (T6; v1.1.33 ADX) |
| distance to SMA200 | own-at-strike (v1.1.169) | the one **positive** result, 12 episodes |
| entry zone, swing low, ATR | — nothing — | untested |

So only the swing low, the ATR cushion and the zone itself were genuinely open.

## Method

Universe is `analysis_snapshots` mode `wheel` — the whole scanned universe per date, not the five
survivors the brief keeps. **4,917 settled liquidity-gated rows across 45 dates**, every technical
recomputed point-in-time from bars on or before the snapshot date. Settled at intrinsic:
`pnl/share = premium − max(0, strike − close@expiry)`, return on capital `= pnl / strike`.

All three de-biasing steps this project mandates:

- **split scale guard** — 10 rows excluded and printed, never silently dropped
- **episode collapse** — one ticker+expiry is one observation → 1,585 episodes
- **regime tell** — SPY over each band's own windows printed beside the result, *not* subtracted
  (a CSP's payoff is capped at the premium, so an excess-of-index figure would be meaningless)

## Result — the principle holds, and two measures carry it

| split | eps | mean | median | assigned | SPY |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| universe (no selection) | 1585 | −0.29% | 1.13% | 25.3% | 1.5% |
| basis ≤ SMA200 | 1099 | **+0.55%** | 1.28% | **17.0%** | 1.5% |
| basis > SMA200 | 671 | **−2.19%** | 0.69% | **42.1%** | 1.3% |
| strike below 20-bar swing low | 869 | **+1.22%** | 1.38% | **13.2%** | 1.5% |
| not below | 1111 | **−1.66%** | 0.91% | **35.7%** | 1.3% |

SPY is flat across every band, so none of this is a band that merely sampled a better market.

**They are complementary, not redundant.** Crossed against each other:

| | below swing | not below |
|---|---|---|
| **basis ≤ SMA200** | **+1.26%**, 11.3% assigned (699) | −0.54%, 25.0% (640) |
| **basis > SMA200** | +0.83%, 21.1% (199) | **−3.06%**, 48.4% (562) |

## The confound that nearly killed it

Below-swing strikes sit **2.6× further OTM** (8.46% vs 3.28%), so "below the swing low" looked
like raw distance wearing a costume. Held inside ATR-cushion quartiles it persists in **all four**,
and is largest exactly where the risk is:

| cushion band | below swing | not below |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 (thinnest) | **+1.48%**, 19.5% assigned | **−4.55%**, 60.8% assigned |
| Q2 | +1.51%, 13.5% | +0.50%, 22.2% |
| Q3 | +0.89%, 15.2% | +0.41%, 18.8% |
| Q4 (widest) | +0.93%, 8.4% | +0.06%, 13.9% |

Own-at-strike survives the identical treatment in all four bands. Neither is a distance proxy.

## The entry zone adds nothing

Its split (858 episodes, +1.23%) is within noise of the swing-low split (869, +1.22%) — because
`entryZoneLow = min(swingLow, SMA50)` and the swing low is nearly always the min. **It is the swing
low renamed.** The zone's own verdict state is the mediocre middle:

```
strike below the zone   +1.23%   13.0% assigned
strike INSIDE the zone  +0.50%   20.6% assigned    <- "✓ IN ENTRY ZONE"
strike above the zone   -1.62%   35.5% assigned
```

Both bounds are the 20-bar low and SMA50 — **recency anchors** — so the zone re-anchors as price
rises and can never say "too expensive". That is the exact construction that made the conviction
band fire on 0 of 39 picks. Live example, AMD on 2026-09-04: zone **$440.50–$499.18** against an
SMA200 of **$340.97**, reading "IN ENTRY ZONE" while trading 39% above its own 200-day.

## The biggest consequence is about own-at-strike

v1.1.169 shipped it as an annotation rather than a gate because its evidence was **12 accept
episodes inside a single ~4-week window**. Here, on a different base and ~100× the sample, it is
the **best selection rule tested**:

| top 5 per date | eps | mean | median | trimmed | assigned |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| universe | 1585 | −0.29% | 1.13% | 0.46% | 25.3% |
| **lowest basis vs SMA200** | 121 | **+2.44%** | **+2.83%** | **+2.97%** | **11.6%** |
| deepest below swing low | 154 | +1.72% | +2.35% | +2.23% | 15.6% |
| widest raw % OTM | 153 | +0.88% | +3.31% | +1.51% | 24.5% |
| widest ATR cushion | 157 | −0.46% | +1.26% | +1.01% | 15.0% |
| INVERTED: thinnest ATR | 171 | −4.75% | −1.63% | −4.10% | 59.4% |

## Correction recorded

Going in I predicted ATR-normalised cushion would beat raw % OTM, on the reasoning that a 5% buffer
means different things on a 4% ATR stock and a 1% ATR stock. **It does not, on returns.** Widest
raw-OTM quartile returns +1.10% / 1.92% median against the widest ATR quartile's +0.49% / 0.99%,
and as a top-5 selection rule ATR is *worse than no selection at all*.

ATR wins on exactly one thing: **assignment monotonicity** — 51.7 → 17.4 → 16.8 → 11.3% across
quartiles, where raw % OTM is non-monotone (50.3 → 14.6 → 15.8 → 18.9%). So ATR is the better risk
gauge and raw distance captures more premium; neither dominates.

Another mechanism read off a formula that the data declined to confirm — the same shape as the
v1.1.176 correction, and worth recording as such rather than quietly dropping.

## Harness check

RSI(14) reproduces its published T6 null: **+0.19% / −0.34% / −0.74%** across oversold / target /
extended bands — a 0.9-point spread against 4.3 points for the 2×2 above. The panel measures what
the earlier study measured.

## Nothing gated

One regime (45 dates, settled through 2026-09-04), and measured on the scanned universe rather than
the brief's picks. Promoting either measure to a gate needs a brief-replay delta under FR-SEY-5.

**Queued in `docs/wheel-parameters.md` T8:** the 2×2 as a dice *annotation*, replayed. The worst
cell — basis above SMA200 **and** strike above the swing low — is 562 of 1,585 episodes at −3.06%
mean and 48.4% assignment: a large enough share to matter, and specific enough to state to the
operator in one sentence.

**Out of scope and permanently untestable:** the Validate view's *fundamental* half (P/E, ROE, D/E,
the AVOID verdict, the analyst rating) disagrees with the wheel critic on live picks — AMD read
**AVOID / analysts SELL** on 2026-06-12 and 06-18 while being a critic-**passed** wheel pick at
+76.5% and +79.2% cost basis vs its own SMA200. `fundamentals_cache` keeps one row per ticker with
no history, so no replay can ever evaluate it.
