# v1.1.294 — 2026-09-19

TradeAnalyzer only. Shadow mode only; the picks themselves are unchanged.

## Bull put spread: the operator's 3-per-sector cap, simulated in the shadow report

The operator answered 08 Q2 (decision S9): **at most 3 open positions per sector** (option B; no per-expiry cap).

Why: the first shadow week held 5 energy and nat-gas positions (EOG, APA, DVN, COP, CF) and 4 tech, all on Oct 16. At $2,500 per position, the energy group alone was a $12,500 single-event risk, half the account.

When it applies: the cap is **simulated now and enforced when the `on` mode is built**. Shadow picks stay uncapped so the evidence is not shaped by the rule, and the report shows both books:

- `simulateSectorCap` walks positions in entry order. A position is skipped when 3 kept positions in its sector are open on its entry date. A position frees its slot on its exit date, and skipped positions never occupy one.
- Sector comes from `constituents.sector`. An unknown sector is its own bucket, never pooled, and the report counts those positions.
- New report fields: `sectorCap` (full vs capped book: positions, settled, mean return, win rate, skipped) and `concentration` (open positions by sector and by expiry). The script prints both and marks `[cap: skipped]` positions.
- `SPREAD_MODE_PROFILE.maxOpenPerSector = 3`, which `on` will enforce.
- Known limit: GICS sectors are coarse. CF is "Materials" although it trades with natural gas.

## Fix found while building it

Positions from one run with equal rank came back in arbitrary order. That made "which position does the cap skip" non-deterministic, and the first entry of an episode could flip too. Order is now (date, slot, rank, **id**).
