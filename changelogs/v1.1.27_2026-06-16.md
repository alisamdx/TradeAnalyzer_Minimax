# v1.1.27 — 2026-06-16

**De-prioritise pin-bet butterflies for directional strategies** (operator-requested follow-up — surfaced by the post-v1.1.25 brief showing put butterflies on bearish picks).

## Problem

The defined-risk preference (v1.1.25) correctly steered bearish picks away from unbounded reverse-big-lizards toward defined-risk structures — but among the defined-risk options it took the highest *lab score*, which for several names was a **put butterfly**. A butterfly only profits if the underlying pins a tight window at expiry (low probability of profit); it scores well on reward/risk (tiny debit, large theoretical max profit) but is a **range/pin bet, not a directional expression** of a bearish thesis. On the 2026-06-16 brief 3 of 5 bearish picks displayed put butterflies.

## Fix

`pickRelevantStructure` gains a third selection tier for **directional strategies** (bullish/bearish/buy), after the defined-risk tier:

> Prefer a directional structure over a pin-bet butterfly — **but only when a directional structure grades ≥ C**. If the only directional alternatives are badly priced (D/F), keep the butterfly.

The grade gate matters: an *unconditional* directional preference would have forced grade-F structures onto ARE/FLUT (whose only non-butterfly defined-risk options were all F), which is worse than a viable C butterfly. The gate trades a pin bet for a directional structure only when the directional one is at least mediocre.

New module constants in `src/shared/strategy-structures.ts`: `PIN_BET_SLUGS = {put-butterfly, call-butterfly}`, `DIRECTIONAL_STRATEGIES = {bullish, bearish, buy}`, `MIN_DIRECTIONAL_GRADE = C`. (Calendars are deliberately **not** treated as pin bets here — scoped to the explicit butterfly case.)

### Effect on the 2026-06-16 bearish brief

| Ticker | Before | After | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| LBRDK | put-butterfly (A) | **poor-man's-covered-put (B)** | a directional B alternative existed |
| ARE | put-butterfly (C) | put-butterfly (C) — kept | only directional options were grade F |
| GPN | long-put-vertical (A) | long-put-vertical (A) | already directional |
| FLUT | put-butterfly (C) | put-butterfly (C) — kept | only directional options were grade F |
| EL | long-put-vertical (C) | long-put-vertical (C) | already directional |

No-op for income (range-bound — iron flies/condors are the right structure and are not pin bets here), inflection (verticals), wheel, and bullish/buy on this brief (their top structures weren't butterflies).

## Why no FR-SEY-5 gate

Like v1.1.25, this changes *which structure* is shown for an already-selected pick, not which tickers are picked or their order (composite-driven, independent of the lab structure/grade). Brief Replay outcomes are ticker-return-based and unaffected. Verified on the live 2026-06-16 brief: exactly one pick's structure changed; tickers and ordering identical.
