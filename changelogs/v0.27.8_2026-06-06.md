# v0.27.8 — 2026-06-06

## Retrospect — Next-Day Accuracy section descriptions

Added explanatory text to the two new sections in the Summary tab.

**Next-Day Accuracy** — explains that it measures the first trading-day close
after the brief, uses strategy-aware verdicts (e.g. Bearish is Positive when
the stock drops), and why it's more useful than waiting for options to expire.

**Brief Accuracy Trend** — explains that a brief only appears once its picks
have at least one trading-day close in price history, so the most recent brief
is often absent until the next day's prices load. Also explains how to use the
trend for signal-weight tuning.
