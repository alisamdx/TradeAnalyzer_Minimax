## QC-603 and QC-905 close their stale findings, and say why

### Problem

On the 2026-09-18 snapshot, QC-603 (earnings freshness) held 67 open findings and QC-905 (recommendation freshness) 82. Only QC-603's 14 were firing. The rest could not close:

| | QC-603 | QC-905 | why quiet |
|---|---|---|---|
| funds (ETF/ETV) | 47 | 47 | raised before v1.1.257 excluded funds; an excluded ticker is never evaluated |
| exempt by a later rule | 4 (CBRS, HONA, SPCX new listings; FPS just reported) | 34 (30 admitted 09-15, after the 09-14 weekly sweep; 4 asked and empty) | evaluated clean *because of* a rule added after they fired |
| fresh data | 1 (GIS) | 0 | a future date is now observed |
| out of scope | 1 (EQR) | 1 (EQR) | delisted, left the subset |

A finding that stops firing without a remediation stays open (P9), and none of these had one.

### Why a clean reading is evidence here

QC-404's findings (v1.1.281) were about a quote on a past day, so a clean quote today proved nothing about them. A freshness finding claims "this ticker's data is stale **now**", so a clean reading today refutes it directly, provided the check says *why* it is clean. That reason decides the resolution kind.

### Fix

Each clean evaluation now carries `closesAs`, which the gate applies only if a finding with that key is open:

- **QC-603:** just-reported grace or new-listing grace → `rule_changed`; a future report date observed within 7 days → `data_corrected`, with the date.
- **QC-905:** a recommendation row stored within 45 days → `data_corrected`; asked within 45 days with no rows → `rule_changed`; admitted within 45 days → `rule_changed`.
- **Both:** `fundFindingClosers` emits an evaluation, with no value, for each open finding whose ticker's current reference is a fund type, and closes it `rule_changed`.

A ticker that is not evaluated for any other reason (EQR, out of the subset) keeps its finding open: a disappearance is not evidence.

On the run that closes them, `evaluated_count` includes the fund closers (QC-603 540 → 587 on the rehearsal). They carry no value, so the distribution is unchanged.
