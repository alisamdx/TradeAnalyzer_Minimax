## A "same issuer" ruling on a QC-210 finding now sticks

### Problem

QC-210 fires when stored bars predate the ticker's current `list_date`, on the reading that such bars belong to a previous occupant. Polygon also moves `list_date` when the **same** issuer starts a new share line, and the check's own comment already warned about this. A `false_positive` ruling could not record that, because QC-210 read no rulings and the next run reopened the finding as a recurrence. It is the same gap QC-205 had until v1.1.276 and QC-209 until v1.1.279.

### Fix

`continuousListingRulings` reads QC-210 findings resolved `false_positive`, together with the `listDate` from their evidence. A ticker ruled against its **current** `list_date` is measured (`value` = bars before listing, `evidence.ruledContinuous`) but not fired. If Polygon later reports a different `list_date`, the ruling does not apply and the check fires again. QC-210 now declares `qa_exception` in `readsTables`.
