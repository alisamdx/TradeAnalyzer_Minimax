# v0.32.70 — 2026-06-10

## Changed

**Brief Stage-4 pool deepened: 10 → 15 candidates per strategy** ([daily-intelligence-brief-job.ts](../src/main/services/jobs/daily-intelligence-brief-job.ts))

After v0.32.69's honest liquidity scoring, the hard gate rejects thin-chain names it previously let through — the 2026-06-10 evening run gated 15 of 25+ candidates, leaving wheel with 4 picks and income with 3. Deepening the E*Trade validation pool from the top 10 to the top 15 per strategy gives the gate enough candidates to still surface 5 quality picks per strategy. Worst-case Stage-4 cap is now 75 unique tickers (5 × 15); actual counts run much lower because the same tickers appear across strategies. Adds roughly 30–60s to the brief run when boards don't overlap.
