# v1.1.143 — 2026-09-01

## Validation — regime detection explored, NOT viable on current data

No code change. Follow-up to v1.1.142, which rejected the `hold_days` sweep and closed by
pointing at regime detection as the next lever. It was explored immediately and does not work.

### The question is not the one already rejected

The v0.32.90 SPY-regime gate asked *should I enter in this regime* and was reverted after
inverting on full-year data. This asks a different question: does an entry-time regime feature
predict **whether a 21-day hold beats a 5-day hold**?

Four features, all computable at entry with no lookahead, regressed against the per-date mean of
`alpha(21d) − alpha(5d)` over the same 125-date replay:

| feature | r | naive t |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ret20` — SPY trailing 20d return | **0.582** | 7.94 |
| `distSma50` — SPY vs 50d SMA | 0.323 | 3.79 |
| `sma20v50` | −0.061 | −0.68 |
| `vol20` — SPY 20d realized vol | −0.119 | −1.33 |

### Why those t-statistics are meaningless

125 dates with **overlapping 21-day forward windows**, and adjacent dates share near-identical
regime features. The effective independent sample is ~6 (125/21), not 125. This is the same
inflation that made the one-month read in v1.1.142 look decisive.

### The counterexample needs no statistics

| month | SPY `ret20` | `distSma50` | mean(21d − 5d) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-04 | +4.93% | **+2.56%** | **+6.60%** |
| 2026-06 | +0.41% | **+2.75%** | **−6.98%** |

April and June carry essentially the same `distSma50` and produce opposite outcomes. Any rule
keyed on distance-from-SMA50 must classify them identically and be wrong on one of them.

### Leave-one-month-out

Fit the sign on five months, predict the held-out one:

```
ret20      4/6 correct   (both misses Feb & Mar, same direction)
distSma50  3/6 correct   (coin flip)
```

4/6 is not evidence — 3 is chance, and 4 arises ~34% of the time from coin flips at n=6. The
existing binary gate is flat as well: SPY>SMA50 → +1.17%, SPY<SMA50 → +0.65%.

### Why this is structural rather than a tuning problem

Whether a 21-day hold beats a 5-day one depends on whether the market **trends over the following
21 days**. An entry-time feature can only help if trend persists, so what is really being measured
is "does SPY momentum persist a month out" — and `ret20` is a momentum proxy in the same family as
the feature that already inverted on full-year data in v0.32.90.

Over Feb–Jul 2026 it persisted in April/May and did not in June/July. **Six observations of a
phenomenon that changes on a monthly cadence cannot support a rule, at any effort level.**

### What it would take

~3+ years of replay for 36+ monthly observations. `historical_prices` starts 2024-05-31 for most
tickers and `iv_history` is far shorter, so this is not testable on the current panel.

**Disposition: do not build.** Re-open only if the price/IV panel reaches 3+ years AND the feature
is pre-registered before outcomes are inspected.
