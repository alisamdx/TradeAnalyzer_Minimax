# v0.22.21 — 2026-06-02

## Quality Signals Wired into Scoring Engine and Daily Brief

Closes the gap between the screener's WheelMetrics quality columns (added in v0.22.20) and the
Trade Scoring Engine. The daily brief now uses FCFToNetIncome, ROIC, NetDebtToEBITDA,
RevenueGrowth5Y, and EPSGrowth5Y when scoring tickers and generating AI rationales.

### Changes

#### `scoreFundamentals()` — updated metric set

Previous composite used ROE, EPS growth, profit margin, revenue growth, D/E, free cash flow.
Updated to incorporate capital efficiency and cash quality:

| Metric | Max pts | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ROE | 15 | Profitability proxy |
| Profit Margin | 10 | |
| ROIC | 15 | Capital efficiency (new) — NOPAT / invested capital |
| FCF / Net Income | 15 | Cash quality (new) — confirms earnings aren't inflated |
| EPS Growth (1Y) | 15 | |
| Revenue Growth (1Y) | 10 | |
| Debt / Equity | 10 | Balance sheet safety |
| Free Cash Flow > 0 | 10 | Binary: positive FCF = full points |

Score is normalized 0–100 using available metrics only (missing fields excluded from denominator).

#### `computeEarningsGrowthScore()` — 5Y blending

The `earningsGrowth` signal (used in Bullish + Buy strategies) now blends 1-year and 5-year CAGR
with 1.5× weight on the 5Y signals to reward compounders over cyclical spikes:

```
score = (epsGrowth1Y×1 + revGrowth1Y×1 + epsGrowth5Y×1.5 + revGrowth5Y×1.5) / Σweights
```

When 5Y data is unavailable (company < 5 years public or Polygon < 20 filings), falls back to
1Y-only behavior — no behavioral regression.

#### New explicit scoring signals

| Signal key | Strategy | Weight | Direction | NormMax | Description |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `leverageQuality` | Wheel | 8 | lower_better | 5 | ND/EBITDA < 3 = safer stock to hold on CSP assignment |
| `leverageRisk` | Bearish | 8 | higher_better | 8 | High leverage amplifies drawdowns — strongest when ND/EBITDA > 5 |
| `revenueGrowth5Y` | Bullish | 10 | higher_better | 30 | 5Y revenue CAGR — compounders get institutional tailwind |
| `revenueGrowth5Y` | Buy | 10 | higher_better | 30 | Same — filters cyclical YoY spikes |
| `epsGrowth5Y` | Buy | 8 | higher_better | 40 | Sustained earnings growth → long-term price appreciation |

#### AI rationale impact

`pick.signals` now includes the new signal keys (when non-null) with their raw values. The AI
synthesis step can produce rationale sentences like:

- *"ROIC at 21% (scoring 78) confirms capital efficiency above our 15% threshold."*
- *"ND/EBITDA of 1.8 (leverageQuality score 64) — manageable leverage for Wheel assignment."*
- *"5Y revenue CAGR of 18% (score 60) — sustained growth, not a cyclical spike."*

### Cache / DB notes

- Existing `scoring_strategy_configs` rows in the DB are **not auto-updated** (`seedBaselines`
  uses `INSERT OR IGNORE`). To pick up the new signals for an existing install:
  **Scoring Config UI → Reset to Baseline** for each strategy.
- `fundamentals_cache` rows pre-dating v0.22.20 will return null for quality fields until
  re-fetched. Run **Cache → Refresh** to repopulate, or wait for 24h TTL.
