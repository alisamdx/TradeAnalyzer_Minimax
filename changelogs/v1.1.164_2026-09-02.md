# v1.1.164 — 2026-09-02

## feat: Roll the Dice gates on capital, plus a defined exception for better setups

Two operator requests: *"switch the gate to capital required, $30,000 ceiling"*, and *"if there is
genuinely good opportunity, I am willing to commit more capital, however we need to define what it
means."*

### 1. The gate is now capital, not share price

Share price was a lossy proxy. The CSP strike sits *below* spot, so what is actually tied up is
`strike × 100`:

```
a $306 stock struck at $285  ->  $28,500 committed
a $299 stock struck at $295  ->  $29,500 committed   (the cheaper-looking one costs more)
```

Over 62 stored wheel briefs the switch moved the hit rate **23 → 25 days (37% → 40%)**, admitting
three AAPL setups whose strike was at or under $300 while spot was over it.

### 2. The exception tier, defined

An exception mechanism is how a disciplined filter becomes a rationalisation engine, so the
stretch tier demands **more** evidence on every axis, all of it committed *before* the setup
appeared. Capital may reach **$50,000** when **all** hold:

| Requirement | Why this one |
|---|---|
| On the conviction list | A name already thought about calmly. A ticker allowlist was rejected — favourite names are exactly where overconfidence lives. |
| Strike ≤ `price_high` | The operator's own "never enter above this". The strike is the price actually paid on assignment, so this asks: *have I already ruled this price out?* |
| Composite ≥ 70 | Eligible picks run 58.9–76.0, median 65.9, p90 73.3. |
| Liquidity ≥ 11/15 | Median eligible is 11; a bigger position needs a better exit, not an equal one. |
| Capital ≤ $50,000 | An exception without a bound is not an exception. |

Over the same 62 briefs it fires **twice** — AMD on 2026-06-12 ($47k, score 73.8) and 2026-06-18
($49k, score 73.2) — lifting the overall rate to **26 of 62 days (42%)**. Rare, which is the point.

### Two calibration findings that changed the design

**`criticVerdict.confidence` is not a discriminator.** It reads `high` on **40 of 41** eligible
picks. Requiring it would have added the appearance of rigour and none of the substance, so it is
deliberately excluded.

**A first draft used composite ≥ 75 and fired on nothing** — the observed maximum is 76.0. The
threshold was set by intuition, checked against the distribution, and corrected to 70.

### A data-quality note on the conviction list

Three of thirteen rows have `price_low > price_mid` (MSFT 433/409, NOW 123/108, PLTR 152/133),
which inverts the intended low → mid → high ordering. The gate therefore uses **`price_high`
only**, the one band whose meaning is unambiguous regardless. Worth correcting the rows, but the
feature does not depend on it.

### Presentation

A stretch pick is announced **before** the order in both the email and the panel — amount and
reason — so it can never arrive looking like an ordinary night's trade.

### Correction to yesterday's analysis

I reported SNOW as failing only the $300 price limit, "out by $5.84", and used it to motivate this
change. That was wrong: the diagnostic evaluated price before liquidity and short-circuited. SNOW
fails on **liquidity 7/15** and was never eligible. The capital-gate reasoning stands on its own
(and on the three AAPL setups it genuinely admits), but that example did not support it.
