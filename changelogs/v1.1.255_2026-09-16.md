# v1.1.255 — 2026-09-16

## The stale-Greek guard is on

`STALE_GREEK_GUARD` (`src/main/services/greek-consistency.ts`) now defaults on. `ETradeDataProvider.getOptionsChain` withholds delta, gamma, theta and vega from a chain whose deltas imply a spot more than 1 % from both the chain price and the put-call-parity price. Every consumer reads chains through that call, so:

- Strategy Lab (and through it the brief and Roll the Dice) selects that ticker's legs by moneyness.
- The wheel shortlist and the analysis-service CSP and jade-lizard paths skip the chain, since they select only by delta.
- Prices and IV are untouched.

## Evidence and the FR-SEY-5 exception

`scripts/pick-greek-audit.mjs`, 2026-01-01 to 2026-09-16, 1,070 stored candidates:

| disposition | stale / invertible |
|---|---|
| picked | 33 / 322 (10.2 %) |
| capped_out | 33 / 305 (10.8 %) |
| failed_liquidity | 141 / 389 (36.2 %) |

The audit uses one contract per pick against the stored spot, with no parity reading and the fixed 5 % rate, so gaps near 1 % sit at its noise level. The material rows are the 7 at 2.8 % or more: OLLI 9/16 wheel put (delta implied 76.09 on 73.23; TradeAnalyzer 2 measured 76.12 independently), ORCL 9/10 wheel and income puts (158 on 152.94), AVAV 9/9 income put (145.35 on 140.80), TWLO 9/4 bullish put and buy call (about 226 on 232.98), GFL 9/11 income put (41.67 on 42.85). For the three short puts whose delta implied a higher price, the put was nearer the money than the recommendation stated.

A replay cannot show this change neutral, because stored candidates keep the stale delta but not the chain. It ships as an **operator-approved exception to FR-SEY-5**, recorded in `Requirements/v2-execution/06-seykota-risk-extensions.md`. It alters a data input, not a scoring weight or policy, and adds no selection rule of its own. `STALE_GREEK_GUARD=0` restores the previous behaviour for a comparison run.
