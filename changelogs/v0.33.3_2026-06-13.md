# v0.33.3 — 2026-06-13

## Brief Replay Score-Signal tab shows all strategies

The score-quintile and pick-rank charts on the Score-Signal tab were hardcoded to curated
3-strategy subsets (buy·bullish·bearish / buy·bullish·wheel), so inflection — and income/
wheel — never appeared there.

- `quintileData` / `rankData` now build a column per strategy from `data.strategies` (filters
  removed); the charts and legend render `signalStrats` (all strategies in the payload) with
  a `STRAT_COLORS[s] ?? '#888'` fallback.
- Titles updated to "(all strategies)"; inflection's values are its direction-adjusted 21d return.
- Display-only change; `tests/replay-direction.test.ts` already asserts inflection is present in
  the chart payload. Suite: 296 passing.
