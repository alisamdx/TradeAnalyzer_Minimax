# v0.22.29 — 2026-06-03

## Fix: Daily Brief email/UI mismatch + date shows tomorrow

### Bug 1 — Email 25 picks vs UI 19 picks
Email was built from `topPerStrategy` (pre-liquidity-gate top 5) while the job
persists `liquidFiltered` picks (post-gate). Anything dropped by the liquidity
gate appeared in the email but not in the UI.

**Fix**: Collect persisted picks in `persistedPicks: Map<ScoringStrategy, BriefPick[]>`
during the Stage 6 loop and pass that to `buildEmailHtml` / `buildPdf` instead
of rebuilding from `topPerStrategy`. Email and UI now show identical data.

### Bug 2 — Brief history shows tomorrow's date
`generated_at` is stored as UTC via SQLite's `strftime('now')`. A run at
10:30pm ET on 6/3 stores `2026-06-04T02:30:00Z`. All history date
comparisons and display used `.slice(0, 10)` which gave the UTC date `2026-06-04`
instead of the ET date `2026-06-03`.

**Fix**: Added `toETDate(isoStr)` helper in `DailyBriefView.tsx` that converts
the UTC ISO string to an ET date string. All history grouping, active-state
comparison, and date button labels now use `toETDate()`.
