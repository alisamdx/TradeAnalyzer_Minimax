# v1.1.265 — 2026-09-17

## The workbench now reports what a reuse ruling settled

`recordReuse` built its own message and dropped `alsoResolved`, the count
`recordTickerReuse` returns. So the operator read:

> Closed 1 membership range(s) the day before 2025-10-30 and opened 1.

and could not tell a ruling that cascaded from one that resolved a single finding and left
the other twenty open. A splice settles every discontinuity, volume-regime and dormancy
finding before it — SOLS carried 21 QC-204 findings on 2026-09-17, JAN 7 — so that number
is the whole confirmation the ruling worked, and **zero is a finding in itself**: it is
what a stale build or a mis-dated splice looks like.

Now:

> Closed 1 membership range(s) the day before 2025-10-30 and opened 1. Resolved 43 further
> finding(s) the ruling accounts for. No bars were deleted — the earlier company's bars
> stay as evidence, and no longer fire.

Extracted as `reuseSummary()` and tested, for the same reason `reuseCandidateFor` was:
inline in `recordReuse` it was reachable only through the app's database, which is how it
came to omit the number in the first place. Three tests, including that zero is printed
rather than omitted.

The CLI has reported this since v1.1.262. The two callers now say the same thing, which is
the point of both going through one `core` function.
