## Fix: unhonourable pins no longer block the gate one contract at a time (QC-415)

On 2026-10-09 the gate had 84 blocking findings, and 61 of them were QC-409 ("pinned contract has no snapshot"). 60 of the 61 were pins on three underlyings E\*Trade stopped answering for on 2026-10-06:

| Ticker | E\*Trade `optionexpiredate` since 10-06 | Pins | Last pinned expiry |
|---|---|---|---|
| MDT | 204, no expirations (the old app's chain call also says "No options are available for this symbol") | 48 | 2026-10-30 |
| PSKY | 400 `10033` "The symbol entered is invalid" (last Polygon bar 10-05) | 9 | 2026-11-06 |
| WBD | 400 `10033` (last bar 10-05) | 3 | 2026-10-23 |

No re-collection can honour these pins, so each finding would have stayed blocking every night until its contract expired.

- **New QC-415** (`chains`, warning): a ticker with live pins whose options E\*Trade does not list on the trading date. That means the date's last expirations response for the ticker, across every run filed under the date, is 204 or 400, and no contract for the ticker was stored. It raises one finding per ticker, naming the pins by reason, the last date the ticker was listed and the last pinned expiry. It is promoted to **blocking** when any pin is `held_in_book`, because a held contract still needs a price. It is exempt from the consecutive-runs escalation, since this is a provider property. Every pinned ticker is evaluated, so one that is listed again closes `provider_fixed`.
- **QC-409** evaluates such a pin as not firing. It closes the pin's open finding `rule_changed` only when the ticker was also unlisted on that finding's last missed date. A gap with another cause (a dead token, a skipped run) stays open for the operator.
- A call that was never sent (latched token, `http_status` null) or a 401 is a collection failure and never reads as a delisting.

Rehearsed on the 10-09 snapshot (`rehearse-gate.mjs`): QC-409 61 → 1 and QC-415 3 (MDT, PSKY, WBD). Blocking went 89 → 29. The one QC-409 left is the CRWD pin below, which expires 2026-10-30.

## Fix: the legacy hand-off stamped the setup's expiry on every leg, and passed IV as a percentage

Both faults were in the old app's leg builder. The builder has moved to `src/main/services/legacy-handoff-legs.ts` (`handoffLegsFor`) so it can be tested.

1. **Each leg's own expiration.** Calendars, diagonals and PMCCs take their long leg from the lab's far chain (`SetupLeg.expiration`), but every leg was stamped with `setup.expiration`, the near expiry. On 2026-09-29 a CRWD far-dated 237.5 call was pinned as `CRWD--261030C00237500`. E\*Trade lists 237.5 on the 10-16, 11-20, 12-18 and 01-15 monthlies but never on the 10-30 weekly, so QC-409 fired every night. The bid, ask and IV stored beside that symbol in `legacy_brief_leg` belonged to a different contract. It is the only live pin of this kind, and it expires 2026-10-30.
2. **IV as a fraction.** The lab holds leg IV as a percentage (`contract.iv * 100`). The builder passed it through under a comment claiming the lab held fractions, and the test that 04 §8 says asserts the conversion did not exist. Every `legacy_brief_leg.iv` row before this version is a percentage in a column declared fraction (2,238 rows since 10-01, 5.0–118.5, mean 50.1). Nothing reads the column yet. Divide pre-v1.1.347 rows by 100.
