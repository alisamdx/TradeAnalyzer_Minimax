# v0.34.10 — 2026-06-14

## 💰 Dollar P&L for directional strategies (buy / bullish / bearish)

### Why
The 💰 "backtest over every pick" button only existed for the premium strategies (wheel, income, inflection) because those have an option structure to simulate. Buy and Bullish are directional stock/options signals, so the replay only showed their forward *stock returns* in percent — no dollar figure, and no way to compare buy vs bullish on a common money basis. The user asked to see dollar P&L on these so they could compare dollar-to-dollar (and wondered whether bearish was even possible).

### What
- The 💰 button now appears on the **buy**, **bullish**, and **bearish** summary rows.
- For these directional strategies there's no option sim — `BacktestEngine.backtestReplayPicks` runs a **fixed-notional stock position** straight off the forward returns the replay already records (`ret_21d`):
  - **$10,000 deployed per pick** (`STOCK_NOTIONAL`).
  - **Long** for buy/bullish, **short** for bearish (uses the stored per-pick `direction`, defaulting to long for buy/bullish and short for bearish).
  - P&L = `$10,000 × (direction × ret_21d / 100)`, marked at the replay's 21-day forward close (`close_date`).
  - Picks without a resolved `ret_21d` are counted as skipped.
- Because every pick uses the identical $10k, **Total P&L is directly comparable between buy and bullish** (and bearish). The modal footnote calls out that the premium strategies above tie up a different capital base (option collateral), so those should be compared by ROC, not headline dollars.
- **Yes, bearish works** — shorting the shares means a stock drop is a gain.

### Code
- `AggregatePickBacktest` (types.ts): `strategy` widened to `ScoringStrategy`; `structure` gains `'stock'`; new optional `notionalPerTrade`.
- `backtest-engine.ts`: `backtestReplayPicks` branches into a directional-stock path; shared aggregation (win rate, avg/median return, cumulative-$ equity curve, $ drawdown) is reused. New `STOCK_NOTIONAL = 10_000`.
- IPC (`ipc-backtest.ts`) + preload (`index.ts`): strategy param widened to the six strategies.
- `BriefReplayView.tsx`: button gating + `handlePnlBacktest`/`SummaryTable` types widened; `PickPnlModal` made structure-aware (header, subtitle, ROC→return labels, chart caption, footnote).
