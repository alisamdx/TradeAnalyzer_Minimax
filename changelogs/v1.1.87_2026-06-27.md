# v1.1.87 — 2026-06-27

## Daily Brief surfaces the defined-risk alternative for bullish picks (display-only)

The chosen follow-up to the v1.1.86 lizard-vs-spread investigation. Rather than wait for the
July backtest to (maybe) enable the engine-side rubric change, this puts the defined-risk
alternative **in front of you at decision time** — without changing what the brief recommends or
the engine trades, so there's no FR-SEY-5 gate.

### What you see

For a **bullish** pick whose recommended structure (usually a Big Lizard) carries a larger
max-loss tail, the brief now shows a green dashed chip:

```
⚖ Defined-risk alt: Bull Put Spread  A+  $475  Jul 17  $1.05 cr  PoP 71%
```

- **Grouped tab** — in the expanded pick detail, between the lab summary and the nav row.
- **Ranked Grid tab** — a compact line under the Best Structure cell.

Clicking it opens the **Payoff Visualizer** pre-loaded with the bull put spread's legs, so you can
compare it against the lizard you came in on.

### Why this is safe to ship now

It's **display-only**. The chip is computed client-side from the lab scores already stored on each
pick (`short-put-vertical` is scored for every ticker even though the bullish bucket can't recommend
it). It does **not** change:

- which structure the brief recommends or ranks,
- the Conviction score,
- anything the v2 execution engine trades.

So it falls outside FR-SEY-5 (which gates changes to *which trades the engine takes*). The engine-side
rubric change stays disabled behind `USE_POP_WEIGHTED_PREMIUM` until the July realized backtest clears
it (`scripts/backtest-lizard-vs-spread.mjs`).

### Selection rule

`definedRiskAlternative(pick, strategy)` (in `src/shared/brief-ranking.ts`) returns the spread only
when: the pick is **bullish**, the `short-put-vertical` is **constructible** (has a valid setup), it
isn't already the recommended structure, and its max loss is **strictly smaller** than the recommended
structure's. Bullish-scoped because the spread is the wrong direction/intent for the other strategies.

### Tests

`tests/brief-ranking.test.ts` (+5, 14 total): surfaces the spread for a smaller-risk bullish case,
null for non-bullish strategies, null when the spread is unconstructible, null when it isn't actually
smaller-risk, null with no lab result. Full suite **780 passing**.
