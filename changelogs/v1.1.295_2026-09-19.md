## 🪜 Spread Shadow: the bull put spread shadow report in the app

The operator asked for the shadow report (`scripts/spread-shadow-report.mjs`) as an in-app screen. The new **🪜 Spread Shadow** view, under Intelligence after Replay, shows:

- **Enable gate:** three tiles with progress bars: intraday capture days (/15), settled positions (/30), and mean return on max loss (> 0), plus win rate and median hold.
- **Sector cap:** the full book next to the 3-per-sector capped book (positions, settled, mean return, win rate, skipped), with chips for open positions by sector (amber at the cap, red over it) and by expiry.
- **Positions**, newest first, filterable by all, open, settled or unsettleable: entry and slot, ticker (+N repeats), sector, a *cap: skipped* badge, expiry, strikes, credit, max loss, status, exit or last mark, return, and marks/gaps. Open positions show an *~italic* paper value at the last mid, labelled as not a result and not a stop signal.
- **Rejection reasons** with tickers and missing-leg causes, and **runs** (collapsed).

It is a reading surface, not the `on` panel of 08 §3.9: it never presents a pick to act on.

## Plumbing

- The IPC channel `spread-mode:shadow-report` (`src/main/ipc/ipc-spread-mode.ts`) returns `buildSpreadShadowReport`, the same function behind `GET /api/v1/diagnostics/spread-shadow`.
- Preload adds `window.api.spreadMode.shadowReport()`.
- The report types (`SpreadShadowReport`, `ShadowPickRow`, `SpreadSettlement`, `BookSummary`, `LegCause`, `ShadowPick`) moved from the main-process service to `src/shared/spread-mode.ts` so the renderer can use them. The service re-exports them.
