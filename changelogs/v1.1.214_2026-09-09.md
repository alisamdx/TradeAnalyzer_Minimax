# v1.1.214 — 2026-09-09

`@tradeanalyzer/core` 0.3.5, dictionary **v9**.

Hosts NewApp's **phase 9** — the IV30 derivation — in the collector's daily run.
Full detail lives in `NewApp/CHANGELOG.md`; what matters to this application:

## Why this ships before the rest of B7b

**The 20-day overlap clock starts when phase 9 first runs in production.**
Acceptance of a purchased IV bootstrap requires 20 trading days on which *both*
series carry a value for the same tickers, and our side is at zero. Shipping the
derivation with the remainder of B7b would have started that clock about four
weeks later for no gain.

## What changes here

- `runDaily` gains a `derive` phase after `events`. It calls no provider, reads
  what the chain phase stored, and writes `derived_iv30_daily` — one row per
  ticker, **including the tickers it could not compute**.
- A day with no chain is a no-op, not an error. The chain phase's own counts
  already report an absent chain.
- Dictionary **v8 → v9**: `source_response_id` and `capture_rule_id` are
  nullable on the `derived` layer. A derived row is a function of many stored
  rows, not a parse of one response — the table as declared at B2 could not have
  been written at all. AC-1 is untouched; it is scoped to raw tables, and a test
  pins that the relaxation does not reach them.
- The generation is empty in that table, so the change applies in place through
  the B7a empty-table rebuild — no reset.

## Also

`COLLECTOR_VERSION` caught up to `0.3.5`. It had been left at `0.3.4` when
`packages/core/package.json` was bumped in v1.1.212, and it is what lands in
`meta_run.collector_version` — so runs were labelling themselves with the wrong
collector. The purity test that pins the two together is what found it.

`1,253` tests here, `324` in `core`.
