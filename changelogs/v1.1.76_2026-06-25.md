# v1.1.76 — 2026-06-25

## Pre-Trade Validator (Payoff view)

Validate a hand-built Payoff structure **before placing it**: declare your intent, get a
verdict, and — when flagged — see a better structure the Strategy Lab suggests. Decision-support
only; it does not place orders and is not wired to the execution engine.

Origin: a 2026-06-25 position-by-position review where the same NVDA short put was a *bad
premium trade* (IV rank 25 → cheap vol) but a *fine ownership trade* — proving the verdict must
be **intent-aware**. See `Requirements/PRE_TRADE_VALIDATOR_SPEC.md`.

### Flow
1. Build legs in the Payoff view as usual.
2. Set intent: **purpose** (income / ownership / directional / hedge), **happy to own at the
   strike?**, **multi-quarter thesis?** (remembered across validations this session).
3. **Validate Trade** → verdict pill (good / caution / bad) + an ordered list of checks, and
   when flagged, a **Lab suggests instead** block with a **Refresh with live chain** action.

### Deterministic checks (the trustworthy core — pure, unit-tested)
- **Reward vs risk** — `maxProfit / |maxLoss|`, but **only for defined-risk spreads** (a long
  option leg caps the loss). Cash-secured puts / covered calls are skipped here (their max loss
  is the whole strike → the ratio would mislead) and judged by cushion + ownership basis.
- **Undefined risk** — uncovered short leg → warn.
- **IV-regime fit** — selling net credit at IV rank < 30 → "selling cheap volatility"; buying
  net debit at IV rank > 60 → "buying expensive volatility". (The NVDA insight.)
- **Direction vs trend** — structure's net-delta sign vs the lab's MA-based bias; a conflict is
  a warning unless intent is ownership/hedge/happy-to-own (then it's a conscious fade → info).
- **Earnings before expiry** — warning, downgraded to info when happy to own at the strike.
- **Cushion vs volatility** — short-strike OTM % vs `atr_pct`; thin (< 1.5× ATR) or ITM → warn.
- **Horizon match** — multi-quarter thesis in a < 60-DTE structure → warn.
- **Ownership basis** — info: effective basis = strike − net credit (single short put).

Verdict roll-up: any `fail` → bad; any `warn` → caution; else good.

### Suggest-a-better-structure
When flagged, `pickSuggestedStructure` chooses the Strategy Lab's best valid structure for the
intent (income → credit; ownership → put-based credit; directional → matches bias; hedge →
protective), defined-risk-preferred then highest grade, and notes when even that is selling into
low IV ("consider waiting for higher IV or simply owning"). This subsumes the rejected
"CSP × big-lizard hybrid" — there is no edge-bearing structure *between* a CSP and a big lizard,
so the validator just picks the right existing one for the regime + intent.

### Data & reuse (~80% existing)
- Payoff metrics: computed renderer-side (unchanged), passed in.
- Lab context + candidate structures: `StrategyLabService.validate` (IV rank, direction bias,
  MA20/50, scored setups), **cached 10 min** per ticker; the **Refresh** button forces a fresh
  chain fetch.
- Earnings: `earnings_calendar` (nearest-date, post v1.1.75 fix). ATR: `technical_signals.atr_pct`.

### Notes / follow-ups (non-blocking)
- Verdict thresholds (`PRETRADE_THRESHOLDS`: low IV 30, high IV 60, cushion 1.5× ATR, R/R
  fail 0.10 / warn 0.25, short-horizon 60 DTE) are first-pass — tune once it judges real trades.
- LLM critic narrative is **not** auto-run (deterministic checks are free + instant); could be
  added as an opt-in button. Validating an **existing open position** (scope b) is a natural
  follow-up. Both recorded in the spec's deferred questions.
