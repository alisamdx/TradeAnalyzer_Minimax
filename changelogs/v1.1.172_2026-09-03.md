# v1.1.172 — 2026-09-03

## T4 — ETF-aware wheel scoring ENABLED, after resolving an unsatisfiable validation gate

`ETF_AWARE_WHEEL_SCORING` is now `true`. ETFs stop being scored as though they had *failed*
two signals they cannot possess.

### The distortion

Wheel's `fundamentalScore` (17) and `leverageQuality` (4) are structurally inapplicable to a fund
wrapper — no P/E, no ND/EBITDA. Measured: **56 of 60 curated ETFs have no `fundamentals_cache`
row**, while all 60 have `iv_history` and `historical_prices`. Under FR-SE-03 a missing signal
contributes 0 to the numerator but keeps its **full weight in the denominator**, so every ETF's
wheel composite was capped at **70/91 = 76.9%** of an otherwise-identical stock. The fix excludes
both from numerator *and* denominator and renormalises — a **×1.30** uplift, ETFs only.

### The gate was unsatisfiable, and that was the finding

The code required "a Brief Replay improvement (or at least neutrality) on ETF wheel outcomes
before shipping enabled." **No such outcome could exist:**

- **0** ETF picks across 62 stored briefs (248 picks)
- **0** ETF appearances in 88 wheel analysis snapshots covering 1,077 tickers
- **0** ETF rows in `options_premium_history` — no ETF CSP has ever been analysed, let alone settled

None could be generated while the flag was off, because the deflation is precisely what kept ETFs
out of the Stage-4 pool where options data is fetched. **The distortion suppressed the evidence
that would justify removing it.** `scripts/fr-etf-wheel-deflation-study.mjs`.

### Resolved inside Brief Replay, where the ON arm can produce ETF picks

`scripts/replay-arm.mjs` (new) captures and compares replay arms, since replay runs only inside
Electron and overwrites `replay_briefs`/`replay_outcomes` each run. Both arms: **45 dates,
2026-06-02 → 2026-08-05, 225 wheel picks each, 100% of outcomes matured.**

| | OFF | ON |
|---|---|---|
| wheel picks | 225 · 53 tickers | 225 · 56 tickers |
| ETF picks | **0** | **6 (2.7%)** — QQQ ×2, IYR ×2, XRT, KBE |
| stayed above strike | 77.8% | 77.8% |
| mean composite | 67.5 | 67.5 |

Six swaps, each an ETF displacing exactly one stock — and **in every case on a higher composite**
(66.7 vs 66.1, 66.4 vs 65.3, 64.5 vs 63.8, 68.4 vs 67.7, 69.0 vs 68.3, 69.3 vs 65.8). The ranking
does its job once the distortion is removed.

| swapped picks | n | stayed above strike | mean 21d return |
|---|---|---|---|
| added (ETFs) | 6 | **100%** | +0.22% |
| dropped (stocks) | 6 | **100%** | +3.49% |

**Exact tie on the metric that maps to whether the cash-secured put paid.** The 21-day underlying
return favours the dropped stocks, but a short put does not capture upside — it is capped at the
premium — so that column credits PAYX's +15.44% run with money the CSP would never have earned.
Neutrality met; the pre-registered condition fires.

### What this test does NOT establish

**All 12 swapped picks stayed above their strike.** The metric did not discriminate — it is a tie
because *nothing failed in either arm*, not because ETFs were shown to handle stress well. Six
swaps, 45 dates, one regime. The honest claim is "no harm detected in a window with no harm to
detect."

Two structural caveats also bound it: replay has no historical chains, so wheel outcomes are
stock-return proxies against a synthetic strike; and replay runs blind to `premiumYield` (19) for
every ticker, which leaves ETFs populated 51/70 against stocks' 72/91 even with the fix on.
**Replay therefore still under-ranks ETFs relative to live**, so 2.7% is a floor on the real
effect, not a ceiling.

### Rollback

`SCORING_ETF_AWARE_WHEEL` now overrides in **both** directions, checked live: `'1'` forces on,
`'0'` forces off. The `'0'` path is the rollback if ETF picks misbehave in live use, and it keeps
the pre-fix deflation exactly reproducible as a regression baseline (58.3 vs 73.8 on the test
fixture — a 21% penalty for a reason unrelated to trade quality).
