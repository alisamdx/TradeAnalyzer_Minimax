# v0.27.3 — 2026-06-06

## "Resend Email" button in Daily Brief

A new **Resend Email** button in the Daily Brief toolbar re-generates the email and PDF from the last saved brief and sends it to your inbox — without re-running the full 6-stage job.

Useful for:
- Validating email/PDF layout changes on non-trading days
- Re-sending after updating email settings
- Getting a fresh copy after the original send failed

The button shows a confirmation message for 6 seconds after sending. If email is not configured or no brief exists in the database, it shows an error message inline.
