# v1.1.5 — 2026-06-14

## v2 execution subsystem — Phase 4a (entry policy)

The first half of the trade logic: eligible brief picks → priced, risk-gated proposals
awaiting approval (semi-auto). Still dormant — wired to nothing yet.

### Migration 046 — entry-policy config

Adds the configurable entry filters/caps (03-lifecycle §3.6) to the singleton
`exec_config` row: `min_composite_score` (70), `max_new_entries_per_day` (1),
`max_new_entries_per_week` (3), `avoid_earnings_before_expiry` (on), `entry_window_start`
/`_end` (10:00–15:00 ET), `reprice_tolerance_pct` (10). Surfaced through `ExecConfig` +
`RiskGovernor.getConfig/updateConfig` (boolean→0/1 coercion added).

### `entry-policy.ts` — `EntryPolicy`

Operates on a normalized `PickInput` (decoupled from v1 brief internals; fully testable
against the mock):

- **`filterPick`** — eligible strategy · critic `pass`/`caution` (never `flag`/none) ·
  composite ≥ floor · adequate liquidity · earnings-before-expiry · entry window (ET).
- **`buildProposal`** — price the combo at the venue (`broker.priceCombo`) → size via
  `governor.sizeEntry` → run the pre-trade gate (`governor.checkEntry`) → persist a pending
  `exec_proposals` row with shelf-life; audited `proposal_created`. Reports the reject
  reason when it can't.
- **`generateEntries`** — applies daily/weekly caps (counting pending/approved proposals +
  open positions), highest composite first.

`market-hours.ts` gains `etMinutesOfDay` + `hhmmToMinutes` for the entry-window check.
