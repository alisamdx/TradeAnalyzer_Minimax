# v0.22.15 — 2026-06-01

## Daily Brief — hard liquidity gate + specific strike/expiry/premium on pick cards

### Problem

The brief was recommending stocks with OI=1 at the relevant strike/expiry because
the composite score is computed from *cached* `analysis_snapshots` (stale liquidity
data) rather than the live chain. Strategy Lab running during Stage 4 did flag the
problem via a low grade, but the stock still appeared as a pick.

### Fix 1 — Hard liquidity gate (Stage 6)

After Strategy Lab validation, picks are now dropped if the best relevant strategy
structure has `liquidityScore < 5` (out of 15).

**Threshold math:**
| OI | Spread | liquidityScore | Result |
|----|--------|----------------|--------|
| 1 | wide (>15%) | 3 | **Dropped** |
| 1 | moderate | 7 | Kept (tight spread makes it workable) |
| <100 | wide | 3 | **Dropped** |
| ≥100 | any | ≥5 | Kept |

Only strategies where the relevant structure exists AND has an available setup
are gated — if E*Trade was unavailable or the structure couldn't be priced, the
pick passes through (can't gate without data).

The batch job progress message and notes now report how many picks were dropped:
`Brief generated — 18 picks | 3 dropped (low liquidity)`.

### Fix 2 — Specific strike + expiry + premium on the pick card

The Strategy Lab grade row now includes a second line showing the exact setup
for the relevant structure:

```
CSP  B
$240 · Jul 17 · $3.25 cr
```

This directly answers "which strike, which expiry, at what premium" without
opening Strategy Lab.

- `primaryStrike()` — returns the short leg's strike (or first leg if all long)
- `fmtExpShort()` — formats ISO expiry as "Jul 17"
- Credit shown in green, debit in amber
