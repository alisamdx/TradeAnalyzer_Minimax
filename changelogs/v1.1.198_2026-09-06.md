# v1.1.198 — 2026-09-06

## feat — `backfill_bars` and the exception review queue (NewApp B5)

`collector-oneshot` now implements `backfill_bars` alongside `universe_seed`,
and expires stale acceptances before any job reports. Still change #4 of the
seven in `NewApp/requirements/06 §5`.

**A red verdict from the backfill is a `success`.** The review queue it opens
*is* the deliverable — the operator has to rule on ticker-reuse candidates
before the pilot's first clean day counts (04 §7.2). A failed job would say the
collector broke.

**Stale acceptances expire first.** Otherwise the email and the review queue
describe what was outstanding yesterday rather than what is outstanding now.

Everything else is in `@tradeanalyzer/core` — see `NewApp/CHANGELOG.md`.
