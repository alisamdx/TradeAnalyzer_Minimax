## Defect

`volumeRegimeAroundSteps` took `series.slice(i - 20, i)` and `series.slice(i, i + 20)` — twenty stored bars. A sparse series' twenty bars span years, so the windows of the shell's own sub-penny steps (0.0001 ↔ 0.000001 is a 99 % move) extended past 2025-10-30 into Solstice's trading.

## Change

- QC-205 windows are `[s − 20, s − 1]` and `[s, s + 19]` in exchange-session index (`tradingDaysInRange` over the series span), each measured only with ≥ `MIN_WINDOW_BARS` (15) volumes.
- The real SOLS splice then has an empty before-window and is not measured by QC-205 — so **QC-209 `dormant series resumes`** (blocking, `thresholdBasis: working`): for each step, `gap = session(cur) − session(prev) − 1`; fired when `gap ≥ 20` and bars exist on ≥ 15 of the 20 sessions from the step. Every step is recorded with its gap. QC-206 (series consistency after re-fetch) and QC-208 (zero-volume day) were already specified in 03 §3.3; QC-209 is the next free id.
- Shared loader `latestSeries`: latest per `(ticker, trade_date)` by `MAX(row_id)`, grouped by ticker, session index over the span.
- Wiring: `BAR_CHECKS`, the backfill gate, `backfillQueueClear` (QC-204/205/209), `next` `REUSE_CHECKS`, Pilot view label.
- Spec 03 §3.3: QC-205 row revised (sessions, ≥ 15 bars), QC-209 row added.
