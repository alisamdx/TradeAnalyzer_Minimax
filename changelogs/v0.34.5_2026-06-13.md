# v0.34.5 — 2026-06-13

## Backtest: drop Starting Capital + sortable trade log

### Dropped Starting Capital
The manual backtest is a one-contract-at-a-time strategy, so a user-entered capital figure
added friction and caused silent flat-zero results on high-priced stocks (the META case).
Removed the form field. The engine now auto-sizes the capital base to one contract
(max spot × 100 ≥ any CSP collateral) and removes the capital gate, so trades always open
one-at-a-time. Metrics (total return %, drawdown, Sharpe, annualized) are now 'return on
the capital one contract ties up.' starting_capital stays in the schema/type for
back-compat but is unused; the v0.34.4 'raise capital' error is removed (moot).

### Sortable trade log
Trade-table columns are click-sortable (toggle asc/desc, nulls last), defaulting to entry
date descending (most recent first).

Tests updated: high-priced stock now runs (no capital gate). Suite 309 passing.
