# v0.27.5 — 2026-06-06

## Daily Paper Trade Snapshot job

New batch job: **Daily Paper Trade Snapshot** — runs at 17:00 ET, after the price-gap-fill and Daily Brief.

### What it does

For every paper trade (any status) whose entry date is before today, inserts one `paper_trade_snapshots` row per available EOD close date not yet recorded. Each row captures:

- `snapshot_date` — the trading calendar date
- `days_held` — calendar days since entry
- `price` — historical closing price
- `return_pct` — (price − entry) / entry × 100
- `verdict` — positive / negative / neutral (same strategy-aware thresholds as the live view)

### Idempotency

`UNIQUE(paper_trade_id, snapshot_date)` + `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` — running the job multiple times in a day writes zero duplicate rows. Completely safe.

### What this enables (next phase)

- **Scoring engine accuracy at fixed horizons** — Day+1, Day+5 win rates per strategy
- **Brief-to-brief comparison** — every brief measured at the same time horizon, not "today's price vs entry"
- **Faster signal-weight tuning feedback** — see accuracy improvement within days, not after options expire
