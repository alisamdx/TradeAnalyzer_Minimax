# v0.24.4 — 2026-06-05

## refactor: Daily Brief and Options Snapshot use full universe, not screener

### Problem
The Daily Brief and Options Snapshot were reading candidates from `screen_results`
(the screener's output). This had two problems:

1. **Stale data dependency** — if the screener hadn't run that day the Brief worked
   off old candidates, even after Market Sync had refreshed all underlying cache data.
2. **Bearish strategy blind spot** — the strict screener filters OUT overleveraged,
   declining-earnings stocks. Those are exactly the stocks the Bearish strategy needs
   to find. A hard quality gate applied uniformly across all 5 strategies was wrong.

### Fix
`loadCandidates()` in `DailyIntelligenceBriefJob` and `loadTopCandidates()` in
`DailyOptionsSnapshotJob` now query the `constituents` table directly:

```sql
SELECT DISTINCT ticker FROM constituents
WHERE index_name IN ('sp500', 'russell1000', 'etf')
```

The scoring engine already has fundamental quality built in as weighted signals
(ROIC, FCFToNetIncome, earnings growth, leverage risk/quality, relative strength,
relative weakness). Weak fundamentals naturally score low for Buy/Wheel/Bullish
and high for Bearish — no pre-filter needed.

### What the Daily Screener job does now
The `DailyScreenerJob` still runs in the batch sequence and still populates
`screen_results`. It serves the **Screener UI** — when you open the Screener tab
you see fresh results from today's run. It is no longer in the Brief's data path.

### Impact
- Daily Brief candidate pool: ~1,100 stocks + 61 ETFs (was ~200–300 screener passes)
- Bearish strategy can now surface fundamentally weak stocks
- No stale-screener risk — Brief always has the full universe available
- `gatherSignalData()` is batched DB reads — scoring 1,100 tickers adds < 1s
