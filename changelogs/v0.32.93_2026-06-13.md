# v0.32.93 — 2026-06-13

## Bearish composite quality gate (≥70); SPY regime gate reverted

### Summary

Bearish was the worst strategy by return-on-capital over the full-year Brief Replay
(2025-05-01 → 2026-06-12): −1.09% per pick, 48.8% win rate, losing real money in a
bull market. Analysis showed the bearish *signal* is not broken — it picks genuine
relative underperformers (−1.01% avg alpha vs SPY) — but the edge is concentrated in
high-composite-score picks. This release gates bearish on score and removes the
previously-shipped SPY regime gate, which the full year disproved.

### Added

- **`minCompositeForStrategy(strategy)`** in `scoring-service.ts` — per-strategy minimum
  composite score to surface a pick. `{ bearish: 70 }`; all others 0 (ungated).
- **Live brief gate** — `daily-intelligence-brief-job.ts` Stage-4 pool filter now drops
  bearish candidates below 70 *before* E*Trade validation (saves validation calls; weak
  days surface zero bearish picks).
- **Replay gate** — `brief-replay-service.ts` pick selection applies the same threshold
  so replay validates the same picks as the live brief. (Existing stored replay rows
  reflect the new gate only after a re-run.)

### Removed

- **SPY regime risk gate / ⚠ badge** (`ReplaySummary.regimeRiskDates`,
  `ReplayPickRow.spyRegime`, and both badge spans in `BriefReplayView.tsx`), introduced
  in v0.32.90 on a 5-month sample.

### Data basis (full-year replay, 1,300 bearish picks)

| Cohort | n | short P&L (21d) | alpha vs SPY | win % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| All bearish (baseline) | 1300 | −1.09% | −1.01% | 48.8% |
| Composite ≥68 | 667 | +0.82% | −2.97% | 52.3% |
| **Composite ≥70 (chosen)** | **323** | **+0.99%** | **−3.32%** | **56.0%** |
| Composite ≥74 | 28 | +2.41% | −5.59% | 60.7% (too few) |

Why the regime gate was dropped — using the *actionable* entry-time signal (SPY vs its
50-day SMA), bearish in "bear regime" performed **worse**, not better:

| Cohort | n | short P&L (21d) | win % |
|---|---|---|---|
| Score ≥60, SPY-down regime at entry | 207 | −7.61% | 36.2% |

The v0.32.90 "downtrend → ~79% HR" figure was a 5-month artifact; below-SMA dips kept
V-bouncing and squeezing shorts over the full year.

### Yield

At ≥70: avg 1.24 bearish picks/date (vs. cap of 5); **108 of 260 dates surface zero**
bearish picks. Rank-agnostic — a rank-2/3 pick clearing 70 is kept; a top-ranked pick
below 70 is dropped.

### Caveat

One bull-heavy year with only ~275 down-market picks; the edge is real but the
"bad market" sample is thin. Threshold is centralized in `minCompositeForStrategy()`
for easy revision as more data accrues.

### Tests

- `tests/scoring-quality-gate.test.ts` — threshold value, ungated strategies, inclusive
  boundary, rank-agnostic slate filtering. Full suite: 263 passing.
