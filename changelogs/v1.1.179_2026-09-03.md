# v1.1.179 — 2026-09-03

## Capital utilisation — the app can finally answer "how much of my money is idle?"

The operator's observation was *"I am not fully utilizing my money, it's idle — that's a waste of
resource."* The app could not answer it at all. Searched 2026-09-03: **no table in the schema held
a cash, buying-power or net-liquidation figure**. The only columns with "cash" in the name belong
to `historical_financials` (a company's operating cash flow) and `leaps_csp_opportunities`
(a screener input). Neither is an account balance.

### Scope, as decided

| | |
|---|---|
| available cash | **E\*Trade only** |
| committed capital | **the discretionary wheel book only** — not the v2 execution broker, whose sizing rules are separate |
| Roll the Dice budget | **unchanged** — the $30,000 line stays a stated position size, not a fluctuating balance |

That last one deliberately: a sizing decision that drifts upward after a good month is exactly the
kind that stops being a decision.

### The arithmetic trap this is built around

E\*Trade **already withholds** cash-secured put collateral from `cashAvailableForInvestment`. So
available and committed are **complementary, not overlapping**:

```
total = available + committed          utilisation = committed / total
```

Deriving idle as `available − committed` would subtract the same collateral twice and report a
working wheel book as far emptier than it is. **Available *is* the idle figure.**

A second double-count sits alongside it: a **covered call commits no capital of its own** — the
shares behind it are already counted as stock — and its market value is *negative*, so including
it would move committed the wrong way. On the live book that is a −$2,852.50 line against 100 TSLA
shares. `CC` contributes exactly zero. Both traps are pinned by tests, because each would produce a
plausible-looking number rather than an obvious error.

### What was built

- **`fetchBalance` / `syncBalances`** on `EtradePortfolioService`, through `etradeGet` — never a
  hand-rolled signature, per the standing rule. `instType=BROKERAGE` and `realTimeNAV=true` are
  both required: without the first E\*Trade rejects the call, without the second the values are
  previous-close, which is the wrong number for "what can I deploy right now".
- **Migration 078 `account_balances`** — one snapshot per account per day, replacing on re-run.
  Snapshots rather than a single mutable row because *"how much is idle"* needs one number while
  *"how long has it been idle"* needs a series, and the second is the half actually asked about.
- **`capital-utilisation.ts`** — committed capital from the open book (CSP at collateral, stock at
  market with cost as fallback, long options at debit, CC at zero), combined with the latest
  balance. An unrecognised position type is **surfaced**, never silently zeroed.
- **A strip on the Portfolio view** — working / idle / utilisation %, with a bar. It warns when the
  balance is more than a day stale, and reports an idle streak: consecutive days where available
  cash never fell, which ends the moment capital goes to work.
- Every field is **null rather than 0** when absent. A missing balance and a zero balance are very
  different facts, and a wheel operator reading "$0 available" would act on it.

### Out of scope, and staying there

Which instrument to park idle cash in — money-market funds, T-bills, sweep — is **personalised
investment advice**. The app reports utilisation and does not rank or recommend places to put
money. Worth knowing while reading the number: E\*Trade sweeps uninvested cash by default, so
"idle" is not necessarily earning nothing.
