# v1.1.161 — 2026-09-02

## The FR-SEY-5 lizard-vs-spread backtest, settled — and a harness bug that inverted its sign

`v1.1.86` shipped `USE_POP_WEIGHTED_PREMIUM` **disabled**, pending a realized-P&L delta that
couldn't be measured because no trade in the sample had expired yet. That sample has now settled.

### The result (160 clean trades, June + July 2026 entries)

| | Big Lizard | Short Put Vertical |
|---|---|---|
| total P&L | **+$97,561** | +$30,482 |
| win rate | **74.4%** | 68.1% |
| avg return-on-capital | 1.30% | **2.65%** |
| worst single trade | −$7,690 | **−$4,985** |
| capital consumed | $4,924,345 | **$422,153** |

**Delta (spv − lizard) = +1.35pp — the `≥ 0` gate is MET.** The lizard earns 3.2× the dollars on
11.7× the capital; per dollar deployed the spread is ~2× as efficient with a smaller tail.

Worth noting the lizard's *higher* win rate: it wins more often and loses bigger. The v1.1.86
static estimate put its PoP at 52.7% (a coin flip) — realized settlement says 74.4%. Static PoP
geometry was not just imprecise here, it was wrong in direction, which is why FR-SEY-5 demands
realized dollars.

### The flag stays OFF anyway

The gate answers "is the spread at worst neutral against the lizard?" — yes. It does **not** answer
what enabling the flag actually does. `short-put-vertical` is in
`STRATEGY_RELEVANT_SLUGS.inflection`, **not `bullish`**. So in the bullish bucket the flag cannot
substitute the spread; it can only *dock the lizard*, redirecting picks to `long-call-vertical`,
`call-zebra`, `call-butterfly` or `poor-mans-covered-call` — **none of which this backtest
measured**. Enabling would ship an unvalidated selection change under cover of a validated one,
which is precisely what FR-SEY-5 exists to prevent.

To enable: backtest the bullish bucket's post-dock alternatives, or move `short-put-vertical` into
`bullish` (tested and rejected in v1.1.86 — changed 22.6% of picks by ~1pt).

### fix: the harness reported the wrong sign

The first run returned **−$70,955** for the lizard. Four CRWD picks carried **pre-split** strikes
(4:1 split; strike/spot **4.12×**) settled against **post-split** closes, contributing
**−$168,516** of pure fiction. Four rows out of 164 inverted the headline.

`split-repair` (v1.1.139) rewrites `historical_prices` but never the option legs frozen inside
`daily_briefs.picks` — and it shouldn't, since rewriting historical legs would falsify what was
actually recommended at the time. **The consequence is counterintuitive: repairing the price
series makes the stored legs *more* inconsistent, not less.** This bug class outlives the repair
that causes it.

The harness now carries a scale guard: a Δ0.50 short put must be struck within 35% of the
underlying's close on the pick date, and rows that disagree are **printed and excluded** rather
than dropped silently or settled as losses. `SCALE_TOLERANCE` is wide enough never to flag a
genuine ATM strike and tight enough to catch any real split ratio (2:1, the narrowest, lands at
0.5×/2.0×).

Also fixed: the harness's own query didn't select `generated_at`, which the guard needs.

**Any future replay reading stored legs needs this guard.** Recorded in CLAUDE.md.
