### Fix 2 — Grade and Score now come from the same system
The email showed two unrelated numbers side by side with no explanation:
- **`compositeScore`** (0–100): ScoringEngine rank used to SELECT which stocks
  make the top 5. A stock with score 18 can still be #3 in a strategy if the
  other candidates scored even lower.
- **`grade`** (A+/A/B/C/F): StrategyLab evaluation of the specific options structure
  (IV fit + direction bias + premium yield + liquidity).

These are independent — a stock with a low composite score can absolutely grade A
if its CSP structure is excellent. But showing them next to each other implied
they should correlate, causing user confusion.

**Fix**: The "Score" column now shows `labScore` — the StrategyLab `totalScore`
(0–100) for the relevant structure. This is the same number that drives the grade,
so Grade A always corresponds to a high lab score and Grade B to a lower one.
Column header renamed from "Score" to "Lab Score" for clarity.

Both the email HTML table and the PDF pick cards are updated.
