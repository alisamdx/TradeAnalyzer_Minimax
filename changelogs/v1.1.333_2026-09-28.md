## QC-008: a stock's capped submissions response closes once it is re-asked uncapped

Two QC-008 findings were open and **blocking** since 2026-09-15: JEF and JPM. Each file so often that
their EDGAR submissions lists came back at the provider cap under the filings sweep's old 400-day
window.

- **v1.1.275 changed the rule.** It shortened the window to 360 days, inside the year EDGAR's `recent`
  block always holds.
- **Both were asked again on 2026-09-24.** Neither answer was at the cap, and neither had a report
  filing the pipeline didn't already hold.
- **They still couldn't close.** QC-008 is keyed per response, and v1.1.283's re-judge left a stock's
  submissions finding open. Whether the capped response reached far enough back can only be read from
  its archived body, so it was left as an operator ruling.

**That was the wrong question.** The pipeline stores only `FILINGS_LOOKBACK_DAYS` of filings, and an
uncapped later answer for the same CIK holds all of them. So QC-008's re-judge now closes a stock's
submissions finding `rule_changed` when the same CIK was asked again later and that answer was not at
the cap. The note names the date and the response. A stock never asked again stays open.

**Rehearsed on 2026-09-25:** both closed, each naming its 09-24 re-ask. The open blocking queue is 13:
QC-603 ×8, QC-601 ×3, QC-401 ×1 and QC-804 ×1.
