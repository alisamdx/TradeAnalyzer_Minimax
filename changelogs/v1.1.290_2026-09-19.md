# v1.1.290 — 2026-09-19

TradeAnalyzer only. `core` and `next` are unchanged.

## Bull put spread shadow picks can now be settled, and the enable gate read

### Why

The mode has run in shadow since v1.1.244 (NewApp 08). Its enable gate (08 §4) needs ≥ 15 intraday capture days and **≥ 30 settled shadow picks with a positive mean return on max loss**. Nothing settled a pick, and nothing outside the app could read `spread_mode_picks`, so the gate could not be checked at all.

### A capture gap, fixed first

A pick is opened at 21–35 DTE and held to the 50% target or to expiry (no time stop), but `captureSpreadChains` re-quoted a series only for `trackDays` = 21 calendar days after its entry capture. A 30-DTE pick lost its daily marks for its last ~9 days. Quotes not captured on the day are gone for good, and the first picks (2026-09-14) would have started losing marks around 2026-10-05. Capture now also tracks every unexpired series a `spread_mode_picks` pick opened, however long ago, across both legs' strikes. It adds one chain call per open picked series per run.

### The settler

`src/main/services/spread-mode-settle.ts`, read-only, recomputed on each read (outcomes are not stored, per migration 084):

- **Target:** the first captured mark after the entry (date, then slot) whose **mid** debit is ≤ 50% of the net credit. The exit debit is realised with `options-fill.ts` fills, as the #43 study decides exits on mid and realises them with costs.
- **Expiry:** otherwise, intrinsic on the expiry-date close (or the last session ≤ 4 days before it), with no closing cost, settled only after the expiry date.
- **Split guard:** an expiry close outside 0.5–2× the entry spot is listed as unsettleable with the reason, not settled.
- **Positions:** repeat picks of the same ticker, expiry and strikes are one position, first entry kept. The gate counts positions.
- A mark missing either leg is a gap and is reported per position.

### Reading it

`GET /api/v1/diagnostics/spread-shadow` returns the gate progress, counts, win rate, median days held, per-run pick/rejection counts, rejection reasons grouped by kind, and every position with its settlement. `node scripts/spread-shadow-report.mjs [--all]` prints it from the running app.

### Tests

- `tests/spread-mode-settle.test.ts` (9): target on mid, realised with fills; the entry slot is never an exit; gaps; intrinsic at expiry, including max loss; open through expiry day; unsettleable without a close; split guard; reason grouping; position collapse and gate progress; holiday-expiry close fallback.
