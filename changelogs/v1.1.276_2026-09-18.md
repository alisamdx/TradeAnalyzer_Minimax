## A durable "not reuse" ruling for QC-205

**What fired.** QC-205 (blocking) on ten steps on 2026-09-18. None is ticker reuse — every listing date predates its step by years and neither QC-209 nor QC-210 corroborates — and each is a real event that multiplied volume:

| finding | ratio | event |
|---|---|---|
| APLD 2022-04-13 | 104x | Applied Digital uplisted from OTC to Nasdaq |
| BMNR 2025-06-30, 07-03, 07-09 | 468x, 404x, 332x | BitMine's crypto-treasury pivot |
| GME 2024-05-13, 05-14 | 15.3x | the May 2024 squeeze |
| QXO 2024-07-24, 07-30 | 125x, 96x | Brad Jacobs' takeover of SilverSun |
| SMMT 2022-12-06 | 52x | Summit's licensing deal and rights offering |
| WAL 2023-03-13 | 15.3x | the regional-bank crisis |

This matches the 2026-09-17 measurement in 03 §3: on gen-002 the ratio is a continuum with 15x at its 90th percentile (tonight p50 1.96x, p90 15.19x, max 468x), and the threshold was deliberately left in force pending the pilot's distribution review.

**Why they could not be closed.** They are historic steps, so they fire every night. An acceptance expires (at most 180 days). A `false_positive` resolution was reopened by the very next run as a recurrence.

**The fix.** A QC-205 finding the operator resolves as `false_positive` — the ruling "this is a genuine event on a continuing listing, not reuse" — makes QC-205 evaluate that exact step as not fired from then on. The step is still measured, so the distribution the pilot review needs stays complete, and the note travels in `evidence.ruledNotReuse`. It is the counterpart of the reuse ruling, and permanent for that step because a historic step's volume regime cannot change. A new step on the same ticker is a different key and still fires. The threshold is untouched.
