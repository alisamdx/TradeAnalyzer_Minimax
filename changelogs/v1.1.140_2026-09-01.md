# v1.1.140 — 2026-09-01

## fix: a `confirmed_real` split verdict was permanent, and 3:2 produced false split labels

Follow-up to v1.1.139, from verifying four days of batch output.

### What v1.1.139 got right

The repair mechanism works. Across Aug 28 – Sep 1 the batch ran clean (zero job failures,
all slots), **8 tickers were repaired** — CRWD, DD, HON×2, KLAC, MNST, MLI, IESC — and their
series are now sane: CRWD's worst single day fell from **−74% to −20%**, HON from **+95% to
7%**. The backlog converged; by Sep 1 the scan flagged nothing.

### Bug 1 — `confirmed_real` never expired

A discontinuity that survived one provider re-fetch was written off as a real market move
and excluded from detection **forever**. That is too strong. Providers backfill corporate
actions late, so a split can be genuinely absent from the adjusted series on the day we
check and present a week later — and that row would never be looked at again. The failure
this module exists to prevent, moved one level up and made invisible.

Observed 2026-09-01, all `confirmed_real` and all still wrong in the stored series:

```
BNY  +1263%   SPCX +632%   MRNA +177%   KD  −55%
CORT  −50%    PRIM  −50%   CAR   −48%   FMC −47%
```

MRNA is the clearest: it stepped 62.96 → 174.38 and **stayed** at 133–158 through Sep 1.
A split steps and stays; a bad print reverts. The `fetched_at` on those bars confirms the
repair pass did re-fetch them and the jump survived.

**Fix:** `CONFIRMED_REAL_TTL_DAYS = 30`. The verdict is trusted for a month, then the
discontinuity falls back into detection and is re-verified. Re-confirming refreshes the
TTL, so a genuine crash costs one API call per month rather than one per run.

**Migration 069** additionally re-opens the verdicts too large to be real — `|move| ≥ 45%`,
the 8 above — so they are re-checked on the next run instead of waiting out the TTL.
Deliberately scoped: the 137 verdicts in the −32%…−37% band are left alone.

### Bug 2 — 3:2 matched ordinary crashes

`nearestSplitRatio` listed 3:2 (−33%) with a ±8% tolerance, so anything from −28% to −39%
was labelled a split. That produced every false label in the Aug 28 scan — SRPT, ALGN, ELF
and SMCI each fell 32–37% on news and none of them split. A one-third single-day fall is
routine; a halving is not.

**Fix:** 3:2 is dropped from the candidate set. Every remaining candidate implies a move of
≥46%.

**The tolerance itself stays at ±8% and must not be tightened** — a split day also carries
that day's real price move, so the observed ratio is never exact. CRWD's genuine 4:1 printed
at **0.2649** against a theoretical 0.25, 6% off, because the stock rose 6% that session. A
3% tolerance would have rejected a true split. The fix for false labels is a safer candidate
*set*, not a narrower band. `SPLIT_RATIO_TOLERANCE` is now an exported named constant so the
reasoning has somewhere to live.

The label is descriptive only — it appears in logs and never drives a repair decision.

### Also fixed — a date-fragile test

`tests/earnings-refresh-tickers.test.ts` computed fixture dates as `now + N days` without
regard to weekday. On 2026-09-01, `iso(18)` landed on Saturday 2026-09-19, the service
correctly applied its documented Saturday→Friday shift (E*Trade returns UTC, so a
Saturday-UTC date is a Friday-ET report), and the assertion failed against an unshifted
expectation. Production behaviour was correct; the helper now snaps to weekdays, which also
matches how real earnings dates fall.
