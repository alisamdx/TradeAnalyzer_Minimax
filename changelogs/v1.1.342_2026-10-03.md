# v1.1.342 — 2026-10-03

## Constituent `sector` holds the GICS sector again; sub-industry kept beside it

**Symptom.** BPS-12 found the spread mode's 3-per-sector cap running per GICS *sub-industry*
(`Application Software`, `Semiconductors`, `Fertilizers & Agricultural Chemicals`), far looser than
intended.

**Cause.** `parseWikipediaTable` chose its columns from the first data row's content. On the S&P 500
page that row is 3M, and its company test requires a name longer than 3 characters — `3M` is 2. The
company search therefore took the next qualifying column, **GICS Sector**, and the sector search,
skipping the column just taken, landed on **GICS Sub-Industry**. Every Wikipedia refresh stored
company_name = sector and sector = sub-industry. Vanguard, the primary source, overwrites company names
but carries no sector, so the names healed and the sub-industries stayed. Verified on the live page
(fetched 2026-10-03) by replaying the old detection: `ticker=0 (Symbol) company=2 (GICS Sector)
sector=3 (GICS Sub-Industry)`.

**What it affected.** The spread mode's simulated sector cap and the shadow report's sector tallies;
the screener's `sector_exclude` filter (off by default — a sector name such as "Energy" matched no
stored value); the watchlist's sector column. **Not** the v2 engine's FR-SEY-1 sector cap, which buckets
by `fundamentals_cache` sector mapped to a SPDR ETF.

**Change.**
- **`parseWikipediaConstituents`** (exported, pure) finds columns by **header name** — Symbol/Ticker,
  Security/Company, GICS Sector, GICS Sub-Industry — and keeps the content guess only as a fallback for
  unrecognised headers. It reports whether the sector came from a header.
- **Migration 091** adds `constituents.sub_industry`. `applyRefresh` carries sector and sub-industry as
  a **pair**: a source with a sector sets both, a source without one (Vanguard) keeps both — otherwise
  the delete-and-reinsert on every Vanguard run would erase the sub-industry.
- **`refreshSectorsFromWikipedia`** sets `sector` and `sub_industry` for every stored row of the S&P 500
  page's tickers, in either index, and touches nothing else. It refuses unless the "GICS Sector" header
  was found and at least 480 rows parsed.
- **`constituents-refresh`** runs it after the membership refresh. The first run after this build
  replaces the stored sub-industries; later runs also give new Vanguard members a sector. A failure
  keeps the stored sectors and reports `warning`.

Live page check: 503 rows, sector from header, the 11 GICS sectors, MMM → company "3M" / Industrials /
Industrial Conglomerates, CF → Materials.

Russell-only names (not on the S&P page) keep their existing value; the Russell 1000 has no Wikipedia
constituents table.

Tests: `tests/constituents-sectors.test.ts` (6) — header detection with 3M first (the exact failing
case), the fallback flagged untrusted, the sector refresh across both indices leaving membership and
names alone, both refusals, and a no-sector refresh keeping the pair. `constituents-refresh.test.ts`
(+2) — the job's warning on a sector-refresh failure and its note on success.
