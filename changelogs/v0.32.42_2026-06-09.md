# v0.32.42 — 2026-06-09

## Improved

### Scoring engine: trend direction signals (baseline_v3)

**Problem**: The Buy scoring engine found technically interesting setups (MACD crossover, hammer, Bollinger touch) regardless of whether the underlying trend was up, sideways, or down. A sideways range-bound stock and a confirmed uptrend stock scored identically on trend-related dimensions, meaning the brief recommended "Buy" candidates that were really income/mean-reversion setups with no sustained directional move.

**Fix**: Added explicit trend direction signals to three strategies.

#### New signal: `trendAlignment` (Buy, weight 10; Bullish, weight 7)

Maps trend direction to a 0–100 raw score:
- `bullish` → 100 (confirmed uptrend: full score)
- `sideways` → 25 (range-bound: ~7.5 pt composite penalty for Buy at wt=10)
- `bearish` → 0 (downtrend: maximum penalty)

A sideways stock that previously scored 72 would now score ~64–65 — enough to be displaced by a properly trending alternative.

#### New signal: `notDowntrend` (Wheel, weight 8)

Protects against selling CSPs on falling-knife stocks:
- `bullish` → 100
- `sideways` → 75 (mild 2 pt penalty — range-bound is acceptable for premium selling)
- `bearish` → 10 (severe ~7 pt penalty — don't sell puts into a confirmed downtrend)

#### Bearish and Income: unchanged

- Bearish already has `downtrendScore` (ADX + below-MA stack) — direction is baked in
- Income already has `trendNeutral` (low ADX = range-bound) — direction is irrelevant for short-premium strategies

#### Trend direction resolution

1. `d.trendLabel` from `analysis_snapshots` (buy mode) — explicit label from analysis engine
2. Derived from `smaStack` + `lastPrice` (from `technical_signals`, available for all ~1055 universe tickers):
   - `price > sma50 > sma200` → bullish
   - `price < sma50 < sma200` → bearish
   - mixed → sideways

#### Weight rebalancing (all sums = 100)

**Buy**: priceMomentumPct 24→22, earningsGrowth 18→17, relativeStrength 14→12, revenueGrowth5Y 7→5, sectorMomentum 6→4, epsGrowth5Y 3→2, +trendAlignment 10

**Bullish**: trendStrength 24→21, earningsGrowth 20→18, rsiMomentum 15→13, +trendAlignment 7

**Wheel**: ivRank 25→22, premiumYield 21→19, fundamentalScore 18→17, technicalNeutral 13→11, +notDowntrend 8

#### DB migration (automatic)

`seedBaselines()` now runs an `UPDATE ... WHERE version IN ('baseline_v1', 'baseline_v2')` on startup, upgrading existing installs to v3 without touching manually customized configs (which have a different version string). `resetToBaseline()` also resets to v3.
