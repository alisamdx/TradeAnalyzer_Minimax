# v1.1.176 — 2026-09-03

## F8's mechanism is refuted — premium richness is not what makes the composite anti-predictive

No behaviour change. `scripts/fr-wheel-selection-study.mjs` added.

### What was claimed, and what is now tested

v1.1.173 (T2) measured the wheel composite anti-predictive at the top of its picks: mean return on
capital **+1.91%** in the 58–63 band against **−4.99%** at 73+. It then *attributed* that to
`ivRank` (22) + `premiumYield` (19) — 41 of 91 weight — on the reasoning that premium richness is
compensation for risk rather than edge. **That attribution was inference from weights and the worst
tickers, not measurement.** This tests it.

`analysis_snapshots` holds the whole wheel universe per date — every ticker's real recommended CSP
with strike, premium, delta, IV rank and liquidity — not just the five that survived to a brief.
So selection rules can be applied to the same universe on the same dates and compared against
**no selection at all**.

**23,722 settled rows across 40 dates**, episode-collapsed, scale-guarded (10 rows rejected).

### The mechanism does not hold

| rule | Δ mean | Δ median | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **top annualized return** | **+1.03** | **+2.67** | better |
| top ivRank + annRet | +0.25 | +1.60 | better |
| top ivRank | +0.16 | +0.91 | better |
| top liquidity | +0.05 | +1.10 | mixed |
| top suitabilityScore | −0.78 | +0.84 | mixed |
| LOWEST ivRank | −0.06 | −0.11 | no different |
| **lowest annualized return** | **−1.21** | **−0.61** | **WORSE** |

Judged on medians as well as means, because short-put returns are tail-dominated — the worst
single outcome here is **−87.5%**, so a mean can be set by two names.

**Ranking on premium richness helps.** Selecting the highest annualized return is the best rule
tested; selecting the *lowest* is the worst. The relationship runs the opposite way from the one
F8 asserted.

### So the finding stands and the explanation does not

- **F8's finding survives**: the composite *is* anti-predictive at the top of the brief's picks.
  That was measured on 101 settled episodes and is unaffected by this.
- **F8's mechanism is refuted**: it is not `ivRank` + `premiumYield`. Those two are 41 of 91
  weight and, tested directly at universe scale, they rank *better* than no selection.

Which points the remaining suspicion at the **other 50 of 91**: `fundamentalScore` (17),
`technicalNeutral` (11), `recentVolatility` (10), `notDowntrend` (8), `leverageQuality` (4).
Awkwardly, 21 of those 50 — `fundamentalScore` + `leverageQuality` — draw on `fundamentals_cache`,
which has no history and is therefore permanently unvalidatable. The other 29 (`technicalNeutral`,
`recentVolatility`, `notDowntrend`) are all computable from prices and **are** testable. That is
the next study.

### The liquidity finding replicates, at 3.5× the sample

| subset | n | mean | median | trimmed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| all usable | 23,722 | +0.47% | +1.43% | +1.10% |
| liquidity ≥ 5/10 | 4,816 | **−0.58%** | +1.07% | +0.31% |
| liquidity < 5/10 | 18,906 | **+0.73%** | +1.53% | +1.29% |

The liquid subset is worse on **all three** measures. v1.1.170 reached the same conclusion on
6,777 income rows; this reproduces it on 23,722 wheel rows from an independent source. Keeping
`optionLiquidity` disabled in scoring is now supported twice over, by different data.

### Correction recorded

An earlier draft of this script claimed it was "the only study in this project that can apply the
earnings gate honestly", because `analysis_snapshots` rows carry a `daysToEarnings` field. **It is
NULL on 100% of 23,722 rows** — the column exists and is never populated. So this study cannot
evaluate the earnings gate either; the only difference from the others is that the reason is an
unpopulated field rather than a missing table.

**Follow-up worth taking:** `brief_candidates` (v1.1.175) does not capture days-to-earnings either.
Adding it would make the earnings gate testable for the first time, from that point forward. One
additive column.

### Caveats

- Tests the **mechanism**, not the composite: `analysis_snapshots` stores no `compositeScore`, and
  21 of the composite's 91 weight has no history, so a faithful replay is impossible.
- The CSP here comes from the **analysis service** (delta band 0.20–0.30), not the Strategy Lab
  profile the brief trades. Same structure and same `findSuitableChain`, different band.
- Earnings gate not applied (see above), so these returns include names that reported inside the
  hold — which the live brief excludes.
- Held to expiry at intrinsic; no roll, early close or post-assignment covered call.
- One regime: 2026-05-14 → 2026-07-29, settled through 2026-09-03.
