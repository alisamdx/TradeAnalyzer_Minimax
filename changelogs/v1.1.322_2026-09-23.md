# v1.1.322 — 2026-09-23

## The companyfacts retry could be starved, and QC-701 said otherwise

QC-701 had three findings: IQV (146 days), JCI and LNT (176 days each). All three
were diagnosed "SEC companyfacts ... still has no facts for the 10-Q ...; SEC's
aggregate is behind, not the pipeline (retried every 7 days)".

**The diagnosis is right.** I decoded the archived responses, and none contains
its company's July 10-Q:

| ticker | 10-Q accession missing from the response | newest filing in the response |
|---|---|---|
| JCI | `0000833444-26-000087` (filed 07-29) | 10-Q filed 05-06 |
| IQV | `0001628280-26-050211` (filed 07-28) | 10-Q filed 05-05 |
| LNT | `0000352541-26-000043` (filed 07-31) | 10-Q filed 05-01 |

**The retry promise was false.** IQV and JCI were fetched once, on 2026-09-15,
and never again. Two causes:

1. **Starvation.** `factsQueue` sorted every never-fetched filer ahead of every
   retry, and a run fetched 10. With 34 never-fetched filers queued, IQV and JCI
   sat 34th and 35th a week later: at least four more runs, and longer while new
   filings kept arriving.
2. **Time base.** The last fetch was `substr(requested_at, 1, 10)`, the **UTC**
   date, compared against an **ET** trading date. The 2026-09-15 21:46 ET fetch
   read as 09-16, so every retry waited eight days, not seven. It is the same
   mixed-time-base shape as the weekly-expiry quirk.

### The fix

- **`collector/facts-queue.ts`** is now the one definition of the queue, shared
  by the collector (which fetches) and QC-701 (which reports). The two had
  computed it separately, which is how the finding could promise what the queue
  did not do.
- **`factsBatch`**: 20 filers a run (`FACTS_PER_RUN`, up from 10), of which up to
  3 are reserved for due retries (`FACTS_RETRY_RESERVE`). Unused slots go to the
  other side, so a quiet day spends the batch on retries and a day with no
  retries spends it on new filings. companyfacts responses are 3–5 MB, and SEC
  allows 10 requests a second, so 20 is well inside fair access.
- **ET retry age**: `lastFactsFetchEt` converts `requested_at` to its ET date. A
  filer is due 7 ET days after its last fetch.
- **QC-701 says when**: `next retry due 2026-09-29`, or `retry due since
  2026-09-22, 1 of 2 waiting retries (3 reserved slots a run)`.

Rehearsed on 2026-09-23:

- The 2026-09-24 batch is 18 new filers plus JCI and IQV.
- LNT, fetched 09-22, reads "next retry due 2026-09-29".
- QC-701 still fires on all three, correctly, until SEC catches up.
