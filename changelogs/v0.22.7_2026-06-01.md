# v0.22.7 — 2026-06-01

## Daily Intelligence Brief & Trade Scoring Engine

Major new capability: automated "best of best" trade shortlist generated post-market by a 6-stage pipeline.

### Architecture

```
Stage 1  Screen universe (cached screener results — no live API calls)
Stage 2  Score all candidates per strategy via ScoringService (DB-only)
Stage 3  Filter overlay (+5 bonus for tickers with active filter alerts)
Stage 4  Strategy Lab deep validation for top 5 per strategy (live E*Trade)
Stage 5  AI synthesis — Claude Haiku writes 2–3 sentence rationale per pick
Stage 6  Persist brief to daily_briefs table (valid until next trading day 9:30am ET)
```

### New components

**Backend**
- `migrations/022_scoring_engine.sql` — `scoring_strategy_configs` + `daily_briefs` tables
- `src/main/services/scoring-service.ts` — `ScoringService`: config CRUD, signal normalization, composite scoring, brief persistence. Includes all 5 strategy baseline configs (expert-backed weights from tastytrade, IBD CANSLIM, McMillan).
- `src/main/services/jobs/daily-intelligence-brief-job.ts` — `DailyIntelligenceBriefJob` batch job. Registered at 16:30 ET; can also be triggered manually.
- `src/main/ipc/ipc-scoring.ts` — IPC handlers: `scoring:get-config`, `scoring:update-config`, `scoring:reset-to-baseline`, `scoring:get-latest-brief`, `scoring:get-brief-history`

**Frontend**
- `src/renderer/src/views/DailyBriefView.tsx` — brief view with strategy sections, pick cards, AI rationale, validated badge, → Strategy Lab button, history dropdown, stale detection
- `src/renderer/src/views/TradeScoringView.tsx` — config page with strategy tabs, signal rows (weight input + toggle + description), auto-normalize total, version labels, Reset to Baseline

**Types** (`src/shared/types.ts`)
- `ScoringStrategy`, `ScoringSignal`, `SignalBreakdown`, `ScoredTicker`, `ScoringConfig`, `BriefPick`, `DailyBriefEntry`

### Computed signals (Phase 1)

| Signal | Formula |
|---|---|
| `priceMomentumPct` | `(currentClose - close_252_rows_ago) / close_252_rows_ago × 100` from `historical_prices` |
| `breakoutScore` | `priceNearSupportScore×0.4 + maAlignmentScore×0.3` (volume omitted — not in analysis snapshots) |

### Phase 2 signal placeholders

`relativeStrengthVsSpy` and `sectorMomentum` appear in the Trade Scoring Config UI as disabled placeholders labeled "Phase 2". They contribute weight=0 and score=0 until Phase 2 syncs SPY/sector ETF price bars.
