## Today's 16 blocking findings: two parser faults, two check faults, one ruling

TradeAnalyzer 2 has not had a clean gate run since gen-002 began (2026-09-11). The 16 blocking
findings of 2026-10-20 (run 97) were all warnings promoted by the 3-run repeat rule, and 15 of them
were our own faults.

### QC-705 (10): the filing-instance parser

`parseFilingInstance` (`packages/core/src/ingest/edgar.ts`) disagreed with SEC companyfacts in two
ways. Both are now fixed.

- **A repeated fact kept its first copy.** An inline filing tags a number wherever it appears, and
  not always at the same precision. Narrative text such as "$0.6 million" carries `decimals="-5"`,
  while the table's exact figure carries `decimals="-3"`. The parser kept whichever came first;
  companyfacts keeps the precise one. Examples: CIK 920522's credit-loss allowance read 600,000
  against 555,000, 589,000 and 333,000. CIK 1707753's unrecognized tax benefits read 29,700,000
  against 29,669,000. Now the most precise copy wins: `INF` ranks highest, and a copy without
  `decimals` never displaces one that has them.
- **A one-day duration was keyed as a range.** A fact whose period starts and ends on the same day
  (e.g. `ProceedsFromMinorityShareholders` 2023-04-03) is an instant in companyfacts. The same fact
  therefore appeared as "only in the filing" next to "only in companyfacts", which accounted for six
  accessions entirely.

The same parser feeds TA1's fundamentals for filings companyfacts omits (`addOmittedFilings`), so
those reads now take the precise value too.

**Stored rows are not edited.** The recipe now records a parser revision (`rev: 2`,
`FILING_FACTS_PARSER_REVISION`), and `filingFactsRereadQueue` lists every filing last read by an
older revision (398 filings), newest first. The queue is served after new filings, from the same
100-a-run budget. QC-705 compares only each filing's newest instance read. The ten findings close as
their filings are re-read, over roughly the next four runs.

### QC-701 (4): one flat window for every cadence

`warnAfterDays: 120` (blocking past 180) is replaced by an allowance per filer, based on the form and
fiscal period of its newest facts:

| Next filing | Allowance | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| 10-Q | 140 days | 91 + p99 10-Q lag 44 + 5 |
| 10-K (the newest facts are a fiscal Q3) | 185 days | 91 + 90 non-accelerated deadline + 4 |
| annual 20-F/40-F filer | 485 days | a year + 120 |

The lags were measured on gen-002's stored report filings, from period end to filing date:

| Form | n | Median | p99 | Max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10-Q | 1,482 | 34 | 44 | 85 |
| 10-K | 494 | 50 | 75 | 93 |
| 20-F | 9 | 76 | 91 | 91 |

Quarter-ends are 91 days apart (p99 95). The finding's value is now days past the allowance, and it
turns blocking at 60 days past.

The flat 120 days fired on any 10-Q filer slower than 29 days, which is over half of them. From about
10-28 it would have flagged most of the universe until the Q3 10-Qs landed, and every company between
its Q3 and its 10-K. Today it held these blocking:

- AS, AU and BLSH, which are 20-F filers with FY 2025-12-31;
- AZO, which is at its fiscal Q3 with the 10-K due about 10-28.

### QC-603 (1): a superseded estimate kept a ticker "stale"

Finnhub dated IBKR 2026-10-20 until 09-24; IBKR reported on 10-15. The old estimate was still read as
"a future date last observed 09-24". Because that date was non-null, it also switched off the
just-reported grace. A row now stops counting once the same source reports a different date for the
ticker later (strictly later `observed_at`, so two dates in one response, such as FDXF's, both stay).

### QC-009 (1): FDXF needs an operator ruling

Every Finnhub response lists three fiscal quarters for FDXF in the same week (10-28 and 10-29), which
is a provider fault. It needs accepting in the Exceptions workbench; it is not changed in code.
