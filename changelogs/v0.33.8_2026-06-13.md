# v0.33.8 — 2026-06-13

## Backtest engine: price off historical IV, not realized vol

The backtest simulator priced every option from 20-day realized volatility. Premium
sellers are paid IV (typically above realized — the variance risk premium), so this
systematically under-stated CSP/CC/Wheel premium and returns.

- runSimulation now builds an as-of lookup over iv_history (ATM IV, % → fraction) and
  uses sigma = ivAsOf(date) ?? rollingVol(closes,20). Realized vol remains the fallback
  for dates with no IV history. Same sigma drives strike selection, entry premium, and
  daily mark-to-market.
- docs/formulas.md#backtest-iv documents the volatility input.

Test: backtest-iv.test.ts — same low-realized-vol price path yields a >2x richer entry
premium when 60% IV history is present (proving IV is used). Suite 304 passing.
