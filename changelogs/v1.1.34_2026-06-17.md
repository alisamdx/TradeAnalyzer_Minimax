# v1.1.34 — 2026-06-17

## ATM structure bodies centered on spot (stale-Greeks skew fix)

### The finding
On the 2026-06-17 brief the critic flagged nearly every income iron-fly and bullish big-lizard
as "not neutral — the short put is in-the-money / requires a rally." Their bodies were
systematically placed **above spot**:

| Pick | Strategy | Spot | Body | Skew |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MRVL | income iron-fly | $278.67 | $300 | **+7.7%** |
| Z | income iron-fly | $33.26 | $35 | +5.2% |
| COHR | income iron-fly | $382.81 | $400 | +4.5% |
| ALAB | bullish big-lizard | $361.71 | $380 | +5.1% (ITM put) |

Root cause: the body legs target **0.50 delta**, and `findByDelta` selected them by delta. But
E*Trade serves deltas computed at an old spot on names that have moved (the known stale-Greeks
issue), and the deviation slips under the `trustDeltas` 8% guard (MRVL: delta-ATM $300 vs spot
$278.67 = **7.65% < 8%**), so the stale deltas are trusted and the "ATM" body lands at the old
spot — a silent directional (bullish) skew on a supposedly neutral structure.

### The fix
- `findByDelta` now selects `deltaTarget ≈ 0.50` (ATM) legs by **moneyness** — the strike nearest
  spot. An ATM leg *is* the near-spot strike by definition, so moneyness is both correct and
  robust to stale Greeks. OTM-wing legs (0.16 / 0.20 / 0.05 delta, etc.) keep delta-based
  selection. This re-centers straddle / iron-fly / big-lizard bodies on spot.
- The **degenerate-spread guard is now expiration-aware**. With ATM bodies on the near-spot
  strike, a calendar's two 0.50-delta legs legitimately collide on the same strike (different
  expirations); the guard compared strike only and falsely marked them degenerate. It now
  requires same strike AND same expiration, so calendars/diagonals build while a real same-expiry
  collision (the mislabeled-condor case it was written for) is still caught.

### Impact / validation
- **Ticker selection is unchanged** — income/bullish composites are computed from
  `analysis-service` signals, not the lab structures, so this only re-centers the displayed/
  recommended structure per already-selected pick. Neutral on Brief Replay by construction → no
  FR-SEY-5 gate. Income/bullish are decision-support (not in the v2 inflection-only launch scope).
- Regression tests in `tests/strategy-lab-service.test.ts`: ATM put/call bodies pick the
  near-spot strike (not the stale delta-ATM), OTM wings still use delta, and the call-calendar
  builds with same-strike legs. Full suite: **542 passing**.
- The persisted 2026-06-17 brief keeps the skewed bodies; **re-run the brief** for centered ones.
