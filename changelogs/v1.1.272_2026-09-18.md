# v1.1.272 — 2026-09-18

`core` 0.6.27, `next` 0.4.15.

## TradeAnalyzer 2's chain view marks Greeks QC-410 found unusable

**What QC-410 reports.** 55 tickers on 2026-09-18 (51 the night before, with about half of them changing each night): E\*Trade's deltas imply a different underlying price than the chain's own quotes — AMG's Greeks imply 418.46 on a chain at 355.80 whose quotes imply 355.73; PCTY's 113.63 on 143.73. The findings are correct and will recur: this is a provider defect (measured v1.1.252), not ours.

**Who was exposed.** TradeAnalyzer has guarded itself since v1.1.255 (it withholds Greeks on such chains, so strategies fall back to moneyness or skip). The collector stores the provider's values verbatim, which is right for a raw table. The one place that showed them uncritically was **TradeAnalyzer 2's Chains view**, which rendered AMG's deltas exactly like AAPL's.

**Fix.** The grid now carries QC-410's verdict for the ticker and date: a notice with the check's own detail, and the delta, gamma, theta and vega cells struck through. Prices, IV and open interest are not marked.

The verdict comes from `greekConsistencyFor(db, date, ticker)`, new in `core`: QC-410's per-ticker assessment extracted so the check and the view run **the same code** and cannot disagree. It is recomputed from the stored chain rather than read from `qa_exception`, because an exception's `affects_trading_dates_json` is overwritten on every run and cannot say what held on a past date. About 130 ms per grid on the real generation.
