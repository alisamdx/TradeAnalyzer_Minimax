# v1.1.32 — 2026-06-16

## One canonical ATM-IV definition (brief accuracy audit, item 2)

### The finding
An audit of the 2026-06-16 brief flagged AMD reading **70.27%** ATM IV in the brief/critic
versus **81.83%** in `quote_cache.current_iv`. The investigation produced two conclusions:

1. **Most of the gap is a legitimate tenor difference, not an error.** The brief/lab measures
   ATM IV at the ~30-DTE expiration it actually trades (Jul-17); the provider's chain-view value
   measures the **nearest** expiration. On AMD's −7.3% day short-dated IV spikes hardest, so the
   nearest weekly read ~82% while the 30-DTE expiration read ~70%. The brief uses **only** the
   horizon-matched 30-DTE value and **never reads** `quote_cache.current_iv` (confirmed: the
   critic reads only VIX and the SPY 200-MA from `quote_cache`). The brief was correct.

2. **One real definitional inconsistency remained.** `etrade-data-provider.getOptionsIV`
   computed ATM IV from the **closest call only**, whereas the lab (`computeAtmIv`) and the
   `iv_history` capture use the **call+put average** at the closest strike — so even for the
   *same* expiration the two could differ by the put/call skew.

### The change
- New pure `atmIvAtClosestStrike(items, underlyingPx)` in `src/main/services/iv-utils.ts`:
  the average IV of every contract at the strike nearest spot (call + put + duplicates), units
  passed through unchanged.
- `strategy-lab-service.computeAtmIv` now delegates to it — **value unchanged** (the helper
  replicates its prior call+put average exactly; the brief's `currentAtmIv` is identical).
- `etrade-data-provider.getOptionsIVAndPremium` now uses it instead of closest-call-only
  (median-IV fallback retained), so the chain-view / dashboard / screener IV includes the put
  side and matches the lab's definition for a given expiration.

### Impact / validation
- **Display surfaces only** — options-chain view, Morning-Briefing dashboard, screener wheel
  metrics, `quote_cache.current_iv`. The brief, critic, IV-rank, and execution paths are
  untouched (the brief was already self-consistent on the 30-DTE value, and its number does not
  change). No FR-SEY-5 gate (not trade logic).
- Regression tests in `tests/iv-utils.test.ts`: call+put average (not calls-only), nearest-strike
  selection, unit pass-through, and null/empty/zero-IV guards. Full suite: **536 passing**.
