# v0.32.41 — 2026-06-08

## Fixed

### DailyBrief watchlist not appearing in watchlist dropdown

The `DailyBrief-MMDD` watchlist was being created correctly in the DB by the brief job but the renderer didn't know about it, so it never appeared in any dropdown until the app was restarted.

Root cause: the brief job runs in the main process and writes directly to SQLite. The renderer holds its own in-memory list of watchlists loaded at startup; nothing was signaling it to re-fetch.

Fix: proper main→renderer IPC push:
1. `DailyIntelligenceBriefJob` accepts an optional `onWatchlistUpdated` callback (6th constructor arg).
2. After `upsertBriefWatchlist()` succeeds, the callback fires.
3. In `index.ts`, the callback calls `getMainWindow()?.webContents.send('watchlists:updated')`.
4. Preload exposes `window.api.watchlists.onUpdated(cb)` — subscribes to `watchlists:updated` push events, returns unsubscribe fn.
5. `App.tsx` subscribes in a `useEffect` and calls `refreshLists()` on receipt.

Result: the new watchlist appears in every dropdown (Watchlists, Screener, Analysis, etc.) within milliseconds of the brief job finishing — no restart required.

**Files changed:**
- `src/preload/index.ts` — added `onUpdated` to `watchlists` namespace
- `src/main/services/jobs/daily-intelligence-brief-job.ts` — added `onWatchlistUpdated` constructor param; calls it after successful upsert
- `src/main/index.ts` — wires the callback via `webContents.send('watchlists:updated')`
- `src/renderer/src/App.tsx` — subscribes to `window.api.watchlists.onUpdated` → `refreshLists()`
