# v1.1.316 — 2026-09-23

## QC-904 counted from settlement as though FINRA published instantly

### The finding

> newest settlement date 2026-08-31, 23 days ago

This fired on runs 28, 30 and 33 and was escalated from its base severity
(warning) to blocking. The domain was exactly on schedule:

| date | event |
|---|---|
| 2026-08-31 | settlement |
| 2026-09-14 | collector's weekly sweep stores it |
| 2026-09-15 | next settlement |
| 2026-09-21 | weekly sweep, too early for the 09-15 data: nothing new |
| 2026-09-24 | FINRA publishes 09-15 (7th business day after settlement) |
| 2026-09-28 | next weekly sweep, the first that can capture it |

So the 08-31 settlement is **28 days old at the earliest moment a healthy
pipeline could have replaced it**. The drafted threshold of 21 days ("FINRA
publishes twice monthly, so three weeks is one missed publication") measured
from the settlement date. It ignored both the publication lag and the fetch
cadence, so it fired for several days in every cycle.

### The fix

The allowance is now the sum of its three parts, in code as named constants:

- **18 days**, the widest gap between consecutive FINRA settlements (mid-month
  and month end, each moved back to a business day);
- **12 days**, publication after the close on the 7th business day, with one holiday;
- **8 days**, the weekly fetch (`EVENTS_RULE.shortInterestCadenceDays`) plus one
  holiday-skipped Monday.

That totals **38**. A missed publication is still caught: the next settlement's data
is then two weeks late, and the stored date passes 38 days.

A clean reading now closes an open finding and says why. Past the old 21 days it
closes as `rule_changed` because the rule changed, not the data. Inside 21 it
closes as `data_corrected`.

### Test

`packages/core/test/qc904-short-interest.test.ts` pins:

- the 2026-09-22..28 window;
- a missed publication;
- the never-loaded case;
- **a year-long sweep** of settlement → publication → Monday fetch. The sweep asserts
  that the previous settlement's age on the day its successor arrives never
  exceeds the threshold. That age is the largest a healthy domain ever reads,
  and it is the number the old threshold never looked at.

### Not changed: ACN's Sunday earnings date

The same day report showed E*Trade dating ACN's next report **2026-09-27, a
Sunday** (QC-604, plus a blocking QC-602 against Finnhub's 2026-10-01). Accenture
announced its call for **Thursday 2026-10-01, 8:00 ET, release before it**, so
Finnhub is right and E*Trade is wrong by four days.

This is not the UTC artefact that the existing app shifts from Saturday to Friday.
An offset of a few hours cannot turn a trading day into a Sunday. A Sunday→Friday
shift would store 09-25, six days wrong instead of four. Both checks reported
the date correctly, so there is nothing to fix in them.

In TradeAnalyzer 1, `EarningsService.refreshTickers` asks E*Trade only when there
is no stored date or the stored date is more than 60 days out, so a stored
Finnhub 10-01 date is kept rather than being replaced by the Sunday.
