## Why a second run was needed

T8 (v1.1.186) measured the 2×2 on the **scanned universe** — 4,917 settled rows, 1,585 episodes —
and found it separates strongly. That is not the same claim as *"it separates on the picks the dice
actually produces"*.

The dice population is what survives critic-pass + non-degraded + a constructible CSP +
liquidity ≥ 8 + earnings clear + real credit, and v1.1.174 measured those gates doing essentially
all the selecting: on 27 of 34 pick-days exactly **one** candidate is eligible. A signal can be
real on the universe and vacuous here, simply because the upstream gates already removed the rows
it would have flagged. This replay asks the three questions the universe study cannot.

The gates are **replayed, not approximated** — `qualityEligible` mirrors
`scripts/dice-fit-demotion-study.mjs`, which mirrors `roll-the-dice.ts`.

## 1. Does it ever fire?

Yes — on half of everything.

| cell | dice picks | eligible candidates | brief picks |
|---|---|---|---|
| A both | 6 (18%) | 7 (17%) | 45 (18%) |
| B own-at-strike only | 5 (15%) | 7 (17%) | 39 (16%) |
| C support only | 6 (18%) | 6 (14%) | 35 (14%) |
| **D NEITHER** | **17 (50%)** | **22 (52%)** | **131 (52%)** |

This is the test the conviction band failed. That band fired on **0 of 39** eligible picks and was
therefore indistinguishable from a rule nothing violated. This one fires on half.

**And the dice's gates do not already handle it** — D holds the same ~52% share before and after
them, so critic-pass, liquidity, earnings and credit are selecting on entirely different axes.

## 2. When it fires, is it right?

On the 186 settled brief picks:

| cell | n | settled | mean | median | assigned |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A both | 45 | 38 | +2.17% | 2.51% | **8%** |
| B own-at-strike only | 39 | 24 | +2.97% | 2.71% | **4%** |
| C support only | 35 | 31 | **+3.46%** | **5.49%** | 26% |
| D NEITHER | 131 | 93 | **−2.10%** | 2.15% | **44%** |
| all | 250 | 186 | +0.35% | 2.80% | 28% |

Same shape as the universe study, which had D at −3.06% / 48.4% assigned.

The 34 dice picks give only 22 settled — too few to carry a result alone — but they point the same
way: D at −2.63% / 29% assigned against +2.00…+2.52% and 0…33% for A–C.

## 3. The result that decides what to build

**The new information is the SUPPORT axis. Own-at-strike is already in the email.**

Among the **166 brief picks whose basis sits above SMA200** — exactly the group today's sentence
warns about — the swing low splits them:

```
C  support only    31 settled    +3.46%  /  +5.49% median   26% assigned
D  neither         93 settled    -2.10%  /  +2.15% median   44% assigned
```

**C is the best of all four cells**, and the current single-sentence annotation lumps it in with
the worst. So the addition is a second sentence about support, not a rephrasing of the first.

## Caution recorded before anything ships to a surface

A sentence that fires **half the time becomes wallpaper.** That is the mirror-image failure of the
conviction band, and it is worth naming now rather than discovering later:

- D's **median is +2.15%**, not negative. The mean is dragged by tails.
- A settled AAPL pick in cell D returned **+1.48%**.
- The cell is worse **on average and on assignment rate** — it is not a prediction of loss.

Own-at-strike is deliberately phrased as a two-state read rather than an alarm. The support line
must be too.

## Limits

- **Dice-pick settled n is 22.** The effect size lives in T8's universe study; this replay
  establishes that the annotation *fires* and *adds*, not how much it is worth.
- **The earnings gate is not validly replayed** — `earnings_calendar` keeps one row per ticker with
  no history, so the replay sees today's next-report date. Applied identically to every cell, so it
  cannot bias cell-vs-cell, but the pick count is not exactly what the operator saw.
- **Nothing is gated and nothing shipped to a surface.** This is a measurement.
