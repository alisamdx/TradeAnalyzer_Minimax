# v1.1.88 — 2026-06-27

## Gap Risk metric on the Validate dashboard (display-only)

### What
The single-ticker Validate dashboard gains a **Gap Risk** indicator card next to IV / IV Rank.

```
gap_t          = (open_t − close_{t-1}) / close_{t-1}        // overnight gap, daily bars
adverseGapP95  = P95 of |gap_t| over downside gaps, trailing ~252 trading days
ivDailyMove    = atmIv / √252                                 // IV-implied 1-day move
gapRiskScore   = adverseGapP95 / ivDailyMove
```

- **> 1** → the name's realized downside-gap tail is *fatter* than IV prices (under-compensated by
  premium for jump risk — matters when owning the stock or selling CSPs).
- **< 1** → realized downside gaps sit within what IV implies.
- Badged **High** (≥1.25) / **Mid** / **Low** (≤0.85), with the figures `gap X% vs IV Y%/day`.
- P95 (not max) keeps it robust to one outlier and naturally mostly ex-earnings.

### Why display-only (not a trade gate)
This shipped out of the §3.3 "negative gamma / gap-risk" research thread in
`Requirements/NEW_FRONTIER.md`. Two Brief-Replay A/B studies (draft **FR-SEY-6**) tested the score
as a short-premium entry filter and it **failed FR-SEY-5**:

- `scripts/fr-gap-risk-study.mjs` (1,317 inflection bull-put picks, real `simulateSingleSpread`
  P&L): the filter **inverts** — high-gap-risk names are the *best* cohort (85.5% win / +11.9% vs
  74.6% / +5.4% baseline). A defined-risk spread's long wing already caps gap loss, so on
  up-breakouts a high score just reads as cheap-IV momentum.
- `scripts/fr-gap-risk-csp.mjs` (2,060 wheel+income picks, naked-CSP assignment-cost evaluator):
  directionally correct on win rate (high-gap tercile 79.7% vs low 87.0%) but the effect is tiny
  and provides **no left-tail improvement** (CVaR unchanged-to-worse) — i.e. not the tail hedge the
  thesis claimed. On the current ~13-month IV panel it clears neither the "helps" nor the
  robustness bar.

So the metric is surfaced for the trader to eyeball, never enforced. Re-evaluate as a gate once
`iv_history` deepens to 2–3 years.
