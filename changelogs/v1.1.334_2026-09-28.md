## QC-601: a Finnhub slip judged against the sweep that made it

QC-601 fires when a stock's nearest earnings date moves more than 14 days *later* between sweeps. That
is the signature of Finnhub's bulk calendar truncating at its cap, which drops the earliest dates, so
the next quarter reads as the next report. This is the NVDA incident. Three findings had been open and
**blocking** since 2026-09-22:

| ticker | moved | in run 30, the old date sat in… | listed there? |
|---|---|---|---|
| OWL | 10-12 → 10-29 | 09-22..10-21, uncapped (386 rows) | no |
| M | 11-11 → 12-03 | 11-06..11-13, uncapped (72 rows) | no |
| MKTX | 10-21 → 11-05 | 09-22..10-21, uncapped | no, and it was back to 10-21 from 09-23 |

Run 30 re-collected 09-22 after the dead run 28, and it covered **every day from 09-22 to 12-20 with
uncapped windows**. Nothing was cut off. Finnhub revised OWL and M (plausibly from estimate to announced
date), and flip-flopped on MKTX. The check could not tell any of that from truncation.

**The spec already said this.** 03's remediation column for QC-601 reads "stays open until the earlier
date is confirmed absent from a non-capped response". The code never tested for it.

**Change.**
- **Finnhub slips over 14 days are now checked for truncation.** If the old date's day sat inside an
  uncapped `/calendar/earnings` window of the new run, and that window didn't list the ticker, it is a
  provider revision. It evaluates not-fired (the magnitude is still recorded) and closes `rule_changed`,
  naming the move and the covering response.
- **Open findings are re-judged with the same test.** The re-judge's note now says which reason closed
  the finding: its old wording ("raised by the first rule, which read two windows of one sweep…") would
  have been a false permanent record for these three.
- **A truncation still fires.** An old date in a capped window, or in no window, fires as before.
- **E*Trade is unchanged.** It is asked per ticker and has no cap to truncate at.

**Rehearsed on 2026-09-25:** all three closed, each note naming its move, response and run. The open
blocking queue is **10**: QC-603 ×8, QC-401 ×1 and QC-804 ×1.

## Tests (`packages/core/test/events.test.ts`)

- An uncapped window covering the old date without the ticker: no fire, closes `rule_changed`, "not
  truncation".
- The same window **at the cap**: fires. That is the truncation case.
- A finding raised before the rule closes, and the record names the move and says it is not truncation.
