### What QC-009 was firing on

QC-009 fires when a natural key is stored more than once from one response. On 2026-10-20 it fired on eight Finnhub earnings keys. `raw_earnings_event` is keyed `(ticker, source, observed_at)`, one date per ticker per observation:

| Ticker | Copies in one response | Same date? | Same EPS estimate? |
|---|---|---|---|
| BBWI, HD, LOW, TGT | FY2026 Q3 and FY2027 Q3 | yes | yes |
| ROST, WSM | FY2026 Q3 and FY2027 Q3 | no (11-12 vs 11-18 / 11-17) | yes |
| DDS | FY2026 Q3 and FY2027 Q3 | no (11-05 vs 11-09) | no |
| FDXF (blocking since run 79) | FY2027 Q1, FY2026 Q4, FY2026 Q3 | no (10-28, 10-29, 10-29) | no |

Finnhub has labelled the retailers' quarter both ways since 10-20; their FY2027 rows go back to 09-14. Every reader of `raw_earnings_event` resolves several rows to the nearest date per run (QC-601/603's `MIN(report_date)`, the collector's E\*Trade gap fill), so none of the copies corrupt anything downstream. They do turn blocking after three runs, as FDXF did, and would need a ruling for each ticker every quarter.

**FDXF:** FedEx Freight announced on 2026-10-01 that it reports on Thursday 10-29 after the close. The report covers a four-month transition period after it changed its fiscal year-end. 10-28 is parent FedEx's (FDX) report date, attached to FDXF as its FY2027 Q1. The two 10-29 rows are the one transition report, labelled two ways. Readers take the nearest date, so FDXF reads one day early, which is the safe direction for anything that avoids earnings.

### Change

- `SAME_EVENT_COLUMNS` in `qa/checks/cross-cutting.ts` names, per table, the columns that make copies one event. For `raw_earnings_event` they are `report_date` and `eps_estimate`. The revenue estimate is left out because it differs by about 0.03 % between the two labels: HD 44,041,604,334 vs 44,055,282,734. `duplicateRows` counts a key as duplicated only when its copies in one response differ on those columns.
- `withRejudgedDuplicateFindings` re-counts each open per-key finding over its own runs, only for a table with such a rule. If today's rule no longer counts it, the finding closes `rule_changed`, saying the copies were one event under two labels. Other tables are untouched: their rule has not changed, so a recount could only agree.
- 03: QC-009's row describes the rule.
