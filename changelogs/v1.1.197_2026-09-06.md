# v1.1.197 — 2026-09-06

## feat — the collector's daily run and quality gate (NewApp B4)

The hosted collector now runs phases 0–4 (preflight, bars, end-of-day snapshot,
corporate actions), evaluates the quality gate, and sends the status email. The
watchdog writes `not_run` and sends the NO RUN email. Still change #4 of the
seven in `NewApp/requirements/06 §5` — no view, IPC handler, service, scoring
rule, existing job or existing table is touched.

### Changed here

- `src/main/services/jobs/collector-daily.ts` calls `runDaily()` instead of
  preflight alone, and the watchdog now writes `not_run` per domain and sends
  the no-run email.

**A red gate verdict is reported as a `success`**, not a failed job. The gate
finding problems is the gate working; a failed job means the collector could not
complete. Conflating the two would leave the batch history unable to answer
either question. A test pins it.

Everything else is in `@tradeanalyzer/core` — see `NewApp/CHANGELOG.md`.
