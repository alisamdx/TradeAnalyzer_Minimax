# v1.1.357 — 2026-10-13

## BPS-14: quote the study's replay tickers directly, and count coverage by ticker too

**Why.** The bull put spread enable gate (NewApp 08 §4) re-runs the study on real costs and needs ≥ 50% of entries priced from intraday quotes. The 10-09 and 10-13 gate runs both priced **660 / 1,495 (44%)**, although quoted tickers rose 169 → 182. The study's entries are a fixed replay set (179 wheel tickers, 2025-06…2026-08). Spread capture only quoted the day's pools, and none of the 13 newly quoted tickers was a replay ticker. 44 of the 179 were quoted, so waiting would not have moved the number.

The entries-only bar was also lumpy. FAST carries 59 entries and MSFT 41, so quoting those two would have passed it with 133 tickers still unpriced.

**What changed**
- **`coverage` capture** (`spread-chain-capture.ts`, migration 093 widens the `reason` CHECK). In the intraday slot only, spread capture fetches one put chain nearest 30 DTE (14–45) for up to 15 wheel replay tickers with fewer than 3 intraday quotes, most replay entries first. A ticker given a coverage chain is not asked again for 7 days. At most 25 expiration lookups per run, so tickers E*Trade cannot serve cannot starve the rest. Tickers already in that run's entry or track work are skipped. Coverage rows are never entries (`spread-mode`, the spread studies and the capture panel read `reason = 'entry'`) and never tracked. Cost: about 30 E*Trade calls per intraday brief run, until every replay ticker is quoted or has been tried.
- `intradayQuoteCounts` counts quotes exactly as the study's `loadQuotedSlip` does: intraday `brief_candidates` with bid > 0 and ask ≥ bid, plus captured intraday puts at |Δ| 0.10–0.40 with a two-sided quote. The comment in the code names both definitions, since drift between them would make capture ask for the wrong tickers.
- **The bar** (08 §4, decision S14): ≥ 50% of entries **and** ≥ 50% of replay tickers. `fr-bull-put-spread-study.mjs` prints the verdict on one line. On 10-13 it reads `FAILS — entries 44% (660/1495), replay tickers 25% (44/179)`.
- The brief's `spread-capture:run` log line reports `coverage tickers`.

**Expected:** ~135 uncovered tickers at 15 per intraday run is ~9 trading days to reach all of them. Re-run BPS-14 after about 5.

Not a trade-logic change: shadow picks, ranking and exits are untouched (FR-SEY-5 does not apply).
