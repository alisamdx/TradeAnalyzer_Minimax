# v0.22.18 — 2026-06-01

## Daily Options Snapshot batch job

Adds a nightly batch job that refreshes `optionLiquidityScore` (and
`annualizedReturn`) in `analysis_snapshots` from the live E*Trade chain,
so the Trade Scoring Engine always uses same-day liquidity data rather than
whatever was cached the last time a user ran Wheel analysis manually.

### Problem

`ScoringService` reads `optionLiquidityScore` from `analysis_snapshots`.
Without a nightly refresh, this value could be weeks old. A stock that was
liquid in the past could score well today even if OI has since dropped to
near-zero. The hard gate added in v0.22.15 catches this at the brief Stage 6
(post-scoring), but it means the stale signal still influences which 5 picks
reach the gate — a better pick could be displaced.

### Solution

`DailyOptionsSnapshotJob` runs `analyzeTicker(ticker, 'wheel')` on the top
300 screener candidates (sorted by pass-score) and saves results as a global
`analysis_snapshots` row with mode=`'wheel'`. The scoring engine picks it up
naturally on the next brief run.

### Schedule

| Trigger | Time |
|---------|------|
| Daily schedule | 06:00 ET (pre-market; OI reflects last night's close) |
| App startup | +5 min delay (300 s, lets E*Trade tokens initialise) |

### Rate limiting

1 100 ms gap between E*Trade chain calls (same as the brief job).
Capped at 300 tickers → ≤ 10 minutes typical runtime.

### Migration 024

`analysis_snapshots.watchlist_id` was `NOT NULL REFERENCES watchlists(id)`.
This constraint was incompatible with snapshots produced outside a user
watchlist context. Migration 024 relaxes it to nullable while preserving all
existing rows and the FK on non-null values.

### New method

`AnalysisService.saveGlobalSnapshot(mode, results)` — inserts a snapshot
with `watchlist_id = NULL`, used by this job.
