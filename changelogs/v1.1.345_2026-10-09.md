# v1.1.345 — 2026-10-09

## Fix: a collector run after midnight is recorded against the previous session

`data-collector` used the ET calendar date at start as its trading date. The scheduled 17:05 run is never affected. A manual chain started late in the evening can reach the collector after midnight, though, and that run would then be recorded against the new date, whose session has not happened yet, even though its bars and chains are the previous session's. On 2026-10-08 the manual chain's collector started at 23:52, 8 minutes before this would have happened. v1.1.344 noted the gap and left it open.

`collectorTradingDate(today, timeEt)` in `jobs/collector-daily.ts`:

- **Before 09:30 ET**, the run gets the latest session strictly before today, from `sessionOnOrBefore` in `@tradeanalyzer/core` (the rule-based calendar, which needs no database). Saturday 00:30 and Monday 08:00 both resolve to Friday, and the Friday after Thanksgiving at 01:00 resolves to Wednesday.
- **From 09:30 onward** the date stays today. A daytime run sees today's live market, and filing it under yesterday would put live quotes on a closed session. That is why this cutoff is the open, not the watchdog's 17:05.

Tests: `tests/etrade-token-expiry.test.ts`, the `daily collector trading date` block.
