TradeAnalyzer only. The falsification test for BPS-4's conclusion. **No application code changed, no
rule changed.**

## BPS-4 resolved: tenor is worth about nothing

The intersection cell — expiry type **and** tradability both held fixed — came back at **15 pairs,
median −0.9%**, the shorter expiry cheaper on 11 of 15. One point of cost share against a 25% gate is
noise-scale, and 11 of 15 is not significant alone (binomial p ≈ 0.06). Every larger number was a
confound:

| cut | n | median (21 − 30) | 21 cheaper | what it controls |
|---|--:|--:|--:|---|
| pooled | 296 | **+13.6%** | 27% | nothing |
| same expiry type | 60 | **−4.9%** | 75% | type only |
| both clear the gate | 78 | **+3.5%** | 29% | tradability only |
| **both at once** | **15** | **−0.9%** | **73%** | **both** |

The two single controls point opposite ways because conditioning on gate-clearance *selects for the
liquid leg*, which is the monthly — so that row was still an expiry-type comparison, measured after
the gate rather than before it.

**The decision is settled separately and does not depend on any of that.** As traded: 30 DTE gives
**156 of 296** candidates clearing the cost gate at a **12.6%** median share; 21 DTE gives **102** at
**18.4%** — a **−35%** tradable pool for twice the friction. Keep 30 DTE. But record why: the rule
lands on a monthly two times in three and inherits its depth (OI **460/565** against **37/42**, 12×).
The justification on file was per-trade edge, which was a time-stop artifact; the one that survives is
liquidity selection nobody credited the rule with.

## Added: the discordant cell, because a hypothesis needs somewhere it could fail

"It is expiry type, not tenor" has been checked only where it is comfortable. The 2×2 holds **32 pairs
where the 21-DTE bucket is the monthly and the 30-DTE bucket is a weekly** — the types swapped. If the
explanation is right, **the sign must reverse there**: the shorter expiry should become markedly
cheaper. If it does not, type is not what is doing the work and the conclusion is wrong.

The report now gives a paired difference and sign count for each non-empty type cell, and — for the
swapped one — how many clear the cost gate on each side. That last number is what a
prefer-the-monthly rule would be worth on cost, which opens **BPS-19**.

## Self-test — 25/25

A third fixture with the types swapped: 2026-06-19 is a third Friday and 2026-06-26 is not, so from
2026-05-29 they sit at 21 and 28 DTE with the monthly in the short bucket. Twelve pairs, over the

row must refuse at n = 0. Without it the new code path was only ever exercised at n = 0.
