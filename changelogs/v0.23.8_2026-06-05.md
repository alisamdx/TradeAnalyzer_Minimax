# v0.23.8 — 2026-06-05

## fix: Daily Brief shows stale EOD price instead of current market price

**Root cause:** `lastPrice` in the brief came from `analysis_snapshots` (last analysis
run) or `historical_prices.close` — both reflecting yesterday's EOD close. During
intraday runs the brief showed yesterday's price (e.g. NVDA $224 when market was $207).

**Two-part fix:**

1. `scoring-service.ts` — Added `loadLivePrice()` as the final step in
   `gatherSignalData()`. Reads `quote_cache.last` and overrides `lastPrice` for any
   ticker with a cached Polygon quote. This covers tickers already refreshed by a
   prior Market Sync or manual view.

2. `daily-intelligence-brief-job.ts` — Added a new Step 5b between AI synthesis and
   brief persistence. Fetches live `getQuote()` from Polygon for the ~25 final pick
   tickers (the only ones that appear in the brief output). Updates both `signalData`
   and writes the fresh price back to `quote_cache` so other views benefit immediately.
   All quote fetches are fire-and-forget (`Promise.allSettled`) — a failed quote
   doesn't block the brief.
