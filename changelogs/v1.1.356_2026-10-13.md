# v1.1.356 — 2026-10-13

## Gate-rehearsal snapshots: keep 2, not 5

`POST /api/v1/diagnostics/generation-snapshot` writes a full copy of the TradeAnalyzer 2 generation into `~/.tradeanalyzer/rehearsal` and pruned to the five newest. Each copy is the whole generation: 5.2 GB on 2026-10-13, growing about 240 MB a night. So the folder could hold 25+ GB, and that ceiling rose with every run. A rehearsal only reads the newest snapshot (`rehearse-gate.mjs --latest`), so `KEEP` in `services/generation-snapshot.ts` is now 2. The second copy is there for a before/after comparison.
