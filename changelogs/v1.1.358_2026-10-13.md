# v1.1.358 — 2026-10-13

## Stochastic RSI on brief picks — display only (Bec's entry timing)

**Why.** This is item 2 of the Tier-1 work in `docs/Bec/STRATEGY-SUMMARY.md` §10. Bec uses one entry-timing indicator, Stochastic RSI, and reads only its turn: never enter while it points down, enter once it has reached the bottom and turned back up. We computed RSI, SMAs, ADX and ATR but not this. Tier 1 is additive: it informs a decision without changing any. The point is to watch it on real picks before asking (with Brief Replay, under FR-SEY-5) whether it should influence anything.

**What changed**
- `services/stoch-rsi.ts` (new): %K and %D at 14/14/3/3, and a turn state:
  - `falling`: %K < %D, or %K = %D ≤ 20 (still pinned at the bottom).
  - `turned_up`: a strict cross of %K above %D within the last 3 bars, with %K ≤ 20 somewhere in the last 5.
  - `rising`: otherwise.
  - `null`: too little history, or a flat RSI window. A flat window gets null rather than an invented 0 or 50.
- `daily-technical-signals-job` stores them in `technical_signals.stoch_rsi_k / _d / _state` (migration 094, CHECK on the state).
- The brief copies them onto each pick as `BriefPick.stochRsi`.
- The Daily Brief grid gets a **Stoch RSI** column: %K with `↑ turned up` (green), `↗ rising` or `↘ falling` (red), and %K/%D in the tooltip. It sorts by turn, then %K. Picks from earlier briefs show `—`.

**Found while testing: equality at the floor decides the answer.** After a hard sell-off, %K and %D both sit at exactly 0. The first version of the rule:
- read a stock still pinned at 0/0 as having **crossed up**, because "not %K < %D" plus a past bar with %K < %D looked like a cross;
- **missed** the real turn out of 0/0, because "%K < %D on the prior bar" is false when both are 0.

Both are fixed with explicit equality rules: K = D at the bottom is `falling`, K = D at the top is `rising`, and a cross needs K ≤ D and then K > D. The first test fixture also hid the problem. A regular sawtooth decline keeps RSI cycling inside its own range, so %K sat near 53 and never reached the bottom. The tests now use a sell-off that really pins RSI at new lows.

**Display only, enforced.** No score, gate, ranking or critic context reads it. A source-scan test fails if `scoring-service`, `critic-service`, `roll-the-dice` or `brief-ranking` ever references it. The scan strips comments but keeps strings, since a SQL read of the column is exactly what it must catch, and has fixtures that check both that it fires and that it stays quiet.

`docs/Bec/STRATEGY-SUMMARY.md` §10 now marks rollability (v1.1.240) and this item as shipped. The next Tier-1 item is cumulative premium against cost basis on portfolio positions.
