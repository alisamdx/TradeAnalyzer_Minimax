## QC-008's EDGAR submissions "cap" was our window, not EDGAR's

**What fired.** QC-008 (blocking) on EDGAR `/submissions` responses for iShares Trust (behind IVV, IWD, IWF, IWM, IWO, IYR and, tonight, LQD), Jefferies and JPMorgan. The rule: 1,000+ entries in `filings.recent` with the oldest on or after the sweep's `since` means the list was cut short of the window.

**What was true.** EDGAR's `recent` block is not a 1,000-row cap. It holds **at least one year of filings, or 1,000, whichever is more** — JPM's held **26,192** on 2026-09-15, iShares Trust's 2,474, Jefferies' 1,065, and in all three the oldest entry was exactly one year before the fetch. The sweep's `since` was the fact pull's 400-day lookback, so for any filer with 1,000+ filings a year, the 35 days past EDGAR's year lay in overflow files, and the response read as truncated every time.

Nothing needed those 35 days. The sweep stores 10-K/10-Q/20-F/40-F filings so the fact pull knows when a filer has reported; a report older than a year is never the newest one. The fact pull reads companyfacts whole and keeps its own 400-day window.

## Fixes

- **`FILINGS_LOOKBACK_DAYS = 360`** — inside the year `recent` always holds. All three archived responses read complete at 360 days (and capped at 400); a genuinely short `recent` still fires.
- **Funds are out of the slice.** They file no 10-K/10-Q, so their submissions could only ever store nothing (every iShares response parsed 0 rows).
- **One fetch per CIK.** iShares Trust stands behind seven universe tickers and was fetched six times in one run.
