### QC-211 member stopped trading (`core`, bars, warning)

- **What it tests.** For every current member: sessions after its last bar, up to the trading date, over a 60-day lookback. It counts only from the start of the member's current membership range, so a ticker admitted yesterday owes nothing earlier. It fires above 2 sessions (working threshold, leaving room for a one-day halt). On 10-09 QRVO reads 5, PSKY 4 and WBD 4.
- **Outages.** Skipped when fewer than half the members have a bar today, because that is an outage and QC-201's finding.
- **The finding.** It names the action: exclude the ticker in TradeAnalyzer (`constituents_excluded`), then run `universe_seed`.
- **Closing.** It closes `data_corrected` once the membership closes. Otherwise a ticker that leaves the universe would stop being evaluated and stay open for ever. It closes `provider_fixed` if bars resume.
- Rehearsed on the 10-09 snapshot: 3 of 1,083 fired, exactly those three.

### Migration 092 (TradeAnalyzer)

Adds QRVO, WBD and PSKY to `constituents_excluded` and deletes them from `constituents`, the same mechanism as migration 085. Treating PSKY's history as SKYD's would splice two securities.
