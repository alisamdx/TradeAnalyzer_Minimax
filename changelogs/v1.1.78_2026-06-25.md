# v1.1.78 — 2026-06-25

## Pre-Trade Validator: intent-gate the IV-regime "cheap vol" check

First threshold tuning, driven by running the validator against the live wheel book.

The IV-regime check warns when you sell net premium into low IV rank ("selling cheap
volatility"). That's the right call for an **income** trade (premium is the edge) but noise for
an **ownership / wheel** trade where you're happy to acquire the shares and premium is
secondary. So it's now **intent-gated**, exactly like the direction and earnings checks:

- **ownership** purpose, or **happy-to-own = yes** → status **info** ("premium isn't your edge
  here, so it's context, not a red flag — just know you're not richly paid for the downside").
- **income / directional** → stays **warn** (premium *is* the edge there).

The "buying expensive volatility" branch (debit + high IV) is unchanged — it stays a warning,
since it applies to directional/hedge debit structures, not ownership.

### Why
Validating the current book surfaced this: NVDA (IVR 24) and TSLA (IVR 28) are both selling
cheap vol, but the user's stance on both is "happy to own, premium secondary." Treating that as
a warning pushed healthy wheel entries to caution on the IV check alone. With the gate:
- **NOW 80 CSP** — IVR 79 (rich), cushion ok → **good** (unchanged).
- **TSLA 340 CSP** — IVR 28 but happy-to-own → IV check is info, cushion ok → **good** (was
  caution).
- **NVDA 185 CSP** — IVR 24 → IV check info, but cushion 5.5% < 5.7% (1.5×ATR) → **caution**
  (cushion only). Correctly still flagged for the real issue (thin cushion on a jumpy name).

### Change
`src/main/services/pretrade-checks.ts` — `iv_regime` cheap-vol branch: `status = premiumSecondary
? 'info' : 'warn'` where `premiumSecondary = intent.purpose === 'ownership' || intent.happyToOwn
=== true`. Test `tests/pretrade-checks.test.ts` pins owner/happy-to-own → info and income → warn
(22 tests). Decision-support only — not FR-SEY-5 (not wired to the engine). Thresholds remain
first-pass/tunable.
