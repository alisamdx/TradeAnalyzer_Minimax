# v1.1.182 — 2026-09-03

## Logged: does the critic's treatment of trend survive contact with the data?

No behaviour change. An open item, raised by a live pick.

### How it surfaced

The Roll the Dice panel showed both readings of the same fact, disagreeing in the open:

> **Critic:** "IV rank at 82 with a **confirmed uptrend** and strong fundamentals **supports** the
> short put thesis"
>
> **Own-at-strike (v1.1.169):** "46.6% **ABOVE** its 200-day average… an **extended price, not a
> discount**"

Both describe DINO having run **+132%** off its 52-week low. Before v1.1.169 only the first would
have been visible.

### The assumption lives in two places, and they are different problems

**1. A rule check, stating its rationale in code.** `critic-service.ts` `checkTrendAlignment`
(~line 404) fires only on a *confirmed downtrend* (`notDowntrend.normalized < 35`) and explains
itself as *"wheel works best on stocks trending sideways to up."* **T6 measured the opposite
ordering:**

| `notDowntrend` band | assigned | mean return |
|---|---|---|
| bearish (10) | **20.5%** | −0.26% |
| sideways (75) | 24.6% | −0.10% |
| bullish (100) | **31.9%** | **−1.01%** |

**2. The LLM, unprompted.** The critic's system prompt says **nothing about trend for wheel at
all**. So "confirmed uptrend supports the short put thesis" is the model's own inference — an
intuition from directional strategies, where it is correct, imported into short premium, where the
evidence disputes it. Nothing in the prompt asks for it or corrects it.

### Corroboration

`USE_ADX_IN_SCORING` was set false in v1.1.33 on a **full-year** Brief Replay A/B, recorded reason:
*strong-trend names mean-revert over the next ~21 days*. Two independent findings now point the
same way.

### Why this is logged and not fixed

- **Assignment rate is not tail severity.** A confirmed downtrend assigning *less often* does not
  mean a falling knife is safer when it does assign. The warning may be defensible even if its
  stated reason is wrong — those are separate claims.
- **T6 tested trend as a selection signal, not as a warning.** Different question.
- One regime, and `notDowntrend` is 8 of 91 weight.

Tweaking a prompt is cheap, which is exactly why it deserves the same discipline that stopped
`notDowntrend` itself being acted on.

### How it becomes answerable

`brief_candidates.signals_json` (v1.1.175) stores `notDowntrend` raw/normalized for every
candidate, and `daily_briefs.picks` stores the critic verdict. After a quarter, **verdict × trend
band × settled outcome** is a direct three-way test — including the sharp version: does a `pass`
on a bullish name underperform a `pass` on a sideways one?

That is the second question tonight's capture unlocks that had no path to an answer this morning.
