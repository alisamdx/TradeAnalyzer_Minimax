# v1.1.36 — 2026-06-17

## Brief Payoff/Options buttons load the recommended structure (user report)

### The finding (reported)
Expanding the wheel AMD pick showed `CSP A+ · Best fit: Big Lizard A+` with a setup line
`$470 · Jul 17 · $2305.00 cr`. Two problems:

1. **Ambiguous card**: the `$470 / $2,305` describes the **CSP** (the recommended wheel trade),
   but it visually trailed the "Best fit: Big Lizard A+" tag, so it read as if the strike/credit
   belonged to the Big Lizard.
2. **Payoff loaded the wrong structure**: clicking **Payoff** opened a **Big Lizard**
   (~$6,232 net credit), not the CSP ($2,305).

### Root cause
`PickNavRow` (the Payoff / Options / legs source) used `result.scores[0]` — the single
highest-`totalScore` structure across all 31 lab strategies — instead of the
**strategy-relevant** structure the card recommends. For AMD that's `big-lizard` (A+ 95), so the
wheel pick's Payoff loaded a Big Lizard. The card body, by contrast, correctly used
`pickDisplayStructure(scores, strategy, …)` (→ the CSP), so the two disagreed. This affected
**wheel, income, buy, and inflection** (the v2 launch strategy — its Payoff loaded a Big Lizard
instead of the bull-put credit spread); bullish was usually correct because its relevant
structure often *is* `scores[0]`.

### The fix
- `PickNavRow` now resolves the structure via the same
  `pickDisplayStructure(result.scores, strategy, pick.breakoutDirection)` selector the card uses,
  and derives `setup` / `expiry` / `legs` / the Payoff `strategy` slug from it. So Payoff,
  Options, and the legs preloaded into the visualizer all match the recommended trade.
- The card's setup line is prefixed with the relevant structure name
  (`CSP: $470 · Jul 17 · $2305.00 cr`), so its terms can't be misread as the "Best fit" tag's.

### Impact / validation
- **Renderer-only — no brief re-run needed**; relaunch the app and the persisted brief renders
  correctly. The relevant-structure selector is covered by `tests/strategy-structures.test.ts`;
  this routes the nav row through it (no renderer component-test infra exists, same precedent as
  the v1.1.30 inflection-direction wiring). Full suite: **542 passing**.
