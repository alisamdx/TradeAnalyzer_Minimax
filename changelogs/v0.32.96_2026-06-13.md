# v0.32.96 — 2026-06-13

## Strategy Lab — PMCC & calendar second-expiration construction

### Why

Buy/bullish strategies systematically grade C/F in high-IV regimes because the lab's
buy/bullish-relevant structures were all single-leg debits — and the four structures
that *could* carry a defined-risk, B-grade-capable setup (Poor Man's Covered Call/Put,
Call/Put Calendar) were stubbed with `unavailableReason: 'Requires two expirations'`.
This was the deferred item in `.ai/AI_CONTEXT.md` §11.

### What

- **Second (far) chain fetch.** `validate()` now fetches one additional chain at a
  far-dated expiration (`selectFarExpiration`, targets ~120 DTE, ≥ near+25 DTE, ≤ 220
  DTE). `explore()` fetches it only for the strategies that need it. Graceful: any
  failure / no suitable expiration leaves those strategies `unavailableReason: 'No
  suitable far expiration available'` rather than synthesizing from one expiry.
- **Two-expiration leg building.** `buildSetup()` draws `farExpiration` legs from the
  far chain and all others from the near chain; each leg records its own expiration.
- **Calendar/diagonal P&L via Black-Scholes.** Max profit has no closed form here (the
  long leg keeps time value when the short expires). It's computed by valuing the long
  leg's residual at the short leg's expiry with `bsPrice`, assuming the underlying pins
  the short strike (the profit peak): `maxProfit = longResidual×100 − netDebit`,
  `maxLoss = −netDebit`. See `docs/formulas.md#calendar-diagonal-pnl`.
- **Shared Black-Scholes util.** Extracted `bsPrice`/`normCdf`/`RISK_FREE_RATE` to
  `src/main/services/black-scholes.ts`; `collared-leaps-service.ts` now imports it
  (was a duplicated local copy).

### Impact

PMCC/calendars now produce a real reward/risk → a real grade, so buy/bullish pick up a
defined-risk B-capable structure in high-IV regimes. Costs one extra rate-limited
E*Trade chain call per ticker in Strategy Lab (incl. the brief's Stage-4 validation,
~15–50 tickers/night).

### Tests

- `tests/strategy-lab-service.test.ts` (+3): call calendar builds across two expirations
  with positive max profit; PMCC diagonal (long far ITM / short near OTM); calendar/PMCC
  stay unavailable when no far expiration exists. Chain prices are generated with
  `bsPrice` so entry cost and residual valuation are arbitrage-consistent. Suite: 272 passing.
