# v1.1.321 — 2026-09-23

## A share class inherits its sibling's earnings date, in both apps

On 2026-09-23 QC-603 had 13 open findings, all "no future report date has ever
been observed". Two of them were share classes whose sibling *was* dated: Finnhub
had **GOOGL 10-27** and **NWSA 11-04** and never GOOG or NWS. E*Trade, asked about
each four times, returned no date for either. In TradeAnalyzer 1 an absent date
reads as "no earnings", so a GOOG put expiring after 10-27 passed every earnings
check: the brief's −20 penalty, the critic and the v2 entry gate. TradeAnalyzer 1
had recommended contracts on 9 of the 13 tickers in the ten days before.

### TradeAnalyzer 1

`EarningsService.syncShareClassSiblings` runs at the end of the earnings refresh
job and copies a class's future date onto its undated sibling, as source
`share_class`. It is symmetric, and the pairs are in `SHARE_CLASS_SIBLINGS`:

- GOOG/GOOGL
- NWS/NWSA
- BRK.B/BRK.A
- FOX/FOXA
- UA/UAA
- LEN/LEN.B
- HEI/HEI.A
- BF.A/BF.B
- Z/ZG

It lives at the writer because ten places query `earnings_calendar` directly;
patching each reader would miss the next one. Its rules:

- it never overwrites a sibling's own future provider date or a manual entry;
- it never copies a copy;
- it replaces a stale row, and follows the sibling when its date moves;
- the job notes say `Share class: N copied`, and the Earnings Calendar view labels
  the source "Share class".

### TradeAnalyzer 2: QC-603

A future date stored under a share class the operator ruled `same_issuer` in
`ref_provider_symbol_ruling` now satisfies the check. These are the QC-909 rulings
that already existed, read in either direction. The finding names the sibling
(`via`) and closes as `rule_changed`. A `different_issuer` ruling never supplies a
date: LLYVA and LLYVK are answered with FWONA's data and keep firing.

Rehearsed on 2026-09-23:

- QC-603 went from 13 of 559 fired to 11 of 559.
- GOOG closed via GOOGL and NWS via NWSA.
- Blocking went from 127 to 125.
- **BRK.B still fires, correctly: BRK.A is undated too.**

### Found, not changed: `analyzeWheel`'s earnings check has never fired

`AnalysisService.analyzeWheel` reads earnings from
`dataProvider.getEarningsCalendar`. The Polygon implementation of that is a stub
that always returns `null`, and has since 2026-05-02. So every wheel result gets
the +2 "no earnings within 45 days" suitability point, and `daysToEarnings` is
always null in wheel snapshots. The brief, critic and v2 entry gate read
`earnings_calendar` directly and are unaffected. Wiring the check up changes wheel
scores, so under FR-SEY-5 it is recorded in `.ai/AI_CONTEXT.md` §11 for a
decision.

### The other 11

BMNR, BRK.B, GLXY, IOT, JAN, JHX, LLYVA, LLYVK, MDLN, ROIV and SUNB are not
covered by either source. They are handled by manual entry
(`EarningsService.setManual`, never overwritten), checked weekly as companies
announce.
