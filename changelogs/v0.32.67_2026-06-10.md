# v0.32.67 — 2026-06-10

Outcome of a deep audit of the 2026-06-10 daily brief (all 25 picks, critic verdicts, rule-check code, structure grades, and verdict-vs-outcome history).

## Fixed

**`annualizedReturn` 100× unit bug** ([analysis-service.ts](../src/main/services/analysis-service.ts))
`premium / (strike × 100) × (365/dte) × 100` — premium is per-share but was divided by per-contract collateral. The ×100s cancel, producing a decimal fraction instead of a percent (MKC: 0.11 instead of 11.2%). Downstream, with `normMax: 60`, wheel `premiumYield` (wt 19) and income `premiumRiskRatio` (wt 22) normalized to ≈0 for **every ticker** — both strategies ranked without their premium signal, degenerating to IVR + fundamentals. Since the highest-IVR names are often those with earnings pumping the IV, the bug systematically floated event-risk names to rank 1 (observed: MKC, IVR 95, earnings June 24, wheel rank 1). Formula fixed to `premium / strike × (365/dte) × 100`; `docs/formulas.md#annualized-options-return` and spec §23.2 corrected (both had the bug enshrined). Snapshot values self-correct on next analysis refresh.

**`GRADE_RANK` missing `'A+'`** ([critic-service.ts](../src/main/services/critic-service.ts))
`GRADE_RANK['A+'] ?? 0` ranked A+ below F. Latent: a pick whose only available structures were A+ and F picked F as "best" and incorrectly fired `poor_structure_grade`.

**Critic liquidity flag reads the chosen structure**
`checkLiquidity` used max liquidity across relevant structures; the LLM context used the chosen (first relevant available) structure. HAYW 2026-06-10: flag said 5/15, context said 3/15 — the critic LLM explicitly called out the contradiction. New shared `selectRelevantStructure()` helper used by every rule check and the context builder.

## Changed

**Earnings penalty: income → income + wheel + bearish** ([daily-intelligence-brief-job.ts](../src/main/services/jobs/daily-intelligence-brief-job.ts))
Wheel sells naked puts, bearish reverse-lizards carry naked short legs — same earnings-gap risk as income strangles. Exported `EARNINGS_PENALTY_STRATEGIES`. 3 of 5 wheel picks on 2026-06-10 had confirmed earnings inside the expiry window; the pipeline promoted them and its own critic then flagged them.

**Critic calibration**
- Prompt: new FLAG SEVERITIES section — info flags are advisory; a pick whose only flags are info can still merit `pass`. "Address the most severe flag" now applies to warning-severity only.
- `low_liquidity` threshold <6 → <4. Liquidity 4–6 is normal for single-name large caps; at <6, half the universe could never reach `pass` (today: 14 caution / 6 flag / 3 pass).

**Buy strategy is stock-first**
Buy's relevant structures (zebra/vertical/calendar) are all debit and grade C/F systematically in high-IV regimes (all 5 buy picks today). New `CriticService.shouldPresentAsStock()`: when no relevant structure grades ≥ B, the pick is a stock purchase — `poor_structure_grade` suppressed, LLM context gets a `presentation` note, and `DailyBriefView` shows `📈 Buy Shares` with the structure demoted to "Optional".

## Added

**Critic rule: bearish SPY mirror** — `checkMarketRisk` flagged bullish/buy when SPY < 200MA but had no mirror; bearish picks on a bullish tape now get an info flag.
