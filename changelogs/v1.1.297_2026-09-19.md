## The bull put spread's own captures are now checked

The 08 §4 enable gate is decided on `spread_chain_capture`. That table is written by TradeAnalyzer itself, from its own E*Trade calls — **TradeAnalyzer 2's quality gate judges the collector's generation and says nothing about it**. So a silent capture failure would not fail anything; it would quietly decide the gate by shrinking or distorting the sample, and a quote not captured on the day cannot be recovered.

`spread-capture-quality.ts` adds four checks over the captures behind the positions:

| Code | Fires when |
|---|---|
| SC-1 | No capture for ≥ 4 days while positions are open, or none ever |
| SC-2 | A live position has no row on a date the capture ran for other series |
| SC-3 | More than 5% of captures were missing one of the two legs |
| SC-4 | An open position's quotes are identical across the last 3 captures (stale or re-served chain) |

SC-2 compares each position against **the days the capture actually ran**, not a calendar, so it needs no trading-day table and cannot fire on a weekend or holiday — the absence-reads-as-pass trap the collector's own checks have hit repeatedly.

The report gains `captureQuality` (capture days, last date, marks, gaps and rate, expected vs missing marks, findings). The 🪜 Spread Shadow view shows it above the sector cap, "✓ nothing to look at" when clean, and `scripts/spread-shadow-report.mjs` prints the same.

## What this does not fix

Both apps still fetch the same E*Trade chains — TradeAnalyzer for this capture, the collector for its chain subset — and only the collector's copy is under a real gate. The proper fix is to move this capture into the collector once TradeAnalyzer 2 leaves its pilot, which ends the duplication too. Recorded in 08 and TODO #44.
