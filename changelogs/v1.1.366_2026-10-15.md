# v1.1.366 — 2026-10-15

## Format-drift guard (`constituents-service.ts`)

- `renamedByPunctuation(added, removed)` (exported) pairs an added and a removed ticker that are equal once `.`, `-`, `/` and spaces are dropped.
- With gates on, `applyRefresh` throws on any such pair, before the turnover gate: "respells BRK.B as BRKB — a change in how the source writes tickers, not index turnover". `refreshIndexAuto` then falls back to Wikipedia (S&P 500 only), or keeps the stored list and the job reports `warning`.
- Why: on 2026-10-15 the six respelled tickers were 12 of 1,019 Russell 1000 changes, far under the 25% turnover gate. Nothing else could see it.

## Tickerless holdings

- `fetchVanguardHoldings` collects entities with an empty ticker as `name (weight%)`. `ConstituentsRefreshOutcome.untickered` carries them, and the job note says "N holding(s) without a ticker skipped: …".
- Vanguard's 2026-09-30 VONE file lists Dillard's Inc. Class A with `ticker: ""`, 552 shares, $369,834, 0.00% (CUSIP 254067101). That looks like a leftover position, not a member, so no ticker is guessed back in.
