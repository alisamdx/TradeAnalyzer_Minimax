# v1.1.319 — 2026-09-23

## `STALE_GREEK_MODEL_DELTA`: a model-delta fallback for stale chains, shipped OFF

Since v1.1.255 `ETradeDataProvider.getOptionsChain` nulls the Greeks of any chain
whose deltas imply a price more than 1 % from both the chain price and the
put-call-parity price (`STALE_GREEK_GUARD`, on). That stops a strike being chosen
by a wrong delta, and it has two costs:

- the wheel paths **skip** the ticker;
- Strategy Lab falls back to a moneyness heuristic that maps a delta target to a
  fixed percentage out of the money, whatever the volatility.

This release adds the alternative, **switched off**. With
`STALE_GREEK_MODEL_DELTA=1`, a stale chain gets our Black-Scholes delta, computed
at the chain's own `underlyingPrice` with E*Trade's IV, instead of null. Gamma,
theta and vega stay withheld. A clean chain is never touched either way.

**Evidence** (`scripts/wheel-delta-selection-study.mjs`, TradeAnalyzer 2 raw chains,
2026-09-14..23):

- About 200 of 228 flagged ticker-days would get a correctly targeted wheel put
  back, roughly 25 a day.
- E*Trade's IV holds up on those tickers: a mid-solved IV picks the same contract
  on 93 % of them.

**Why it is off:** it adds candidates on tickers whose feed is currently
unreliable, which is exactly what FR-SEY-5 exists for, and none of them can be
scored before expiry. It is recorded in the FR-SEY-5 register as shipped-disabled
pending realized outcomes.

Tests (`tests/greek-consistency.test.ts`):

- the flag is off by default, and a stale chain is still withheld;
- when on, delta equals Black-Scholes at the chain price, and the shortlist then
  chooses a lower (really 0.35–0.45 delta) strike than the stale Greeks would;
- a clean chain is untouched;
- with no date to compute DTE from, the fallback withholds rather than guesses.
