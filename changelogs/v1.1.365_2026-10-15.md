# v1.1.365 — 2026-10-15

## Why

- **companyfacts drops filings.** SEC's companyfacts is derived from each filing's XBRL instance. It dropped most 10-Qs filed 2026-07-28/29, 35 filings still absent 70+ days later. Retrying companyfacts never fixes that.
- **The fallback was gated badly.** v1.1.360's instance fallback ran only after companyfacts had been read at least 14 days after a filing and still lacked it. Its first live run (run 85, 2026-10-15 18:51 ET) sent 20 index requests, and all 20 got HTTP 429 from SEC's 10/s limit (paced since v1.1.364). `filingFactsQueue` counted every captured response as an attempt, so each of those filings waited until 10-22. CTSH's 10-Q (0001058290-26-000031) was one of them; it kept QC-701 blocking and the day not clean.
- **TA1 was silently stale.** `sec-edgar-provider.ts` read companyfacts only, with no way to notice a missing filing. For those filers, TTM revenue/income/cash flow, YoY growth and the latest balance sheet were a quarter old without any error.

## Collector (core 0.6.77)

- **`isSecAnswer` / `filingAttempts`** (`collector/facts-queue.ts`). A 2xx or 404 is an answer and starts the `FACTS_RETRY_DAYS` wait. A 429, 5xx or null status is not, and the filing is due again next run. `filingAttempts` also keeps the last failure's status for QC-701/704.
- **Filing first.** `filingFactsQueue` lists every report filing with no facts from any source, with no companyfacts gate, newest first, `FILING_FACTS_PER_RUN` 20 → 100. `runEventsPhase` runs `runEdgarFilingFacts` **before** `runEdgarFacts`. Its capture rule is now `{ trigger: 'new_report_filing', primary: true }`.
- **companyfacts' role.** `factsQueueEntries` treats a filing as pending when it has no facts **from companyfacts** (joined on `meta_response.endpoint = COMPANYFACTS_ENDPOINT`). companyfacts is still fetched after each report filing, for back history and the cross-check, under the same 20-a-run / 3-retry-reserve rules.
- **`parseFilingInstance`** (`ingest/edgar.ts`) is the database-free parse behind `makeFilingFactsIngest`. It is exported from the package root with `filingInstanceName`.

## Checks

- **QC-701** causes: `no_newer_filing_seen`, `awaiting_instance_read` (with its place in the queue), `instance_read_failed` (status and date), `instance_without_facts`, `filing_has_no_xbrl`. `sec_companyfacts_missing` and `awaiting_fetch` are gone.
- **QC-704** is now "report filing without facts from any source": warning, per accession, fires 3+ days after filing (`FILING_FACTS_DUE_DAYS`), cause in evidence, `escalatesOnRepeat: false`. It closes `data_corrected` and names the source that delivered the facts.
- **QC-705** (new) checks that the two sources agree. For each accession with facts from both, it compares (taxonomy, concept, unit, period_start, period_end) keys and values at a relative tolerance of 1e-12. It fires on any value difference or any key only one side has. Warning, structural threshold 0. The first test showed why 1e-9 was wrong: it hid a $1 difference on $11.7B.

## TA1 fundamentals

- `getEdgarFundamentals` calls `addOmittedFilings` after reading companyfacts. When the newest 10-Q/10-K period end is at least `FILING_CHECK_AFTER_DAYS` (100) days old, it fetches `submissions/CIK.json`. For up to 2 report filings newer than companyfacts' newest `filed`, it reads `index.json`, then the instance, and merges the us-gaap facts (`mergeFilingFacts`) as companyfacts-shaped entries. It never throws, and every call is logged through `edgarFetch`.
- Why 100 days: a current filer's newest period end is at most about 136 days old (10-Q due 40–45 days after quarter end), or about 181 at a 10-K. 100 catches every late case and costs a small submissions call on the rest.
