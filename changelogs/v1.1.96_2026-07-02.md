# v1.1.96 — 2026-07-02

## Roll / Closure Analyzer

### Why
Payoff reviews a structure *before* you enter it. There was no equivalent for the *exit*
decision on a position you already hold — when to close, hold, or roll a short option, and
if rolling, which expiry/strike is most efficient. The user (a discretionary wheel/CSP
trader) asked for a screen to make that call.

### What
New **Analysis → 🔄 Roll/Close** screen (`RollAnalyzerView.tsx`), scoped to a single short
option — cash-secured put or covered call (the wheel book).

- **Input**: manual (ticker, put/call, strike, expiry, contracts, entry credit) or **load an
  open CSP/CC from the E*Trade-synced portfolio** (`portfolio.etrade.listPositions`).
- **Current state**: close-now P&L and % of the max credit banked, moneyness + DTE,
  assignment probability (|Δ|), breakeven, capital at risk, credit at open.
- **Recommendation**: transparent rule-based hold / close / roll (no LLM) — close when most
  of the credit is banked; roll when ITM/high-assignment and a credit roll exists; hold when
  comfortably OTM with no compelling roll.
- **Roll candidates**: scanned across the next ~6 expiries and a strike band (0.80–1.15 ×
  current strike, i.e. roll down / same / up), **credit-rolls only** (net credit > 0). Each
  shows net credit, annualized ROC, capital efficiency, assignment Δ, the new breakeven (roll
  credit folded into the cumulative basis), and a liquidity flag.
- **Four selectable ranking metrics** (the user asked for all four on the screen): annualized
  return on capital, largest net credit, risk-adjusted yield (delta-penalized), capital
  efficiency. Switching re-ranks client-side instantly — no chain re-fetch.

### Design notes
- The metric math is pure and unit-tested (`buildRollCandidates`, `scoreByMetric`,
  `recommendAction`); the service only orchestrates the rate-limited chain fetches.
- Marks are bid/ask midpoints — surfaced with a "confirm real fills" caveat and a per-leg
  liquidity flag; consistent with the app's other mid-priced estimates.
