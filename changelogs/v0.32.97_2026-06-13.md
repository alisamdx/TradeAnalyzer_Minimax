# v0.32.97 — 2026-06-13

## Baking liquidity into the numbers: fill modeling + volume

Liquidity *screening* was well-layered (pre-rank signal, structure score, hard gate,
critic flag), but it gated *which picks survived* without affecting *the dollar figures
shown* — all P&L used mid prices, so credits/returns were optimistic exactly where
liquidity is worst. And the income liquidity signal advertised "OI, volume, tight
spreads" while the code only used OI + spread. This release closes both gaps.

### #1 — Realistic fill modeling (no more mid)

- **`options-fill.ts`** — `fillPrice(bid, ask, action, slip = SLIPPAGE_FRACTION)`:
  sells fill below mid (toward bid), buys above mid (toward ask), by `slip` (0.5) ×
  half-spread. `slip=0` → mid (old behavior), `slip=1` → natural price. Locked/crossed/
  degenerate quotes fall back to mid. Never returns negative.
- **Strategy Lab** (`buildSetup`): net credit/debit — and therefore PMCC/calendar net
  debit and reward/risk — are computed from `fillPrice`, not raw mid. Credits drop
  slightly; the haircut is largest on wide-spread legs.
- **Wheel & income** (`analysis-service.ts`): replaced `premium = mid / 2` (an
  indefensible flat 50% haircut) with the realistic short fill. Premiums rise toward
  accurate. **This shifts wheel/income scoring rankings** (premiumYield / premiumRiskRatio
  / annualizedReturn signals) **and the premium captured in `options_premium_history`** —
  intended: the old mid/2 was a placeholder, the new value reflects a real fill.

Decision (user): unify everything on the realistic fill model rather than leave the
inconsistent mid/2.

### #2 — Volume in the liquidity score

- **Strategy Lab `liquidityScore` (0–15)** rebalanced: spread **8**, open interest **4**,
  same-day volume **3** (per leg, averaged). A high-OI/zero-volume strike no longer scores
  as fully liquid; an actively traded low-OI strike is credited.
- **Wheel `optionLiquidityScore` (0–10)**: OI **4**, spread **4**, volume **2**.
- `SetupLeg` gains a `volume: number | null` field (populated from the chain contract).

The brief's hard gate threshold (5/15) is unchanged; the rebalanced components keep
roughly the same pass/fail behavior while letting volume rescue or penalize at the margin.
