# v0.28.1 — 2026-06-06

## Retrospect — Trade recommendations, AI learning capture

### Trade recommendations (In Progress tab)

Every open position now shows a real-time **Action** badge replacing the old "Signal" column:

| Badge | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| ⚑ Close (red) | Close signal triggered — move too large or pin risk |
| ✓ Take Profit (green) | Position is profitable, time to collect |
| ↻ Roll (blue) | Approaching expiry, worth rolling for more premium |
| ◉ Watch (orange) | Negative but within threshold — monitor closely |
| — Hold (gray) | No action needed |

Each badge shows a one-line reason. Expanding the row reveals the specific suggested action.

Rules are strategy-aware: income/wheel strategies get 50% profit rule and roll signals; bearish
strategies flip the direction; pin risk (≤3 DTE) triggers unconditional close.

### AI learning capture (History tab)

When a trade closes or auto-expires, Claude Haiku analyzes the trade and captures:
- **Entry fit**: Good entry / Too early / Too late / Wrong setup
- **Strategy worked**: yes/no
- **Key lesson**: one sentence on the main takeaway
- **Suggested tweak**: one sentence on what to change next time

Learning is captured automatically (fire-and-forget, ~2-3s). In the History tab, rows with
learning show a ▶ expand arrow — click to reveal the full insight.
