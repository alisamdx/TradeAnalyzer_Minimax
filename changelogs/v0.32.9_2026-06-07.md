# v0.32.9 — 2026-06-07

## Sidebar: LEAPS and Collared LEAPS merged into Strategy Lab

LEAPS and Collared LEAPS were separate sidebar entries under the Strategy section. They are now tab buttons (🔬 Strategy Lab | ⚡ LEAPS | 🛡️ Collared LEAPS) at the top of the Strategy Lab screen, reducing the sidebar from 26 to 24 entries.

**Implementation**: a thin `StrategyLabShellView` wrapper holds the tab state and conditionally renders the three existing view components unchanged. The shell forwards `initialTicker` to `StrategyLabView` so cross-view navigation from Daily Brief still pre-fills the ticker.
