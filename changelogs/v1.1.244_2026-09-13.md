## What

**Bull put spread mode, in shadow.** After every brief run: pick up to 5 bull put credit spreads from the
wheel pool, built from the chains `captureSpreadChains` stored for the same run, and store the picks and
every rejection in `spread_mode_picks`. No panel, no email, no engine input.

## Operator decisions implemented

| | |
|---|---|
| Structure | bull put credit spread (premium, not direction) |
| Profile | 21–35 DTE nearest 30; short 0.25–0.35Δ nearest 0.30; long 5–7.5% of spot below the short |
| Costs | `options-fill.ts` fills; round-trip cost ≤ 25% of mid credit |
| Exits (guidance) | take profit at 50%; no loss stop; no time stop |
| Size | fixed max loss per position ($1,000 default), 10 positions |
| Pool | wheel only (income unmeasured) |
| Switch | ships `shadow`; `off` available; `on` built when the §4 enable gate passes |

## Why shadow

The evidence (TODO #43) is Black-Scholes priced on one replay. §4 needs a study re-run on captured
quotes and ≥ 30 settled shadow picks with a positive mean after fills. Shadow storage is what produces
those picks.

## Mechanics

- `src/main/services/spread-mode.ts` — `computeSpreadMode`, `buildBullPutSpread`, `pickSpreadExpiration`,
  `readSpreadModeSettings`. `src/shared/spread-mode.ts` — profile, settings keys, defaults, types.
- Reads `spread_chain_capture` rows for today's date, this slot, `reason = 'entry'`; no provider calls.
- Rejections, in order: critic flag · earnings unknown · earnings stale · no chain this run · no 21–35 DTE
  expiry · earnings on/before every in-window expiry · no underlying price · no short · no long · net credit
  ≤ $0.05 · round-trip cost > 25% · one contract over budget · ranked below top 5.
- Ranking: net credit ÷ max loss, ties by ticker — unvalidated and labelled so.
- Storage: a slot re-run deletes and rewrites that slot; the other slot is kept; 730-day retention.
- Brief job: runs right after spread chain capture; logs `spread-mode:run` (INFO counts / ERROR message).

## Revision to the design

08 §3.9 described `off | shadow | on`. This build implements **`off` and `shadow`**; the `on` surfaces
(Daily Brief panel + email) are built when §4 passes, since until then they would have nothing to show.
A stored `on` reads as the default (`shadow`).
