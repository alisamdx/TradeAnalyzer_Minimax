# v1.1.292 — 2026-09-19

TradeAnalyzer only. `core` and `next` are unchanged. Shadow mode only: nothing is emailed, shown or traded.

From the first shadow week's rejections (v1.1.291 report, 80 rejections over 10 runs).

## Leg rules match real strike grids, and the study

25 of the 80 rejections were strike spacing, not missing or bad data. `diagnoseLegs` found only 2 chains with no deltas, so the stale-Greeks guard was not the cause.

- **Short put: 0.25–0.35Δ → 0.20–0.40Δ, nearest 0.30.** The 0.10-wide band fell between two strikes on liquid names (DVN ×4, EOG ×3, COP ×2, MDT ×2, EXPE, OLLI, OXY), all of which were picked on other days. That made 12 "no put in the Δ band" rejections.
- **Long put: 5–7.5% of spot → the strike nearest short − 5% of spot, within 3–10%.** On a ~$120 stock with $5 strikes, a 125 short has 120 (4.1%) and 115 (8.3%), and neither is inside 5–7.5% (CF ×5, MRVL ×3, OXY ×3, BG, DOCN). That made 13 "no strike in the wing range" rejections. The #43 study took the strike *nearest* short − 5% of spot, so the mode was stricter than the evidence it rests on. 3–10% bounds that rule; the study's wing grid tested 5, 7.5 and 10%.

## ETFs skip the earnings gate

EWZ was rejected 5 times as "earnings date unknown". An ETF has no earnings, so the gate could never pass it. ETFs (`constituents.index_name = 'etf'`) now skip the earnings check, and their pick stores `earnings_date = NULL`.

## NTAP / INTU "earnings calendar stale": investigated, no change

Both reported in late August and have no announced next date. The API logs show E*Trade's quote-with-earnings lookup made for NTAP in every brief run (09-14 to 09-17) and for INTU on 09-14/15, and Finnhub's per-ticker lookup runs nightly ("82 not yet announced" on 09-18). Neither source has a date, and the mode fails closed as 08 §3.4 intends.
