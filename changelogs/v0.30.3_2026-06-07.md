# v0.30.3 — 2026-06-07

## Payoff Visualizer — P&L Risk Matrix

New "⬛ Risk Matrix" toggle in Payoff View alongside the existing chart.

### What it shows
A 2D grid: underlying price (rows) × time (columns) → P&L at each intersection.

**Rows** — actual chain strike prices within ±25% of spot. Falls back to
synthetic strikes at $1/$2.50/$5/$10 intervals (scaled by spot magnitude)
when chain data isn't loaded.

**Columns** — Today + chain expiry dates filtered by:
- All expirations with DTE ≤ 45 (shows everything in the near term)
- If fewer than 10 within 45 days, extends to the first 10 expiries
  (handles monthly-only chains that might otherwise show only 1–2 columns)

**Cells** — P&L computed differently per column:
- Today column: Black-Scholes with full time remaining per leg
- Intermediate expiry columns: BS with T = (leg_expiry − col_date)
- After a leg's own expiry: intrinsic value only (the leg has expired)
- Multi-leg strategies with different expiries handled correctly — each
  leg transitions to intrinsic at its own expiry date

**Zone column** — at-expiry P&L classification per row:
★ Max Profit · ✓ Profit · — Breakeven · ✗ Loss · ✕ Max Loss

### Math
- Black-Scholes: Abramowitz & Stegun normal CDF approximation
- Risk-free rate: 4.5% constant
- IV: from `leg.iv` (set when legs loaded from chain); falls back to 30%
  with a footer warning when IV is missing

### Visual
- Color-coded intensity: deep green (max profit) → neutral → deep red (max loss)
- Spot price row highlighted with blue outline and ◀ marker
- Last expiry column separated by heavier border
- Sticky header row for scrolling wide tables
