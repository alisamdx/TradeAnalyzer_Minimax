## A recurring finding kept its escalated severity (gate bug)

A finding that fires on 3 consecutive runs is promoted one level, for example warning to blocking. When
a resolved finding fires again, the gate reopens it and resets its counter to 1. **It kept the escalated
severity**, so the escalation outlived the resolution that should have ended it.

Measured on 2026-09-25: **10 QC-410 findings reopened as blocking with `consecutive_runs = 1`**. They
were AIZ, AYI, CRL, DLB, JAZZ, LLYVA, MTB, PCTY, UHAL and UTHR, each still citing "fired on 3
consecutive runs". AIZ and PCTY had escalated, closed themselves on 09-24 when their Greeks came right,
and fired again on 09-25.

**Fix:** a recurrence starts from that run's own severity, which is the check's base or whatever its
value or fraction rule promotes it to. It then re-earns the escalation by firing 3 runs in a row. This
affects every check.

## QC-410 no longer escalates by repetition

QC-410 flags tickers whose E*Trade Greeks imply a different price than the chain's own quotes: 60–80
tickers a night, about a third of them different from one night to the next.

- **It is a provider defect nothing here can repair, and it is already handled where it matters.**
  TradeAnalyzer's `STALE_GREEK_GUARD` withholds the deltas on a flagged chain, which is why the spread
  mode sometimes reports "chain carried no deltas". TradeAnalyzer 2's chain view shows the same verdict
  (`greekConsistencyFor`).
- **Escalating each ticker on repeat made about 25 of them permanently blocking.** That held the chains
  domain `unusable` and the pilot's clean-day streak at zero. It was the gate-wide RED v1.1.252 had
  removed, back again one ticker at a time.

**New check option `escalatesOnRepeat: false`** (03 §5.4). A check with it takes each run's own severity
and never escalates on repetition. Findings already escalated return to their base severity, one event
each, whether or not they fire that run. The not-firing case matters for REXR, which was blocking with
no strike near the money to evaluate since 09-24. Value and fraction promotion still apply. QC-410 is
the only check with it.
