# v1.1.15 — 2026-06-14

## v2 execution — FR-SEY-1: sector-concentration cap

The third Seykota extension. Diversification by *correlation*, not just count: cap how many
concurrent open positions share a sector bucket, so a basket of correlated names (five
semiconductor spreads) registers as one concentrated bet rather than five independent ones.

See `Requirements/v2-execution/06-seykota-risk-extensions.md` (FR-SEY-1).

### Behaviour

- Bucket = **SPDR sector ETF** (XLK, XLF, …), derived from `fundamentals_cache.payload_json.sector`
  via `sectorStringToEtf` — coarse enough that "semiconductors & related devices" and
  "electronic computers" both map to XLK (the whole point of a *correlation* cap).
- A candidate is rejected (`per_sector`) when the count of open positions in its bucket
  reaches `maxPositionsPerSector`. Unmapped names (no sector, or no fundamentals_cache row)
  are left **uncapped** by design.
- **Default OFF** (`maxPositionsPerSector = null`) → no change to behaviour.

### Changes

- Migration **050**: `exec_config.max_positions_per_sector` (NULL = off).
- `src/main/services/sector-etf.ts` (new): the `SECTOR_ETF` map + `sectorStringToEtf` mapper,
  extracted from `scoring-service.ts` (was module-private) so the scorer and the execution
  governor share one definition. `scoring-service.ts` now imports it.
- `src/main/execution/risk-gate.ts`: `GateCandidate.sector?` + `GateContext.openPositions[].sector?`;
  new pure `per_sector` check (skipped when the cap is null or the candidate is unmapped).
- `src/main/execution/risk-governor.ts`: `sectorBucket(ticker)` resolves the bucket from
  `fundamentals_cache`; `checkEntry` enriches the candidate + open positions with sectors,
  but only when the cap is enabled (avoids needless reads).
- UI: Execution Settings → Risk limits exposes the cap (blank = off).
- Types: `ExecConfig.maxPositionsPerSector`.
