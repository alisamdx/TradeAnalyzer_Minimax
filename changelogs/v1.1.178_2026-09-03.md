# v1.1.178 — 2026-09-03

## T6 — the price-derived signals add nothing, and `notDowntrend` is anti-predictive

No behaviour change. `scripts/fr-wheel-price-signals-study.mjs` added.

### Where this sits

v1.1.173 found the wheel composite anti-predictive at the top of its picks and blamed premium
richness. v1.1.176 **refuted** that — ranking the universe on `ivRank`/`premiumYield` beats no
selection. That left the other 50 of 91 weight, of which 29 are testable:

| signal | weight | testable? |
|---|---|---|
| `fundamentalScore` | 17 | no — `fundamentals_cache` has no history |
| `technicalNeutral` | 11 | **yes** — RSI(14), target range 40–60 |
| `recentVolatility` | 10 | **yes** — 20d annualized realized vol, lower better |
| `notDowntrend` | 8 | **yes** — SMA stack: bullish 100 / sideways 75 / bearish 10 |
| `leverageQuality` | 4 | no — `fundamentals_cache` has no history |

Each is replicated exactly from `scoring-service.ts` (`extractSignalRaw` + `normalizeSignal` +
`computeTrendDirectionScore(mode='wheel')`), computed point-in-time from bars on or before each
snapshot date, and applied as a selection rule to the whole wheel universe — the same method that
cleared premium richness. **4,816 settled, liquidity-gated rows across 40 dates.**

### As a group they add nothing

| rule | Δ mean | Δ median |
|---|---|---|
| top technicalNeutral | +0.12 | +1.50 |
| top recentVolatility | +0.15 | −0.09 |
| **top notDowntrend** | **−1.31** | +0.81 |
| all three combined | +0.14 | −0.18 |
| **INVERTED — worst of all three** | **+0.43** | **+0.95** |

Weighting all three exactly as the composite does moves the needle by +0.14 mean / −0.18 median
against no selection at all. **Inverting them is the only rule that comes out better on both.**

### The banded view is where the signal actually is

| `notDowntrend` band | n | mean | median | **assigned** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| bottom 25% — bearish (10) | 435 | −0.26% | +1.13% | **20.5%** |
| mid 50% — sideways (75) | 907 | −0.10% | +1.19% | 24.6% |
| **top 25% — bullish (100)** | 491 | **−1.01%** | +0.93% | **31.9%** |

**Selecting for "not in a downtrend" produced more assignment and worse returns.** The assignment
gradient — 20.5% → 24.6% → **31.9%** — is monotone and is the robust part of this, since assignment
counts are not tail-sensitive the way mean returns are.

`technicalNeutral` is flat across its bands (means −0.27 / −0.58 / −0.10, assignment ~25% at every
level) — no discernible signal in either direction. `recentVolatility` is weak and mildly the wrong
way round: its *high-vol* end returned a better median (+1.92%) than its low-vol end (+0.72%),
which is the opposite of what a `lower_better` weighting assumes.

### This is not a new phenomenon — it is the ADX finding again

`USE_ADX_IN_SCORING` is already `false`, removed in v1.1.33 on a **full-year Brief Replay A/B**,
with the recorded reason: *strong-trend (high-ADX) names mean-revert over the next ~21 days*.
`notDowntrend` measures nearly the same thing through the SMA stack, and reproduces the same
result on different data. A trend-following term inside a short-premium composite has now been
found anti-predictive twice, independently.

### Not acted on

`notDowntrend` is 8 of 91 weight, the effect is small, and this is one regime and an observational
study rather than an A/B. A faithful weight sweep is **not currently possible** — the wheel
composite needs `fundamentals_cache`, which has no history, and income (the usual proxy) carries
no `notDowntrend` term at all.

**What changes that: the capture built in v1.1.175/177.** `brief_candidates.signals_json` stores
each candidate's per-signal `{raw, normalized, weight, missing}`, so after a quarter the composite
can be recomputed with any one term removed and compared on real outcomes — the exact A/B that
cannot be run today. This is the first concrete payoff of that instrumentation.

### Caveats

- Tests these signals **as ranking inputs on the universe**, not the composite. 21 of its 91 weight
  can never be replayed.
- The CSP is the analysis service's (delta band 0.20–0.30), not the Strategy Lab's.
- Earnings gate not applied — no point-in-time earnings data exists for this period.
- Held to expiry at intrinsic; no roll, early close or post-assignment covered call.
- One regime. Medians reported alongside means because short-put returns are tail-dominated.
