# v1.1.2 — 2026-06-14

## v2 execution subsystem — Phase 2 (risk governor)

The safety core: the fail-closed risk layer no order may bypass (02-risk-governor).
Split into a pure decision layer (exhaustively unit-tested) and a DB-backed stateful
service. Still dormant — wired to nothing yet.

### `risk-gate.ts` — pure logic (no I/O)

- **`sizePosition(maxLossPerContract, limits)`** (§2.4): `contracts = floor(perTradeRisk /
  maxLoss)`, also capped by remaining deployable capital — the tighter wins. Rejects (0
  contracts) with `exceeds_per_trade_risk` / `insufficient_capital` / `invalid_max_loss`.
- **`evaluateGate(ctx, candidate)`** (§2.5): the ordered, fail-closed 10-check pre-trade
  gate — engine_running · broker_connected · defined_risk_eligible · per_trade_risk ·
  max_concurrent · per_ticker · per_strategy · capital_ceiling · buying_power · daily_loss.
  All must pass; the first failure supplies the reject reason. Returns `ExecGateResult`.

### `risk-governor.ts` — `RiskGovernor` (DB-backed)

The single source of risk truth. Owns `exec_config` + `exec_risk_state`:

- **config** read/update (array round-trip for `eligibleStrategies`).
- **engine state machine** (§2.7): `start` (rejects while kill-tripped), `stop` (hard stop,
  always allowed), `pause` (only from running), `tripKillSwitch`, `clearKillSwitch` (manual
  re-arm only) — validated transitions.
- **day-P&L** (§2.6): `updateDayPnl(realized, unrealized, today)` resets at a new session
  anchor and **auto-trips the kill switch** when the day loss (realized + unrealized)
  reaches the configured % of the ceiling; halt-new-entries only.
- **capital**: `recomputeDeployed()` = Σ max_loss of open/closing/needs_attention positions.
- **`sizeEntry()` + `checkEntry(candidate, broker)`**: assembles a gate context from the DB
  + broker (buying power, connection) and runs the pure gate, auditing the decision.
- **idempotency + forensics**: `newClientOrderId()` (the COID written before any send) and
  the append-only `exec_audit_log`. Every state change commits atomically with its audit
  entry.
