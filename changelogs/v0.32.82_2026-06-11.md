# v0.32.82 — 2026-06-11

## Fix: earnings blind spot — missing calendar data read as "no earnings risk"

### The gap

Found during the post-batch deep-dive: 119 of 1,068 universe tickers (11%) have no
future-dated row in `earnings_calendar` (the Finnhub bulk pre-fill doesn't cover
everything — DELL was a final pick tonight with no row at all). The critic's
`checkEarningsInWindow` returned null for both "earnings confirmed to be after
expiry" and "we have no idea when earnings is" — for an options position those are
very different risk statements.

### The fix — three layers

1. **Per-pick E*Trade fallback (brief job)** — before the critic stage, the job calls
   `earningsService.refreshTickers(finalPickTickers, creds)` for the ~25 final picks.
   `refreshTickers` already smart-skips tickers with valid future dates and never
   overwrites manual entries, so this typically issues only a few E*Trade quote calls.
   Outcome recorded in `runMeta.earningsFallback` ({updated, skipped, failed}).
   `EarningsService` + `getEtradeCreds` are now constructed earlier in `index.ts`
   (before the brief job) and passed in as optional constructor params.

2. **Critic rule check** — when a pick has an options structure but the ticker has no
   future earnings row, an info-severity `earnings_unknown` flag is emitted: "No
   earnings date on file … earnings risk inside that window is unverified."
   Info severity means it won't alone force a 'caution' verdict (per the existing
   severity calibration), but it appears in the brief.

3. **Critic AI context** — new `earningsKnown` boolean next to `earningsInWindow`,
   with prompt guidance: missing calendar data = unverified risk, not absence of risk.
