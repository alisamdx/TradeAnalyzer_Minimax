## NewApp B10a — pilot-exit machinery (`core` 0.6.0, `next` 0.4.0, dictionary v13)

The existing app is bumped because the collector's quality gate runs inside it: the new checks and
the streak fix only take effect once this app bundles the new `core`. Full write-up in
`NewApp/CHANGELOG.md`; spec in `NewApp/requirements/03 §3.9, §8.1`, `05 §6`, `06 §9`, `02 §1`.

### Streak counted across unrecorded days (bug)

`cleanDayStreak` iterated `DISTINCT trading_date` from `qa_domain_day_status`. A trading day with no
rows — the host app not running at 17:05 or at 19:30, so neither the run nor the watchdog wrote
anything — was absent from the walk and the streak counted across it. It now walks `ref_trading_day`
(with the rule generator as fallback) and a trading day with no rows breaks the streak. Regression
tests: `packages/core/test/pilot-exit.test.ts` ("breaks on a trading day nothing recorded", "runs across
a weekend and a holiday").
