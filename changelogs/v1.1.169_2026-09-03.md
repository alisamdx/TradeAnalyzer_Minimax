# v1.1.169 — 2026-09-03

## feat — own-at-strike is answered from the stock's own history, not from a curated list

### The finding that reframed the task

v1.1.168 demoted the conviction band from a gate to an annotation, and that shipped with a
recorded cost: *"nothing now enforces own-at-strike."* Measuring the band to build a replacement
showed **that cost was overstated — the band was never enforcing anything.**

It is anchored to spot on the day it was written. Across all 13 stored rows, `price_high / spot`
at write time has median **1.126**. An OTM cash-secured put strikes at median **0.930** of spot.
So `strike / price_high ≈ 0.83` by construction, and the test cannot fire until spot has risen
~21% above where the band was last written. Over 62 stored briefs, **0 of 39** picks on a listed
ticker breached the line; the closest was still 10% under it.

The band also drifts badly once written — SPY's and VOO's never-above lines now sit at the
**195th percentile** of their own 52-week range, i.e. above every price those funds have traded
at. It was decorative before v1.1.168, not a protection that demotion removed.

### The replacement

`src/main/services/own-at-strike.ts` reports where the **effective cost basis** (`strike −
premium` — what assignment actually costs) sits against two point-in-time anchors from the
ticker's own bars: its 200-day average and its 52-week range.

```
basisVsSma200Pct = (costBasis / sma200 − 1) × 100
atOrBelowSma200  = costBasis <= sma200
rangePctile      = (costBasis − lo52) / (hi52 − lo52) × 100
```

Available for **100% of stored wheel picks**, needs no curation, cannot go stale. Missing history
(< 200 sessions) reads as "not enough history", never as a pass. `rangePctile` is deliberately
unclamped — a basis outside the 52-week range is real information.

It leads the ownership sentence in both the email and the panel; the conviction band drops to
secondary context so it cannot read as an all-clear it did not earn.

### What it is worth (`scripts/own-at-strike-study.mjs`)

Outcome = the underlying's return **from the cost basis** at expiry + 30 days, **excess of SPY**
over the identical window, computed for every pick rather than only the ~27% actually assigned.

| | accept (`strike ≤ SMA200`) | reject | separation |
|---|---|---|---|
| raw return | +14.20% | +0.61% | 13.6 pts |
| SPY, same windows | +3.95% | +3.89% | — regime is not the explanation |
| excess of SPY | +10.24% | −3.29% | 13.5 pts |
| **per independent episode** | **+12.10%** (n=12) | **−5.32%** (n=35) | **17.4 pts** |

Episodes collapse repeated picks of the same ticker+expiry into one observation — NVDA appeared 7
times and AVGO 6 across 8 consecutive days, which is one move counted 13 times. The separation
**grew** under de-duplication, survived leave-one-out (14.3 pts with the +46% INTU outlier gone)
and survived dropping NVDA and AVGO entirely (19.1 pts on n=8). 11 of 12 accept episodes positive.

### Why it annotates and does not gate

The accept arm is **12 episodes across three expiries in one ~4-week window** — only picks whose
expiry + 30d has already passed are measurable, which truncates everything after early July 2026.
This project has rejected several levers that looked exactly this good on a single regime: the
SPY-regime gate, the overbought guard, the expiry-selection lever. Under FR-SEY-5 that is not
enough to change which trades are taken, so it informs and the operator decides. Re-run the study
once ≥3 months of briefs have matured past expiry+30d.

**Fundamentals were deliberately excluded** even though `fundamentals_cache` carries P/E, EPS,
value/growth scores and grades: it holds one row per ticker with no history, so a replay scores
against *today's* values — the same trap that makes the earnings gate unreplayable. Only price
anchors are honestly testable, so only price anchors were used.
