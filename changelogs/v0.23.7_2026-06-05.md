# v0.23.7 — 2026-06-05

## revert: Daily IV Capture — back to E*Trade (rolled back MarketData.app)

Reverts the v0.22.23 switch from E*Trade to MarketData.app for the Daily IV Update
batch job. MarketData.app hit rate limits in practice and two other jobs (Options
Snapshot, Intelligence Brief) already depend on E*Trade — maintaining a second provider
purely for IV capture adds complexity without benefit.

Changes:
- `daily-iv-capture.ts` restored to original E*Trade implementation (uses
  `ETradeDataProvider.getOptionsExpirations` + `getOptionsChain` + `captureFromEtradeChain`)
- `index.ts` wiring reverted: `optionsProvider as ETradeDataProvider` (was `marketdataProvider`)
