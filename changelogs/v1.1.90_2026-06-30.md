# v1.1.90 — 2026-06-30

## Added — Daily Brief: Level-2-tradeable (debit) vertical chip (display-only)

### What
Both brief tabs (Grouped card + Ranked Grid) now show a second alternative chip on
**bullish** and **bearish** picks:

```
📈 L2-tradeable · Long Call Spread · $540/$560 · Jul 31 · $7.50 db · risk $750 · BE +1.5%
```

- **Bullish → Long Call Spread**; **Bearish → Long Put Spread** (a *debit* vertical).
- Narrowed to a small max-risk budget (**$750** default): long leg ATM, short leg
  pulled in to the **widest** strike whose net debit still fits the budget (max profit
  under the risk cap).
- Shows debit, max risk, and **breakeven + move-to-profit** — **not** a PoP.
- Click → Payoff Visualizer pre-loaded with the two legs.

### Why
The brief's recommended/credit structures (bull-put spread, big lizard, iron condor)
all require IBKR **options Level 3**. A Level-2 account can't place them. This chip
surfaces the structurally-equivalent *directional debit* spread a Level-2 account
**can** trade — so the user can place small, defined-risk trades (e.g. to build an
options track record toward an L3 upgrade) instead of nothing. Origin: account-building
discussion, 2026-06-30.

### How
- New pure builder `buildNarrowDebitVertical` in
  `src/main/services/level2-debit-vertical.ts`: filters same-side strikes with a usable
  price (chain mid, or Black-Scholes from the contract IV when unquoted), takes the ATM
  long, and widens the short while `debit × 100 ≤ targetRisk`. Falls back to the
  narrowest positive-debit spread flagged `exceededBudget` when nothing fits.
- Computed inside `StrategyLabService.validate()` from the **same chain it already
  fetched** (no extra E*Trade call, no new brief-job stage) and attached to
  `StrategyLabValidateResult.level2DebitAlts: { call, put }`, which the brief already
  persists per pick.
- Accessor `level2DebitAlternative(pick, strategy)` in `src/shared/brief-ranking.ts`
  (bullish → `.call`, bearish → `.put`; null otherwise).
- Renderer: `Level2DebitAltLine` in `DailyBriefView.tsx` (full + compact), blue
  `.level2-alt` styling to distinguish from the green credit-spread chip.

### Why breakeven, not PoP
The lab's `popEstimate = 1 − |short-leg delta|` is correct for *credit* spreads (you
defend the short strike) but **wrong for debit spreads** — for a long call spread it
returns the chance the underlying stays *below* the short cap, not the chance you
finish above breakeven. On AMD it reads ~74% while the real profit odds are ~35–40%.
The chip shows the **breakeven and the % move required** instead.

### Scope / safety
Display-only / account-building context. At high IV a debit vertical is buying
expensive premium and is **not** the optimal structure — the chip is framed as
"L2-tradeable", never as a recommendation. It does **not** change which structure the
brief recommends, the Conviction score, or what the engine trades → **no FR-SEY-5
gate**.
