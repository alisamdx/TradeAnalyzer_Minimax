# v1.1.224 — 2026-09-11

## One Finnhub limiter, shared by both consumers

v1.1.223 gave the collector the Finnhub key it had never had. That closed the failure and opened the
next one: **nothing paced those calls.** Finnhub's free tier allows ~60 calls/minute **per key**, the
collector's weekly recommendations phase makes one call per chain-subset ticker, and the subset fills
to a few hundred once gen-001 has 20 bars per ticker (early October).

The limiter is **one instance shared by both consumers**, not one each:

- `EarningsRefreshJob` — the bulk sweep (up to `MAX_BULK_CALLS` 160) and the per-ticker fallback.
- The NewApp collector — `runEarningsSweep` and `runRecommendations`, through `httpGet`.

Two buckets would be two budgets spending one allowance, and the overrun would only appear when both
ran at once — which is exactly what the evening chain does, since the earnings job runs ahead of the
collector in the same after-close sequence. This is the reasoning that put the collector inside this
app for Polygon (NewApp 01 §63, 04 §1.1), applied to the second shared key.

- `TokenBucketRateLimiter({ requestsPerMinute: 55, burstSize: 5 })`, constructed once in `index.ts`
  and passed to both. 55 leaves margin; the small burst stops a sweep firing a minute's allowance in a
  second, which a full-size bucket would allow.
- `httpGet` acquires **before the latency clock starts**, as `polygonGet` does — a queue wait folded
  into `responseLatencyMs` makes the provider look slow exactly when the limiter is working hardest.
- `sweepBulkWindow`'s fixed 1,100 ms sleep now runs only when no limiter was supplied (the optional
  constructor argument keeps existing tests constructing the job with three arguments). Pacing twice
  would only make the sweep slower.
- EDGAR, Treasury and FOMC/BLS calls are unchanged — none of them is rate-limited per key here.

### Tests

`tests/collector-host.test.ts`: `httpGet` acquires from `opts.finnhubLimiter`; `index.ts` constructs
exactly **one** 55/min limiter and passes it to both the collector host and `EarningsRefreshJob`. The
count assertion is the point — a second `new TokenBucketRateLimiter({ requestsPerMinute: 55` would be
the bug this is here to prevent.
