## Bull put spread: max loss per position set to the operator's $2,500

Spec 08 §6 Q1, answered 2026-09-19: a **$25,000** account, **10% per position** → `SPREAD_MODE_DEFAULTS.maxLossPerPosition` = **$2,500**, replacing the study's $1,000 placeholder. There is no Settings field for it; a stored `bullPutSpreadMaxLossPerPosition` still overrides the default.

Effects on the shadow picks, from the next brief:

- **Sizing:** contracts = floor($2,500 ÷ one contract's max loss). For example, an $820-per-contract spread goes from 1 contract to 3.
- **Filtering:** "one contract risks more than the budget" now needs more than $2,500 per contract. MPC (×7), MRVL, COHR and HUM were rejected at $1,000.

Max open positions was not given, so it stays at the placeholder of 10, and is guidance only until `on` enforces it. At $2,500 × 10, the open book's worst case is the whole $25,000. The §4 study re-run must use `--capital 25000 --risk 2500`, because the #43 portfolio result (+75%, 9% drawdown) was measured at $1,000 per spread.
