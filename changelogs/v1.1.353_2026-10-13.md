## Fix: a spread's two legs are two positions, not one stored twice

QC-009 ("the same natural key stored twice from one response") had three blocking findings open. Investigated 2026-10-13:

| Finding | Table | What was duplicated | Cause |
|---|---|---|---|
| #14130 | `raw_account_position_snapshot` | AMD on 2026-09-24, 09-25, 09-29 | **Our key.** An AMD put spread (long 520P, short 540P, 10-16 expiry) is two positions sharing the underlying `symbol`. The key `(account, symbol, date)` could not tell them apart. |
| #184 | `raw_analyst_recommendation` | XOM, period 2026-07, in every weekly sweep (runs 8, 27, 44, 60) | **Finnhub.** The response carries the same month twice with identical counts. |
| #185 | `raw_earnings_event` | FDXF in runs 63, 64, 69, 75 | **Finnhub.** One response lists two upcoming reports a day apart for the new FedEx Freight spin-off: 10-29 (FY2026 Q4) and 10-28 (FY2027 Q1). |

**Changed:** the natural key of `raw_account_position_snapshot` gains `osi_symbol` (dictionary v23). It is null for stock rows, so equity positions key as before. QC-009 closes a finding raised under a since-redefined key as `rule_changed`. Rehearsed on the 2026-10-12 snapshot, #14130 closes and blocking goes 24 → 21.

**Not changed:** #184 and #185 are provider data stored as sent. They are operator rulings.
