# v1.1.183 — 2026-09-04

## feat — the Roll the Dice email links straight into E*Trade

The email states an order to place. Until now, acting on it meant retyping the ticker into a
browser — which is the point at which a "you can act on this unread" feature stops being one.

**Both pages, because they answer different questions.** The snapshot quote page is *what is it
doing right now*; the Quotes & Research option chain (`content=3`) is *what is this strike
actually bid*. Neither substitutes for the other at the moment an order goes in, so the email
gives both rather than choosing:

- the ticker in the order headline (`Sell 1 **MKC** $50 put`) links to the quote page — the
  instinctive tap target
- a link row directly beneath carries `E*Trade: quote · option chain` explicitly
- the `withinBudget` alternative card carries the same pair, smaller

**One module owns the URLs** (`src/main/services/etrade-links.ts`). This is not premature tidying —
the two URLs disagree about symbol casing (`symbol=MKC` uppercase for the quote page,
`sym=mkc` lowercase for quotes-and-research) and the options URL contains a bare `&` that must be
escaped inside an HTML attribute or the href silently truncates in a strict parser. Both facts are
invisible at a call site that is copy-pasting a string. `brief-report-builder.ts` and
`filters-prefill-report-builder.ts` already carried the chain link inline in three places; they
now call the helper (`etradeOptionsHref`, the `&`-escaped variant for builders with no escaper of
their own). No behaviour change to those two reports — the emitted URLs are byte-identical.

**Rendered as plain `<a>` text links, not buttons.** A bare anchor is the one construct every mail
client renders the same way, and this email is read on a phone.

## Tests

`tests/dice-email-builder.test.ts` gains two cases: both URLs present with the right casing for
the pick, the same for the within-budget alternative, and a negative assertion that no bare
`content=3&sym=` survives into the HTML. The existing "leads with the order to place" assertion
was updated — the headline sentence is now split by the anchor around the ticker, so it asserts
the parts and the link rather than the flat string.
