# v1.1.243 — 2026-09-12

Existing app only. NewApp TODO **#42** (first of #42–44, the bull put spread track).

## Why

Operator decisions 2026-09-12: second strategy after wheel is a **bull put credit spread** — premium
not direction, **5–15 day** hold, risk as fixed dollars or a percent of the trade, delivered as a
**new brief mode alongside wheel**. Draft criteria (to be tested, not shipped): 21–35 DTE entry,
short put 0.20–0.30Δ, credit ≥ 30% of width, IV rank ≥ ~30; exits at 50% profit, 15 days held,
or 2× credit loss.

No stored data can price a spread honestly: `options_premium_history`, `brief_candidates` and
`iv_history` hold the short leg or ATM IV only. A Black-Scholes wing at ATM IV ignores put skew and
overstates the credit; model marks would drive the 50% exit. Capture is one-directional, so it
starts before the study.

## What

- Migration 083 `spread_chain_capture` — one row per (date, slot, ticker, expiration); `reason`
  `entry` | `track`; `puts_json` = `[[strike, bid, ask, delta, iv_pct, oi, volume], …]` ascending.
- `src/main/services/spread-chain-capture.ts` — `captureSpreadChains()` + `selectPuts()`.
  - entry: latest brief run's `wheel`/`income`/`bullish` candidates with disposition
    `picked`/`capped_out`, expiries 14–45 DTE, |Δ| 0.03–0.50 or (no delta) strike 65–100% of spot.
  - track: entry series from the prior 21 calendar days, unexpired — re-quoted without a listing
    call, keeping every strike in the entry's range so a breached short strike is still marked.
    Names that left the pools stay tracked (dropping them would bias exits toward survivors).
  - 730-day retention; never throws.
- Brief job: runs after the shortlist, **outside** the email block, with this run's slot; outcome
  logged as `spread-capture:run`.
