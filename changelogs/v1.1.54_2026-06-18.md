# v1.1.54 — 2026-06-18

## v2 execution trades the Wheel (defined-risk put credit spread) — multi-strategy engine, step 1

The engine moves from a single setup (inflection) toward a **book** of validated edges. Wheel is
the first addition; iron condors (income) are next.

### The change
`brief-entry-source` is generalized from inflection-only to **per-strategy**:

- `loadBriefPickInputs(db, strategies, now)` reads the latest brief for each eligible strategy and
  maps its picks; `loadInflectionPickInputs` is now a thin wrapper.
- `briefPickToPickInput(pick, strategy, briefId, opts)` resolves the credit structure per strategy:
  - **inflection** → direction-locked vertical (short-put-vertical up / short-call-vertical down).
  - **wheel** → **short-put-vertical** (the small-account "wheel": a naked CSP can't be sized at
    $10k — a $50 put is $5,000 cash-secured — so trade its defined-risk put-credit-spread cousin,
    which the lab already computes, budget-sized, for every ticker).
- `ExecutionEngine.generateEntriesFromLatestBrief` now pulls from `config.eligibleStrategies`
  (the union), and `EntryPolicy` filters + caps across all of them. All pricing/sizing/fills/exits
  are reused unchanged — the engine was already strategy-agnostic.

### Validation (FR-SEY-5)
`scripts/fr-wheel-spread-study.mjs` — the same budget-width short-put-vertical sim (BS/IV-priced,
30 DTE, 50% PT / 2× stop, $500 budget) over each strategy's replay picks:

| | n | win | return / max-loss |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **wheel** | 1,388 | **79.8%** | **8.2%** |
| inflection (benchmark) | 1,383 | 75.1% | 6.1% |
| baseline (wheel tickers, entry −180d) | 1,415 | 74.3% | 3.7% |

Wheel beats its own random-day baseline by **+5.5 pts win / +4.5 pts return** — a real selection
edge, not just the structural high win-rate of put spreads — and exceeds the already-shipped
inflection edge. Wheel composites (66–75) clear the 50 floor and every pick carries a critic
verdict, so the existing gates apply with no special-casing. Migration **056** adds `wheel` to
`eligible_strategies` (preserves a user override).

### Why this is the right kind of expansion
The daily/weekly caps apply across the **union** of strategies, so adding wheel **widens the
candidate pool** (more days have a tradeable, validated setup) **without raising the trade rate** —
the engine still takes its best 1/day, 3/week. That's "capital deployed in validated edges," not
"trade every day."
