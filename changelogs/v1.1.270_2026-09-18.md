# v1.1.270 — 2026-09-18

`core` 0.6.25, `next` 0.4.13 (TradeAnalyzer 2 has no code change; rebuilt to bundle the new core).

## E*Trade becomes the collector's second earnings source

**What QC-603 reported on 2026-09-18:** 17 chain-subset stocks with no future report date ever observed — 13 of them promoted to blocking after six runs.

**What they actually were.** Every one was a **Finnhub coverage gap**, not a truncation: the night's sweep covered 2026-09-18..12-16 and subdivided every capped window down to uncapped slices. They fall into three groups:

| group | tickers | Finnhub |
|---|---|---|
| another share class | GOOG, NWS, LLYVA, LLYVK, BRK.B, GFL | lists the sibling (GOOGL 10-27, NWSA 11-04, FWONA 11-03 — a different company) or nothing |
| recent listings / spin-offs | HONA, SUNB, JAN, MDLN, SPCX, CBRS, BMNR | nothing yet |
| established names | SWKS, IOT, ROIV, JHX | nothing, though each reports inside the window |

The collector had one source, so nothing could fill them. QC-602 (Finnhub vs E*Trade) had been reporting "no second source" since the generation began.

**Fix — collection.** `runEtradeEarnings` runs right after the Finnhub sweep and asks E*Trade `/v1/market/quote/{t}?detailFlag=ALL&requireEarningsDate=true`, the existing app's per-ticker source:

- every non-fund subset stock this run's Finnhub sweep gave no future date (capped at 60, so a failed sweep cannot become ~500 calls) — ~17 calls tonight;
- plus a **rotating 10-ticker overlap sample** of stocks Finnhub *did* date, never-asked first and then longest since last asked. Without it the two sources are disjoint by construction and QC-602 would compare nothing.

Parsing: `nextEarningDate` as `MM/DD/YYYY` (the existing app's parser of the same field) or ISO → stored as observed, **no Saturday shift** (QC-604 flags the UTC artefact; a raw table that corrects its source cannot be replayed). Empty means no announced date and writes no row. **Any other format throws**, so the response is indexed as a parse failure instead of storing a wrong date or quietly storing none. Rows are keyed on the ticker asked, not the body's symbol (v1.1.257).

**Fix — gate.** QC-602 now also fires when both sources are present but **no ticker is dated by both** — "no overlap" and "clean overlap" must not look alike.

## What this cannot promise

E*Trade may be blank for many of these: the existing app's `earnings-refresh` asked it about IOT and JAN tonight and got no date for either (82 "not yet announced" in all). New listings with no announced first report will keep QC-603 firing, and they should — for them there is genuinely no earnings date to protect a trade with. The format assumption is from the existing app's parser, not a stored response; the first live run settles it, and a wrong assumption surfaces as parse failures on `/v1/market/quote/{symbol}`, not as wrong dates.
