# v1.1.181 — 2026-09-03

## fix — total capital now uses E*Trade's net liquidation, not a derived sum

Found by verifying the first live numbers rather than trusting that they rendered.

### The discrepancy

| | |
|---|---|
| derived total (available + committed) | **$82,541** |
| E*Trade `totalAccountValue` | **$79,688** |
| gap | **$2,853** — almost exactly the covered call's liability |

`available + committed` ignores short-option obligations. A written call reduces net liquidation
without freeing cash or releasing the shares behind it, so the derived sum overstates the account
by the cost of buying that call back. Quoting it as "total" would have disagreed with the
operator's own E*Trade screen by the size of the option they wrote — the kind of small mismatch
that quietly discredits an entire panel.

### The fix

`total` now prefers the broker's `totalAccountValue`, falling back to the derived sum only when
the response omits it. `totalSource` records which, and `unreconciled` carries the gap so the
three headline figures visibly reconcile instead of silently disagreeing. The strip explains it
in plain words — *"short option obligations, which reduce account value without freeing cash"* —
in neutral styling, because it is normal whenever a short option is open, not a problem to fix.

Utilisation on the live book therefore reads **47%** of $79,688 rather than 46% of $82,541.

### What was confirmed correct

The double-count guard held: committed reads **$37,637** with the covered call contributing zero.
A wrong implementation would have shown **$34,784**.

### Also noticed, not acted on

The account reports `marginBuyingPower` of **$194,684** — it is a margin account, not a cash one.
Available cash ($44,904) is therefore not the binding constraint on how many puts could be sold;
buying power is far larger. Utilisation is deliberately still computed against cash and net liq,
since the operator's practice is cash-secured, but the figure is captured in `account_balances`
if that ever needs revisiting.
