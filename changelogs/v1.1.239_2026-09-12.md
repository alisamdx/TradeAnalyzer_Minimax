# v1.1.239 — 2026-09-12

Existing app only. `@tradeanalyzer/core` 0.6.8 and TradeAnalyzer 2 0.4.8 unchanged.

## Why now

Pre-Monday audit. The chain subset rule (`close ≥ $10`, 20-day dollar volume ≥ $233M) admits ~500 tickers (NewApp 04 §11, measured 2026-09-05). It had admitted about one, because bar volume was stored as 0 until v1.1.236. Monday 2026-09-14 is therefore the first daily run whose chain phase runs at its designed size — and its E\*Trade path had never been paced.

## Defect

- NewApp 04 §3 plans phase 7 at 120 E\*Trade calls/min (~4,500–7,000 calls, 33–54 min).
- `ElectronCollectorHost.etradeGet` had no limiter; `runChainsPhase` loops sequentially with no delay. At ~300 ms per E\*Trade call that is ~200 calls/min.
- The only E\*Trade pace this app has sustained: `daily-iv-capture.ts`, `DEFAULT_CALL_DELAY_MS = 1_100` after each ticker's expirations + chain pair (~110/min), commented "stays under E*Trade rate limit".
- E\*Trade documents per-module, per-second, per-user limits and drops the excess. `etradeGet` renews and retries on any failure, and `runChainsPhase` counts a failed chain call without retrying.

## Change

- `index.ts`: `const etradeLimiter = new TokenBucketRateLimiter({ requestsPerMinute: 120, burstSize: 2 })`, passed to `makeCollectorHost`.
- `ElectronCollectorHostOptions.etradeLimiter`; `etradeGet` acquires before the first `etradeGetRaw` and again before the post-renew retry.
- NewApp 04 §3: revision note that the budgeted pace is now enforced.

## Tests

`tests/collector-host.test.ts` — `paces every E*Trade market call, the post-renew retry included`: counts `etradeGetRaw(` calls in `etradeGet` (2) against `await this.opts.etradeLimiter.acquire(1)` (2), checks ordering, one construction in `index.ts`, and the factory wiring.
