# v1.1.317 — 2026-09-23

## QC-602 escalated on repetition, not evidence, and fired on estimate noise

### What the check was doing

On 2026-09-23 QC-602 fired on 10 of the 11 tickers it compared. Four of those
findings were blocking: AAPL, ABT, ACN and ABBV. Two separate faults were behind that.

**1. A single observation was re-compared every night.** The collector asks
E*Trade for a rotating overlap sample of 10 Finnhub-covered tickers per run. About
half come back with no date, so each covered ticker is asked roughly once every
eleven weeks. The check read each ticker's *latest* E*Trade row, whatever run it
came from, and compared it against the night's fresh Finnhub date. So AAPL's
E*Trade row, fetched once in run 27, was compared on runs 27, 28, 30 and 33. Each
of those counted as a consecutive firing, and the gate's escalation promoted it
to blocking. The escalation measured repetition of the comparison, not repetition
of the observation. These findings also could not clear: ACN's E*Trade date would
never be re-fetched before its report.

**2. The threshold was tighter than the noise.** These are all the same-run pairs
through 2026-09-23:

| ticker | Finnhub | E*Trade | gap | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ACN | 10-01 | 09-27 (Sun) | 4 | **announced 10-01 before the open**: E*Trade wrong |
| ABBV | 10-23 | 10-30 | 7 | **announced 10-23 before the open**: E*Trade wrong |
| AEP | 10-22 | 11-02 | 11 | not announced |
| AAPL, ABT, ADI, ADM, ADP, AFL, AIG | | | 2 each | not announced |
| AGNC | 10-19 | 10-20 | 1 | not announced |

Before a company announces, both sources publish an *estimate*, and here they
differed by about 2 days almost every time. With a threshold of 1 day, those
estimates were bound to fire. The two announced dates in the sample were both
E*Trade errors, of 4 and 7 days.

### The fix

- **E*Trade is compared only in the run that fetched it.** A ticker not re-asked
  this run is not evaluated. Its open finding stays open, not firing, and cannot
  escalate.
- **Threshold 1 → 3 days (`measured`).** Three separates the two populations. A
  threshold of 5 would have missed ACN, a real error 10 days before the report.
- **Open findings are re-judged on their own recorded evidence**, never on absence.
  Each is closed as `rule_changed` when:
  - its recorded gap is inside the new threshold;
  - its severity was escalated under the old rule (the note names the single run
    it came from and keeps the gap in the evidence);
  - both of its report dates have passed.

  A real disagreement that is still in the future and at base severity stays open
  until E*Trade is asked again.
- **A clean same-run evaluation closes a finding as `data_corrected` only if the
  dates moved.** The same gap judged under the wider threshold is a rule change,
  and the record says so. The first rehearsal caught this: AFL and AIG had closed
  as "re-asked… data_corrected" on the observation that opened them.

### Result

Rehearsed on the 2026-09-23 generation, QC-602 went from **10/11 fired to 0/10**
and blocking from 131 to 127. Seven findings closed as inside the threshold, two
(ACN, ABBV) closed as escalated under the old rule, and AEP's 11-day disagreement
stays open as a warning.

### Tests

Five new tests in `packages/core/test/events.test.ts` cover:

- no escalation across four runs without a re-fetch;
- the 2-day estimate gap not firing, while ACN's 4-day gap does;
- each of the three closers;
- a real disagreement staying open;
- a re-observation that moved closing as `data_corrected`;
- the same gap re-judged closing as `rule_changed`.

All five fail against the previous check.

### Noted

Finnhub has ACN as `amc` (after the close). The release is before the open, so
the time of day is wrong even though the date is right. It matters to
`isEarningsPending` only on the report day itself.
