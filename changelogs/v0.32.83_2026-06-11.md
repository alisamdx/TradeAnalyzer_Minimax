# v0.32.83 — 2026-06-11

## Change: EDGAR fundamentals use true TTM (10-Q roll-forward)

### The staleness problem

Slot-0 income/CF values came from the most recent 10-K only. For a company early in
its fiscal year that is badly stale — MSFT's fiscal year ends in June, so in June 2026
its "TTM" revenue/EPS were from June 2025 (11.5 months old) while P/E divided today's
price by year-old earnings. Apple was ~8.5 months stale.

### The fix

New `ttmPair(entries)` in `sec-edgar-provider.ts` — the standard XBRL roll-forward:

```
TTM        = FY      + YTD(current)    − YTD(1 year back)
TTM prior  = FY prev + YTD(1 yr back)  − YTD(2 years back)
```

- **Current YTD**: longest 10-Q duration fact starting after the FY end (at a given
  quarter-end both the single-quarter and cumulative YTD facts exist with the same
  end date — ties break by duration so the cumulative fact wins).
- **Matching comparatives**: same duration ±21 days, ending 350–380 (or 700–760) days
  earlier; latest-filed wins. The two-years-back YTD comes from the year-ago 10-Q's
  own reporting, which is present in companyfacts.
- **Consistency rule**: TTM mode only when the full chain (YTD, −1y, −2y) exists.
  Otherwise both slot 0 and slot 4 fall back to plain fiscal years — never a TTM
  numerator over an FY denominator, which would misstate YoY growth by the stub
  difference.
- Right after a 10-K (no newer 10-Q yet) there is no current YTD → FY mode, which is
  exactly correct at that point in the cycle.

5Y CAGR (slot 16) keeps its annual base from the deduped `annualSeries`.
Balance sheet was already point-in-time (latest instant) — unchanged.
