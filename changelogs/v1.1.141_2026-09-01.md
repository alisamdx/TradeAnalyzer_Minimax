# v1.1.141 — 2026-09-01

## fix: a reassigned ticker splices two companies into one price series

Third and last bug class in the `historical_prices` integrity thread, after unadjusted
splits (v1.1.139) and permanent misclassification (v1.1.140).

### The problem

A symbol can be reassigned to a completely different company — rename, reverse merger,
de-SPAC, delisting then reuse. The stored series then splices two unrelated entities, and
**no re-fetch can repair it**: the provider keys by ticker too, so it returns the same
spliced data. v1.1.139/140 could only ever mark these `confirmed_real`, which is exactly
what happened.

Found 2026-09-01 after the re-opened verdicts came back `0 repaired, 8 confirmed real`.
Volume told the real story — a split rescales share count and settles, a news event spikes
then decays, but only a *different company* shifts the volume regime permanently:

| ticker | median vol before → after | ratio | verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BNY — "Bank of New York Mellon Corp." | 46,254 → 3,519,645 | **76×** | reuse |
| SPCX — "Space Exploration Technologies" | 1,531 → 82,047,547 | **53,592×** | reuse |
| MRNA | 5,163,266 → 25,820,564 | 5.0× | genuine |
| PRIM / FMC / CORT / KD / CAR | — | 2.7× / 2.6× / 2.0× / 2.0× / 0.4× | genuine |

The six genuine moves top out at 5.0×, so the 15× threshold sits three times above the
largest of them. Medians over 20 bars either side, skipping a 3-bar buffer so the event-day
spike is excluded.

**This also corrected an earlier wrong call of mine.** I had suspected MRNA of being an
unadjusted 1:3 reverse split because it stepped 62.96 → 174.38 and stayed. Volume settles
it: 46× volume and a 35.7% intraday range on the day is a real repricing event. A split day
has a *normal* intraday range, because all four OHLC values are already in the new scale.
"Steps and stays" does not imply a split — a permanent repricing on news does the same.

### Fix

- **`volumeRegimeRatio`** (pure) — median volume after ÷ before, returning `null` when
  either side is too thin to judge so the caller can decline to act.
- **`isTickerReuse`** — true beyond `TICKER_REUSE_VOLUME_RATIO` (15) in either direction;
  reuse can cut volume as well as raise it.
- **Checked BEFORE any re-fetch.** It saves a pointless API call, and it is the only one of
  the three verdicts that is not a question about price adjustment at all.
- **Truncation, not flagging.** The previous company's bars are deleted from
  `historical_prices` **and `iv_history`**. Deleting is right because every consumer reads
  those tables directly — indicators, scoring, backtests and Brief Replay all become
  correct with no change to any of them. `iv_history` matters as much as prices: it feeds
  IV rank over a 252-day window, and BNY held **246 of 316 rows** on the wrong company.
- Recorded as `status='ticker_reuse'` with the `volume_ratio` evidence (migration 070
  rebuilds the table for the extended CHECK and the new column, then re-opens BNY and SPCX
  so the next run classifies them properly).

Run note now reads `Split scan: N repaired, M confirmed real, K ticker-reuse truncated`.
