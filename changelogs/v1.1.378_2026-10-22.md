# v1.1.378 — 2026-10-22

## TradeAnalyzer 2: a live pin no longer keeps a delisted ticker in the chain subset

`core` 0.6.86, `next` 0.4.71.

### What was firing

On 2026-10-22 (run 104) six gate findings were blocking. Two were QC-603 (earnings freshness) on PSKY and WBD. Both were delisted by mergers: no bars after 2026-10-05, out of `ref_universe_membership` on 10-12 (`index_drop`), and E\*Trade answered `optionexpiredate` with HTTP 400 / code 10033 "invalid symbol" on runs 97, 101 and 104. Finnhub stopped listing them on 10-09, so QC-603 fired, and the 3-run rule promoted both to blocking.

They were still judged because their `ref_chain_subset_membership` rows were open — the only two open subset rows with no open universe membership. Phase 7 closes a member `delisted` on the invalid-symbol 400, but only when the ticker had no live pin (`stickyExpirations.size === 0`). PSKY holds 9 `recommended_by_legacy` pins (last expiring 11-06), WBD 3 (10-23). QRVO, EA, EQR and WBS closed correctly earlier because they had none.

### Change

- `runChainsPhase` drops the pin condition: a member answering the invalid-symbol 400 closes `delisted` whether or not it holds a live pin.
- Why the condition bought nothing: the same phase already asks about every pinned ticker outside the subset (`pinOnly`, at the pinned expirations only), so closing the membership does not stop pinned contracts being asked for. QC-415 already reports pins on a ticker E\*Trade no longer lists. The open membership only made every subset-scoped check (QC-603, QC-905, …) keep judging a dead ticker.
- QC-603 needs no change. Its `departedFindingClosers` already closes a finding `rule_changed` once the ticker has a closed subset range and no open one.
- 04 §4.2: the invalid-symbol removal path, and why a pin does not hold it open.
