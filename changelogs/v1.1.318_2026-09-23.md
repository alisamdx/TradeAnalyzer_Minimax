# v1.1.318 — 2026-09-23

## QC-410 closes findings on a clean day, and a study of what flagged Greeks cost the wheel

### QC-410: a clean evaluation now closes the finding

QC-410 asks whether a ticker's E*Trade Greeks were computed off the price its own
chain reports. The chain is re-fetched every run, so that is a claim about
*today*. A clean evaluation carried no closer, though. A ticker that drifted back
under the 1 % threshold kept its finding open for ever: on 2026-09-23 there were
**132 findings open against 69 firing**, 89 of them warnings that no longer fired.
A clean evaluation now closes its finding as `provider_fixed`, the same shape
QC-603 uses for earnings freshness. Rehearsed on 2026-09-23: 96 findings closed,
the 69 still firing untouched, blocking unchanged at 127. Escalation to blocking
is unaffected. Unlike QC-602's, it rests on genuine daily re-observation: AMG,
LOPE, PCTY, HLNE, JAZZ, ITT and CW have been off by 7–29 % on every run.

### What the flagged tickers cost the wheel

`scripts/wheel-delta-selection-study.mjs` re-runs the wheel CSP rule from
`AnalysisService.findSuitableChain` on every E*Trade chain stored in the
TradeAnalyzer 2 generation, 2026-09-14..23. The rule is: first expiration at
30–45 DTE, |delta| in 0.20–0.30, bid > 0, nearest 0.25. It runs twice, once with
E*Trade's delta and once with a Black-Scholes delta at the chain's own price.

| QC-410 verdict | ticker-days | same contract | different | provider pick outside 0.20–0.30 by our model |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| flagged | 228 | 68 | **113** | **67** |
| clean | 2,968 | 2,361 | 550 | 99 |

On flagged tickers the provider-delta rule makes real errors:

- **AMG, 2026-09-14:** the $390 put, **11 % in the money, true delta −0.83**, chosen as "−0.29".
- **FERG and OC, 2026-09-16:** puts 2.6–2.7 % in the money, chosen as out of the money.
- **CRL and VKTX:** puts 16–21 % out of the money, chosen as 0.25 delta.

**The clean row is the other half of the finding.** Even where E*Trade's Greeks
are consistent, its delta and a plain European Black-Scholes pick different
contracts 19 % of the time. That is the known model gap (E*Trade's American
pricer sits 0.02–0.03 below Black-Scholes), not an error. So replacing the
provider delta everywhere would quietly shift the wheel's target moneyness on
every ticker. That is a strategy change needing FR-SEY-5 validation, and it
cannot be scored until the picks expire. **Nothing in TradeAnalyzer's strike
selection changes in this release.** The fix under discussion is narrower: use a
model delta only on tickers whose Greeks fail the consistency check.

An IV solved from each contract's mid agrees with E*Trade's IV on the chosen
contract 2,898 times in 3,110. E*Trade's IV is usable even where its delta is not.
