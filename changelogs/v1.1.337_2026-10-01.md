# v1.1.337 — 2026-10-01

## Brief Replay settles income/wheel picks on real premium

v1.1.336 measured Brief Replay's income/wheel proxy against real outcomes on ~58,000 settled puts
(entries 2026-06-15 → 08-26). The proxy — a synthetic strike at 0.85 × entry, "kept" if no low over
21 sessions touches it, no premium — **agreed only ~77% of the time and overstated "kept"**: 85% of
puts, against ~73% that actually avoided assignment. About one put in five it called kept was
assigned.

**Change.**
- **Migration 089** adds `replay_outcomes.outcome_source` (`real` | `proxy`) and `real_strike`,
  `real_premium`, `real_expiration`, `real_settle`, `real_assigned`, `real_roc_pct`,
  `real_skip_reason`.
- **`BriefReplayService.settleRealOutcomes()`** settles each income/wheel pick on the CSP that
  `options_premium_history` recorded for the same date, ticker and strategy, held to expiry:
  `ROC = (premium − max(0, strike − S_T)) / strike`. It calls `settleStoredCsp` — the same function
  behind `scripts/premium-roc-report.mjs`, extracted from `premium-roc.ts` so the two cannot disagree —
  including its split-scale guard.
- **When it runs:** after every replay's outcome evaluation **and** whenever Brief Replay loads its
  summaries, because a put expires weeks after the replay that picked it. A `real` outcome is final.
- **Fallback, labelled:** a pick with no recorded CSP (every replay date before premium history
  began), an unexpired put, or one the scale guard refuses stays `proxy`, with the reason stored.
- **Shown separately, never blended:** a new "Income & Wheel — real option outcome" table (settled
  count, assigned %, average and median ROC, then the proxy-only count and its "kept" rate), a
  per-pick **Real ROC** column (`A` = assigned; hover for strike, expiry and premium, or why it is
  still a proxy), and the premium-history banner now points there.

**Unchanged:** the HR and Avg 5d/10d/21d columns still score the stock's move for every strategy;
the 💰 P&L backtest still simulates a theoretical CSP per pick.

Tests: `tests/replay-real-premium-outcome.test.ts` (9) — kept and assigned settlement, the
proxy-says-kept-but-assigned case, proxy fallback with no recorded put, an unexpired put settling on
a later load, a split refused with its reason, strategy matching, non-premium strategies untouched,
real and proxy-only summarised separately, and a real outcome never revisited.
