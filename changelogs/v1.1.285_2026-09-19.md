# v1.1.285 — 2026-09-19

`core` 0.6.40, `next` 0.4.28.

## Findings on a ticker that left the subset close from the recorded exit

### Problem

QC-603 (earnings freshness) and QC-905 (recommendation freshness) judge **current chain-subset members** only. A ticker that leaves is never evaluated again, so any finding open on it stays open for ever (P9). On the 2026-09-18 snapshot that was EQR:

| record | date |
|---|---|
| `ref_universe_membership` S&P 500 and Russell 1000 closed, `change_reason = 'index_drop'` | 2026-09-15 |
| `ref_chain_subset_membership` closed | 2026-09-17 |
| last bar | 2026-08-17 |

EA and WBS left the subset the same day without open findings. Every future delisting or acquisition will produce the same situation.

### Why the gate may close it

v1.1.282 kept EQR open on the principle that a disappearance is not evidence. This is not a disappearance. The collector's own reconciliation **recorded** the exit as a closed membership range, with the universe's reason beside it, and that record is what the check reads. A ticker with no membership row at all has no such record and stays open.

### Fix

`departedFindingClosers(ctx, checkId)` in `earnings-fundamentals.ts`, used by QC-603 and QC-905: for each open finding whose ticker has a closed subset range and no open one, and is not a fund (funds belong to `fundFindingClosers`), it emits a not-fired evaluation with `closesAs: rule_changed`. The note names the subset exit date and, when the ticker has left the universe entirely, the universe exit date and each list's reason, sorted so the text is deterministic.

### The resolution kind

The operator chose `rule_changed`, read as **"the rule no longer applies to this key"**, which the spec now states. None of the four kinds names a scope exit: `data_corrected` and `provider_fixed` claim a correction that did not happen, and `false_positive` would say the finding was wrong while EQR was a member. A dedicated `out_of_scope` kind would change `resolution_kind`'s CHECK, which is baked into the populated `qa_exception` table and cannot be rebuilt within a generation, so it waits for the next one.

### Measured
