# v0.32.51 — 2026-06-09

## Retrospect › Brief Trend: 5-day trading-day columns

### Changed
- Replaced the single "Day+5 Price" column with five individual columns (Day 1 – Day 5).
  Each column shows the closing price on the Nth trading day after the brief date.
  Weekends and market holidays are naturally skipped because the query uses
  `historical_prices` (which only holds market-open days) with `ROW_NUMBER() OVER
  (PARTITION BY ticker ORDER BY date ASC)`.
- A cell shows the price once that trading day has passed; ⏳ while still pending.
- Return % = (latest available day price − brief price) / brief price. Updates daily.
- Outcome verdict is based on the latest available day (not locked to Day 5).
- Tracking caps at Day 5 — no day 6+ columns.
- Removed Entry Zone and In Zone? columns.

### Technical
- `getBriefPickDrilldown` in `paper-trading-service.ts` now queries `historical_prices`
  directly instead of `paper_trade_snapshots`. Entry-zone lookup from `analysis_snapshots`
  removed.
- `BriefPickDetail` in `shared/types.ts`: replaced `entryZoneLow/High`, `day5ReturnPct`,
  `day5DaysActual`, `day5Verdict` with `day1–5Price`, `returnPct`, `latestVerdict`.
