# v0.27.4 — 2026-06-06

## Retrospect — brief date dropdown in In Progress tab

The In Progress tab no longer stacks all brief dates into one long scroll. Instead a dropdown at the top lets you select which brief to view:

- Dates sorted descending (latest first)
- Latest brief selected by default on load
- "— latest" label on the most recent entry
- Pick count shown next to the selector
- Selecting a different date clears expanded rows
