# v1.1.46 — 2026-06-17

## v2 execution — Phase 6b (part 1): the brief → entry source

### Context
Phase 6a (v1.1.45) put the IBKR adapter behind the `ExecutionBroker` seam, but the engine had
no source of entry candidates — the proposals queue stayed empty unless `generateEntries` was
called by hand. This wires the daily brief's inflection picks into the engine.

### The change
New `src/main/execution/brief-entry-source.ts`:

- `briefPickToPickInput(pick, briefId, { nextEarningsDate })` — **pure** mapping of one inflection
  `BriefPick` to a `PickInput`. It selects the direction-locked defined-risk vertical via the
  shared `pickRelevantStructure(scores, 'inflection', breakoutDirection)` (up → short-put-vertical,
  down → short-call-vertical), then projects the Strategy Lab setup onto the seam shape:
  - **legs** ← `setup.legs` (side→right, action, strike, expiry, qty→ratio);
  - **compositeScore** ← `pick.compositeScore`; **criticVerdict** ← `pick.criticVerdict.verdict`;
  - **hasAdequateLiquidity** ← lab `liquidityScore ≥ 8` **and** every leg two-sided;
  - **earningsBeforeExpiry** ← injected next-earnings date ≤ the vertical's expiry;
  - **maxLossPerContract** ← `abs(setup.maxLoss)`.
  Returns **null** when there's no viable direction-matched structure, the setup is unavailable,
  or max loss is unbounded/absent — those picks are skipped, never traded.
- `loadInflectionPickInputs(db, now)` — reads the latest committed `inflection` brief from
  `daily_briefs`, resolves each ticker's next earnings date from `earnings_calendar`, and returns
  the mapped `PickInput[]` (+ brief id). Never throws (empty list on missing/malformed brief).

`ExecutionEngine`:

- The market-cycle hook now runs **manage-then-generate**: after the management loop, it calls
  `generateEntriesFromLatestBrief(now)`. Wrapped fail-safe — entry generation can never throw into
  the cycle.
- `generateEntriesFromLatestBrief()` is a no-op when the broker is disconnected or there's no
  brief/picks; otherwise it hands the mapped picks to `EntryPolicy.generateEntries`.

### Why on the cycle, not at brief-commit
The brief commits post-market (16:30 ET) but the entry window is 10:00–15:00 ET, so generating at
brief-commit would reject every pick as `outside_entry_window`. Running on the market-open cycle
means the entry window, daily/weekly caps, critic/composite filters, and **live IBKR re-pricing**
all apply naturally; the daily cap (`generateEntries` short-circuits once the day's entry exists)
prevents repeats across cycles, and re-pricing against the live chain means a stale brief never
trades a stale price (only the strike selection comes from the brief).
