# v0.27.9 — 2026-06-06

## Fix: Price Gap Fill staleness threshold (-2 day → -1 day)

**Root cause:** The staleness check used `date('now', '-2 day')`. On Saturday Jun 6,
that evaluates to Jun 4 — the same date as the latest bar in historical_prices. So
`MAX(date) < Jun 4` = FALSE and the job found no stale tickers, even though Friday
Jun 5's close was missing entirely.

**Fix:** Changed both queries to `date('now', '-1 day')`. Behaviour by day:

| Today | Threshold | Last bar | Stale? |
|-------|-----------|----------|--------|
| Sat Jun 7 | Fri Jun 6 | Thu Jun 5 | ✅ yes — re-fetches Fri close |
| Mon Jun 9 | Sun Jun 8 | Fri Jun 6 | ✅ yes — re-fetches Mon close |
| Tue Jun 10 (after 16:00) | Mon Jun 9 | Mon Jun 9 | ❌ no — already fresh |

No behaviour change on normal weekdays when the job runs after market close.
