## QC-009 files one finding per duplicated key, not per table

**Why.** QC-009's finding key was the table name, so a single ruling covered every duplicate that table would ever hold. Investigating its open findings on 2026-10-13 showed what that meant in practice. Two of them were provider quirks worth accepting: Finnhub sends XOM's 2026-07 recommendation twice in every weekly sweep, and it listed FDXF twice in one earnings calendar. Accepting the `raw_analyst_recommendation` finding would also have hidden a duplicate for any *other* ticker until the acceptance expired.

**What changed** (`qa/checks/cross-cutting.ts`)
- One finding per duplicated natural key, keyed `<table> <key values>`: `raw_analyst_recommendation XOM 2026-07-01`, `raw_earnings_event FDXF finnhub`. The detail names the columns and values, the number of copies and how many responses it appeared in. The evidence lists the run ids.
- Each table still gets one clean evaluation, so `evaluated_count` keeps counting tables.
- More than 50 duplicated keys in one table become a single `<table> *` finding. That many is a parser emitting rows twice, not a provider quirk, and listing them would bury it.
- **Migration of open table-level findings.** Each is re-counted per response over the runs it was seen in. Each key still duplicated there is raised as its own finding, with `affects` set to the trading dates of the exact runs it appeared in. The table-level finding then closes `rule_changed`, naming its replacements. Closing it without that carry-over would lose XOM, whose duplicate lives in the weekly sweep's runs, not tonight's.
- A per-key finding closes only when its table's natural key is redefined. One that simply was not fetched tonight stays open, as before.

**Not migrated:** accepted findings. On gen-002 the operator accepted #184 (`raw_analyst_recommendation`, until 11-12) and #185 (`raw_earnings_event`, until 10-30) shortly before this change. They stay accepted and move to "acceptance expired" on their dates, to be resolved `rule_changed` by hand. Any new duplicate opens its own per-key warning in the meantime.

Rehearsed on a fresh 2026-10-12 snapshot: blocking 21, the AMD position finding #14130 closes `rule_changed` (dictionary v23), and the accepted findings are untouched.
