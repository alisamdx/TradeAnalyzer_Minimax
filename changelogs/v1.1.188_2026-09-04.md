# v1.1.188 — 2026-09-04

## The ownership read carries two axes in one sentence — and a caution comes out

The Roll the Dice ownership line now merges the long-run read with a **support** read, in a single
sentence. The measurable change to what the operator sees is a **withdrawal**, not an addition.

## What it reads like, in all four cells

Real numbers, from picks the replay actually scored:

```
A · both agree
If assigned you own VKTX at $27.55 — 17.8% BELOW its 200-day average, at the 23rd percentile
of its 52-week range — and the $30 strike sits below its 20-day low of $31.70, a price it has
not traded at in the last month.

B · long-run only
If assigned you own GLW at $137.75 — 2.7% BELOW its 200-day average, at the 36th percentile of
its 52-week range — but the $145 strike is above its 20-day low of $130.00: it has already
traded below your strike this month.

C · support only
If assigned you own SNOW at $267.75 — 22.8% ABOVE its 200-day average, at the 67th percentile
of its 52-week range — but the $280 strike sits below its 20-day low of $303.89, a price it has
not traded at in the last month.

D · neither
If assigned you own MPC at $349.30 — 44.6% ABOVE its 200-day average, at the 79th percentile of
its 52-week range — and the $360 strike is above its 20-day low of $302.20: it has already
traded below your strike this month. An extended price with no recent floor under it.
```

The connective is the merge: **and** when the two readings agree, **but** when they conflict.

## The change is a removal

*"That is an extended price, not a discount"* fired on **every** basis above SMA200 — cells C and D
together, **166 of 250 stored brief picks (66%)**.

Cell C measured **+3.46% mean / +5.49% median**, the **best of the four cells**.

So the clause was being applied to the picks most worth taking. It now fires only when both axes
agree — **52%** — and is reworded to say what it actually means: *"An extended price with no recent
floor under it."*

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| clause fires on | 166/250 (66%) | 131/250 (52%) |
| includes the best-performing cell | **yes** | no |

Nothing new fires at 50%. An existing line fires less, and stops being wrong about cell C. That
also answers the wallpaper objection raised when this was first proposed — a line appearing on two
thirds of picks was already close to wallpaper.

The in-app panel's warning colour used the identical over-firing condition
(`atOrBelowSma200 === false`) and now follows the sentence exactly, via `ownershipWarns`.

## Fail-closed on an unmeasurable axis

Fewer than 20 bars → `swingLow20` is `null` → `strikeBelowSwing` is `null`, and the sentence falls
back to the **exact** pre-v1.1.188 text, "extended price, not a discount" included.

An axis that could not be measured must never be the reason a caution disappears. Same reasoning as
the earnings gate's absent calendar row, and the reason `rangePctile` reports "not enough history"
rather than a pass.

## An asymmetry that is deliberate

The long-run half tests the **cost basis**. The support half tests the **STRIKE**.

That is exactly what was measured — T8 and its replay both split on `basis ≤ SMA200` and
`strike < 20-day low` — so the code matches the study rather than being tidied into symmetry. It is
called out in the type, in the function, and in `docs/formulas.md`, because it looks like a bug.

## Three copies became one

`own-at-strike.ts` moved from `src/main/services/` to **`src/shared/`**, because the sentence and
its type had quietly been duplicated:

- `DailyBriefView.tsx` kept a **hand-copied duplicate of the function** (with a comment saying "the
  panel and the email must never disagree" — which is exactly what a copy cannot guarantee)
- `preload/index.ts` kept a **hand-mirrored copy of the `ownership` type**, which would have
  silently omitted the new axis

This is the strategy-slug-map trap from v1.1.23/25, where four copies drifted until the card, the
email and the critic featured different structures for the same pick. One definition now, imported
by main, preload and renderer alike.

`readOwnAtStrike` takes `(strike, costBasis, window)` — the strike is new, because the support test
needs it.

## Evidence, and what did NOT change

| cell | share of brief picks | mean | median | assigned |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A both | 18% | +2.17% | 2.51% | 8% |
| B long-run only | 16% | +2.97% | 2.71% | 4% |
| C support only | 14% | **+3.46%** | **5.49%** | 26% |
| D neither | 52% | **−2.10%** | 2.15% | **44%** |

From `scripts/fr-wheel-entry-quality-study.mjs` (4,917 settled rows / 45 dates) and
`scripts/dice-entry-annotation-replay.mjs` (250 brief picks, 186 settled). Both axes survive
controlling for raw distance — below-swing strikes sit 2.6× further OTM, yet the split persists in
all four ATR-cushion quartiles.

**It remains an annotation. Nothing gates on it.** The evidence is one regime, which under FR-SEY-5
is not enough to change which trades are taken.
