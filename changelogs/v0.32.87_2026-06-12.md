## Fix: stale-concept guard (ORCL fake net-cash) + financial-sector ndEbitda exemption

### Issue 1 — ORCL netDebtToEbitda = −1.37 despite ~$90B of debt

Diagnosis from live EDGAR data: Oracle's only `LongTermDebt` entry in us-gaap is a
**$0 value from FY2022** (one entry, total); `LongTermDebtNoncurrent` and
`LongTermDebtAndCapitalLeaseObligations` are absent. Oracle tags its real debt in
dimensioned per-instrument contexts that companyfacts' us-gaap section doesn't
aggregate. `pickBestConcept` accepted the lone stale concept, `latestVal` served the
2022 zero as "current debt", and against $38.4B of current cash the ratio implied a
large net-cash position.

**Fix**: `conceptIsStale(entries, now)` — a concept whose newest entry (any form) is
older than **540 days** is skipped during concept selection. Any active filer has a
10-Q/10-K entry within ~120 days, so the window only rejects abandoned tags while
tolerating annual-only filers awaiting their next 10-K. Applied in `pickBestConcept`
(all roles) and the composite D&A base. Result for ORCL: debt role resolves to
**null** — `netDebtToEbitda`/`debtToEquity` null, no fabricated values.

Verified live: T, VZ, MO all tag current concepts from our list and are unaffected.

### Issue 2 — banks spuriously passing the leverage criterion

RF (Regions Financial) had `netDebtToEbitda` = −176; CE = 252, JAZZ = 909.
The value/growth-score criterion is `passLte(netDebtToEbitda, 3)` — a bank's absurd
negative *passes* it, silently inflating valueScore/growthScore/stockGrade.

**Fix** in `computeRatios()`:
- Financial sector → null (reuses the existing `isFinancial` detection that already
  exempts D/E) — EBITDA is not meaningful for banks.
- |ratio| > 50 → null (near-zero EBITDA denominators carry no signal).
- Legitimate negatives (true net-cash companies, e.g. SAM) are kept.
