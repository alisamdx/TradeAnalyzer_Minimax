# v0.32.90 — 2026-06-12

## Brief Replay: SPY regime risk gate for bearish picks

**Motivation**: 5-month replay analysis showed bearish HR averages ~79% during market downturns but only ~25% during SPY bull runs. The bearish signal is valid but regime-dependent — users need to know which historical picks were made in an unfavorable regime.

**Implementation**:
- `BriefReplayService.computeSpyRegimes()` — new private method. Queries SPY `historical_prices`, computes the 50-day SMA for each `as_of_date` in `replay_briefs`, returns a `Map<date, 'bullish' | 'bearish'>`. No schema change — purely read-time computation.
- `getPickRows()` — annotates each row with `spyRegime: 'bullish' | 'bearish' | null`
- `getSummaries()` — adds `regimeRiskDates: number | null` (bearish only: count of dates where SPY was in bullish regime)
- `ReplayPickRow` — new `spyRegime` field
- `ReplaySummary` — new `regimeRiskDates` field
- `BriefReplayView.tsx` — ⚠ badge in picks table strategy cell; "⚠ N/Total regime risk" annotation on bearish summary row with tooltip showing the historical HR differential

**Data from replay analysis** (Jan 2 – May 22, 2026):
- Bearish HR in Feb 2026 (SPY downtrend): 79%
- Bearish HR in Mar–May 2026 (SPY uptrend): 24–30%
- ~76 of 98 replay dates were in bullish SPY regime
