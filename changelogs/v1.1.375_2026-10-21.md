### What was wrong

QC-705 had no `closesAs` and no remediation. Under 03 §5.1 a finding that stops firing closes only on evidence from its check. So a filing re-read clean would have kept its finding open, blocking and `still_firing = 0` for good. That breaks the clean-day streak on its dates. v1.1.371's changelog said the ten "close as their filings are re-read", and a correction note has been added there.

### Change

`QC705_FACT_SOURCE_AGREEMENT` reads the open/acknowledged QC-705 keys. A clean evaluation of one of them carries `closesAs: data_corrected`, with a note naming the instance read (`source_response_id`) and its parser revision (`meta_ingest_recipe.args_json.rev`, default 1). The check re-compares the newest instance read against SEC's own extraction on every run, so a clean reading is evidence, not absence. A filing that still differs offers no closer. Neither does a clean filing with no open finding.
