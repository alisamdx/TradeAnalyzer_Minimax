`core` 0.6.32, `next` 0.4.20.

## QC-204 closes the price steps it can verify as genuine moves

### Problem

On 2026-09-18 QC-204 (and its alias QC-501) held 123 open findings. Most were genuine violent moves on data that was already correct, and nothing could close them: auto-resolve needs a remediation to change the data, which a correct series never does, and spec 05 §5 (P9) forbids the operator from bulk-resolving them.

### The test

A step is a **verified genuine move** when all of these hold:

1. **R6 succeeded** on the finding — the ticker's splits and dividends were re-fetched and none explains the boundary.
2. **One fetch covers both bars** — the bars either side share `adjusted_as_of`, and the step lies before it. A split executed before that fetch would already be applied, so the step is not an unadjusted split.
3. **The step is more than 90 days old** (`VERIFIED_MOVE_MIN_AGE_DAYS`). Providers backfill corporate actions late; a recent step keeps waiting.
4. **No reuse evidence** — no open or acknowledged QC-205/QC-209 on the same key, or QC-210 on the ticker. A reuse splice is not a genuine move and stays for the operator.

The check then evaluates the step as not fired (still measured, `evidence.verifiedGenuineMove`), and the gate resolves the open finding as **`false_positive`** with actor `gate` and a note naming the fetch date and the R6 time.

### Mechanism

- `Evaluation.closesAs` (new): a non-firing evaluation may name the resolution kind and note. `reconcileStoppedFiring` applies it before its default, so the record says the check was wrong to fire rather than that the data was corrected.
- `verifiedGenuineSteps(ctx, keys)` is exported from `checks/bars.ts`; QC-501 uses it so both findings close in the same run rather than QC-501 lagging a night.
