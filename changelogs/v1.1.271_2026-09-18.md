## QC-701's IQV and JCI findings: SEC's aggregate is behind, and our queue could not tell

**What QC-701 reported:** IQV's newest fact period ends 2026-04-30 (141 days), JCI's 2026-03-31 (171 days), both blocking after escalation.

**What was actually true.** Both companies filed their June-quarter 10-Q in late July (IQV 07-28, JCI 07-29), and SEC's submissions data flags both `isXBRL=1, isInlineXBRL=1`. The collector saw those filings and fetched companyfacts for both on 2026-09-16, **after** they were filed. The archived response for each stops at the May 10-Q (newest `filed` 05-05 and 05-06). A fresh fetch on 2026-09-18 returns the same. **SEC's companyfacts aggregate has not picked those filings up**, seven weeks on. JBS N.V. (CIK 1791942) is the same case: a 10-Q filed 08-10, newest fact filed 03-25. So QC-701's description — "past two quarters it is our pipeline, not the filer" — was not true of either finding.

**But the pipeline did have a fault, and it hid the evidence.** The fact pull took "the 10 newest filers with an unharvested report filing" every run, with no memory of having asked. A filer SEC never updates stays pending for ever and ranks near the top, so JBS was fetched on four of six runs, while 119 pending filings across 32 filers waited behind the newest few — IQV and JCI were asked once and never again.

## Fixes

- **`factsQueue`** — a pending filer is *due* when companyfacts has never been fetched for it, or was last fetched before its newest unharvested report filing (a new filing: ask now), or was last fetched more than **7 days** ago (SEC still lacked it: ask weekly). New filings first by filing date, then retries oldest-fetch first; still 10 per run. JBS drops to one call a week and the queue drains.
- **QC-701 names the cause** in its detail and `evidence.cause`: `no_newer_filing_seen` (a late filer, or one the submissions slice has not reached), `awaiting_fetch` (our queue), or `sec_companyfacts_missing` (fetched after the filing, still absent). Severity and escalation are unchanged — IQV's fundamentals really are four months old, and a trade reading them should know it.

## Not done (decided)
