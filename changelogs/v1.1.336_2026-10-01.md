# v1.1.336 — 2026-10-01

## Real income/wheel return on capital

`options_premium_history` (v0.32.94) was built so that, once enough captures matured, income and
wheel could be measured on **real** premium instead of a proxy. Readiness needs 40 capture dates at
least 45 days old; on 2026-10-01 the settled panels alone held 43, so the analysis is unblocked.

**What Brief Replay measures today is a proxy.** For income/wheel picks it invents a strike at
0.85 × the entry price and calls the pick "kept" if no low over the next 21 sessions touches it.
There is no premium in it, the strike is not the one recommended, and the test is a path low rather
than the close at expiry. Nobody has measured how often it agrees with what really happened.

**Change.** `services/premium-roc.ts`, read through `GET /api/v1/diagnostics/premium-roc?strategy=…`
and printed by `node scripts/premium-roc-report.mjs`:
- Every stored CSP is settled at expiry on its real premium:
  `ROC % = (premium − max(0, strike − S_T)) / strike × 100`, `S_T` = close on the last bar ≤
  expiration (handles holiday expirations). see `docs/formulas.md#premium-roc`.
- **Does not require bid/ask/OI**, unlike `settled-csp-panel`: every wheel row before v1.1.132 has
  them null, so requiring them would drop the whole early wheel history.
- **Split-scale guard.** A row whose stored `last_price` disagrees with the close on its capture date
  (> 15 %), or whose settlement close is outside 0.35–3.0× its entry, is excluded and listed by ticker
  — the CRWD rows once turned a backtest's +$97k into −$71k when settled across a repaired split.
- **Brief Replay's proxy is computed on the same rows**, so the report prints a 2×2 of proxy verdict
  against real assignment, the agreement rate, and real ROC within each proxy verdict.
- The report also breaks results down by entry month and IV rank, compares promised against realized
  annualized return, and gives a date-weighted mean so a day with many rows cannot outvote one with few.

**Not changed:** Brief Replay itself still scores income/wheel on the proxy. Whether to replace it
depends on what the agreement numbers show.

Tests: `tests/premium-roc.test.ts` (13) — settlement arithmetic, a row with no bid/ask, a holiday
expiration, a repaired split and an unrepaired step both excluded, a genuine 40 % drop kept as a real
assignment, the proxy mirrored exactly, and the proxy disagreeing with reality in both directions.
