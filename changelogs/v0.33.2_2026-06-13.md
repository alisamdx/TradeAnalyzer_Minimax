# v0.33.2 — 2026-06-13

## Fix: Brief Replay charts missing the Inflection strategy

The Inflection strategy scored in Brief Replay (v0.32.99+) but didn't appear correctly in
the Charts modal.

### Cause

- The **Monthly hit-rate chart** ("all strategies") iterated a hardcoded list
  `['buy','bullish','income','wheel','bearish']` — inflection was omitted from both the
  line series and the legend. The backend monthly builder also didn't emit an `inflection`
  field.
- `STRAT_COLORS` / `STRAT_LABEL` in `ReplayChartsModal` had no `inflection` entry, so it
  rendered with the gray fallback color and an un-labeled name in the Performance, Regime,
  and Ticker tabs (which already iterate `data.strategies`).

### Fix

- `ReplayChartMonthlyRow` gains an `inflection` field; the backend monthly builder adds
  `inflection: mHr('inflection')` (uses the already-direction-adjusted 21d return, so up = win).
- `ReplayChartsModal`: added inflection to `STRAT_COLORS` (`#00bcd4`), `STRAT_LABEL`, and the
  Monthly chart's line + legend lists.
- Unchanged by design: the Score-Signal and Rank charts are curated subsets
  (buy·bullish·bearish / buy·bullish·wheel) illustrating the anti-predictive pattern.

### Tests

- `tests/replay-direction.test.ts` (+1): `getChartData()` includes inflection in `strategies`
  and emits a direction-adjusted `inflection` monthly hit rate. Suite: 296 passing.
