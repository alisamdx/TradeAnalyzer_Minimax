# v0.32.36 — 2026-06-08

## AI Critic: prevent price hallucination from stale training data

### Problem
The critic LLM (claude-sonnet-4-6) was applying its training-data knowledge of historical stock prices to validate the live prices in the brief. When AMD appeared at $490 (correct current price), the critic flagged it as "fabricated data" because its training knew AMD at ~$110 from 2022–2023. This produced incorrect `flag` verdicts on real, valid picks.

### Fix — `critic-service.ts`

**Context**: replaced top-level `price` with a `marketData` block:
```json
{
  "price": 490.33,
  "date": "2026-06-08",
  "price52wReturnPct": 342.5
}
```
`price52wReturnPct` is pulled from `pick.signals['priceMomentumPct'].raw` when available. If AMD is up 342% in the last year, the model can calibrate that its remembered price is years out of date without needing an absolute recall.

**System prompt**: added an explicit anchor paragraph:
> All market data in the `marketData` field is live and current as of the `date` provided — sourced directly from real-time market data feeds. Do not use your training knowledge to assess whether a price is "too high", "wrong", or "unrealistic" — stock prices change dramatically over time and your training data may be years out of date. Treat the provided price as ground truth.
