# v0.30.0 — 2026-06-07

## Conviction List Phase 2 — Alerts

Implements FR-CV-01, FR-CV-02, and FR-CV-03 from `Requirements/conviction-list.md`.

### FR-CV-01 — Zone-entry toast notification
`App.tsx` polls `conviction:list` on mount and every 5 minutes. When a stock
transitions from any other zone into Buy Zone (price ≤ price_low), a green
success toast appears: "⚡ NVDA entered your Buy Zone ($178.40 ≤ $180.00)".
The toast auto-dismisses after 12 seconds and includes a "View My Stocks →" CTA.
First-load is excluded from triggering notifications (prev zone unknown).

### FR-CV-02 — Daily Brief Conviction Alerts section
`DailyBriefView.tsx` gains a `ConvictionAlertsSection` component rendered above
the strategy sections. It calls `conviction:list` on mount, filters for zone
`buy` or `mid`, and renders a compact table (ticker, price, zone badge, IV, IVR).
Hidden entirely when no stocks qualify.

### FR-CV-03 — Sidebar badge
The "⭐ My Stocks" sidebar button shows a green count pill when one or more
conviction stocks are in Buy Zone. Driven by the same poll that powers FR-CV-01.
