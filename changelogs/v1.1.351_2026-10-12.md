## New universe members get five years of bars from the nightly run

**The gap.** `backfill_bars` runs once per generation (2026-09-12 for gen-002). The nightly bars phase asks every member for the last five days only. So a ticker that joins the universe later never got its history. The 2026-10-12 `universe_seed` admitted **VMRK** (Vivmark Residential, the AVB + EQR merger from 08-17) with no bars at all, and from tonight it would have collected 10-06 onward and nothing earlier. The same applied to every future index addition.

**The fix** (`collector/phases/market.ts`). For any active member that holds no `fetch_kind = 'backfill'` bar, phase 2 asks Polygon for **five years instead of five days**. That is the same single call per ticker, so the run costs nothing extra.
- The rows are stored as `backfill` under the backfill capture rule, exactly as the job stores them. QC-204/205/209/210 therefore read the new history the same way.
- The next night the ticker has history and goes back to the five-day tail.
- The phase reports how many members it backfilled (`backfilled`).
- A split inside the new history is handled as any other: R6 re-fetches that ticker's splits when QC-204 fires.

Measured on the 2026-10-12 snapshot: exactly one active member lacks history, VMRK.
