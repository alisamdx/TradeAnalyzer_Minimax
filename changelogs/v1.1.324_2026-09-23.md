# v1.1.324 — 2026-09-23

## QC-501 now follows an accepted QC-204 step

QC-501 is QC-204 seen from the corporate-actions side: the same 23 steps, asking
whether a split or dividend is missing rather than whether the price series is
broken. It followed QC-204 in two of the three ways a QC-204 finding ends:

- a **reuse** ruling closes it directly;
- a **resolved** QC-204 closes it the same way at the next gate run (v1.1.278).

The third was missing. A QC-204 step the operator **accepted** (a genuine move
on the same issuer, e.g. GME's May 2024 squeeze or WAL in the bank crisis) left
QC-501 with nothing to match: it reads only open QC-204 findings and mirrors only
resolved ones. So its twin stayed open and blocking for ever. The only way out
was accepting both findings, with two expiries to keep in step.

Now:

- **Accepted QC-204** → QC-501 closes its matching finding as `false_positive`,
  noting "QC-204 finding accepted until <date>: <note>". The operator manages one
  acceptance.
- **Lapsed acceptance** (`expired_review`) → QC-501 treats the step as open
  again, fires, and the gate reopens its finding as a recurrence. A step cannot
  drop out of QC-501 silently once the 30 days are up.

Test (`packages/core/test/remediation.test.ts`):

- raise the step;
- accept QC-204 until 2026-10-17 → QC-501 resolves with the acceptance named;
- let the acceptance lapse → QC-501 is open again.
